Tier 115 units

_Light defense · Dealer, CC · Weapons: Staff, Rod_ An entry-level Light elemental mage with Range 3. Pike

Tier 16 units

_Medium defense · Dealer · Weapons: Spear_ A self-sustaining AoE starter class: sweeping several tiles ahead and hitting 2 or more enemies triggers Vigilance, raising ATK and restoring HP at the end of each turn. Spearfang Shot knocks enemies into walls or other enemies to stun them for 1 turn, so herding foes toward the edge of the terrain is key.

Rogue

Tier 19 units

_Medium defense · Dealer · Weapons: Dual Daggers_ A beginner melee dealer who leaps to enemies and lands dagger damage with +5~10% Crit Rate. He dives in safely via Stealth (cannot be targeted, +20% Crit Rate, and re-enters Stealth with extra Movement when he defeats an enemy), teleporting to the backline with Shadow Flash and finishing off enemies at 50% HP or below with Combo Slash's follow-up strike. The core of playing him is whether defeats chain into the next Stealth, letting him clear multiple enemies in a single turn.
