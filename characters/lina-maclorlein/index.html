Lina MacLorlein

Nun of the Pandragon Sanctuary

A nun of the Pandragon Sanctuary. Though born in the Dagal Kingdom, she took refuge in the Sanctuary after the village she lived in as a child was raided, and thereafter walked the path of a priest.

Bold and responsible in nature, she has the trust of the saint Lucretia and the other nuns. After Richard's enthronement, Lucretia's order to protect the princesses sends her to the Tomb of the Brave.
