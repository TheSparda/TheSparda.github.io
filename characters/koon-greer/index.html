Koon Greer

Four Imperial Guardians

One of the Four Imperial Guardians. Originally a witch of the Pandragons, she defected to the Empire and was recognized for her prowess, becoming one of the Four Guardians. She can wield a powerful spell called the Triple Elemental Blast, a three-element spell that originates from the Pandragons. She always appears proud and confident, but deep down, she resents her master, Cameo, for abandoning her. (Earlier material spells her name 'Kun Greer' — same character; 'Koon Greer' is the spelling used on her official character card.)

One of the three prized disciples of Cameo Beaufort, head of the Pandragon Royal Magic Corps — alongside fellow students Haya Belle and Michael. Cameo taught each of the three a different elemental specialisation; Koon Greer received ice. She is described as the most gifted of the three, having mastered dual elements.

Her defection from Pandragon stems from a misunderstanding: when Pandragon Castle fell, she believed her master Cameo had deliberately abandoned her and left her behind. In truth, Cameo had sent her on an errand trusting her ability to handle herself, and the evacuation was decided before Koon returned. That misunderstanding drove her to Beradin, who recognised her talent and recruited her to the Four Imperial Guardians. She was once friends with fellow Cameo disciple Haya Belle, who now resents her deeply for the betrayal.

Running Arc

  • Killing Cameo

    At the Second Battle of the Gratias Plains, Koon's magic drives back the Silver Arrow Alliance until Cameo puts her own life on the line to raise a Magic Seal, giving the Alliance its opening to win — Koon kills her former master in the process.
