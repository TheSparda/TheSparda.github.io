Erica Myers

A member of the Cutis Resistance. Has a quiet and gentle personality but joined the Resistance to free Cutis from Geysir. Has a long-standing romantic relationship with Arcia Landino, while also respecting them as the Resistance's commander. While working with the Resistance, she is captured by Imperial forces and imprisoned at Cutis Castle — but is saved by Landam and G.S.
