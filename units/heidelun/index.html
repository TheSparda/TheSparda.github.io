Signature Gear

Guide

Overall: An even stronger but more fragile ultra-burst glass cannon. A more extreme upgrade of Christian: durability got even more frail while firepower got even stronger, and with a single skill click he can gain type advantage against all attributes/troop types, greatly increasing solo-all-kill potential. Depending on class choice he can be run as an extreme counter to buff-stacking parties, or actively apply debuffs for equivalent returns, allowing flexible operation. Achieving Manifestation 1+ lets him actively enter the "Entropy Ghost" state, projecting overwhelming firepower one-sidedly while invincible (even if only for 1 turn), which is extremely powerful. A high-Manifestation, high-awakening exclusive-weapon Heidelloon in the arena can Ghost -> Tactical Maneuver for TP 2 -> Blackhole to slash the whole party's HP, then sweep with follow-up Aftershock, and even if the opponent survives, he is in Entropy Ghost state so nothing can touch him unless it is another Entropy Ghost Heidelloon. Everything (awakening/manifestation, weapon awakening, exclusive armor) is designed so he grows honestly in proportion to money spent; judge the right line for yourself.

Passive: A passive that forces him to be a glass cannon. Heidelloon has enormous attack plus a Christian-era trait that attacks again on enemy kill, so the Entropy Ghost state (which secures survival and crit rate at once) is very strong. He can attack banished/Entropy Ghost enemies, so in PVP it is the only way to kill an enemy Entropy Ghost Heidelloon. Absolutely avoid raising his HP and defense. Manifestation adds attack boost and converts the Banish state into Entropy Ghost, letting him counter Diablo's Phantom Walk, Joan's bind, etc. In high-difficulty dungeons (challenges, late simulation training) where enemy gunners fire banish-bullets, a high-Manifestation Heidelloon becomes stronger when banished, so he counters banish-bullet gunners.

Classes: The Tier-3 options are Root Hacker (buff counter), Chaos Blinger (debuff counter), and L.T.K. Root Hacker is recommended as 1st-priority raise and endgame class: its Paradox Worm (buff-treated debuff, max 10 stacks) applies easily (2 stacks per hit, ~4 stacks in 1 turn with the exclusive weapon's double attack), and its damage-amp scales with buff count so Paradox Worm counts toward it too; its "penetration damage taken" increase also supports penetration-type allies (i.e. Joan). Warning: synergy with Black Prince's Atomic Blast (which wipes buffs and blocks new buff gain) is the worst, since Paradox Worm is a removable buff and cannot be applied to enemies under the Despair debuff; Isabel's exclusive Longinus can also wipe buffs from 5-awakening, and buff-steal units like Silver can steal this debuff, so combine carefully. Chaos Blinger is the debuff counter: actively applies many debuff types, fires Collapse Bullet for area dark damage, and its contagion spreads debuffs, so it excels vs Fierce-Battle Raid / World Boss Raid (debuff-heavy, buff-scarce) and synergizes well with Black Prince's Death Lord (which spreads Loss-of-Will debuffs). L.T.K. (max bullets 4, only class with base movement 4, most mobile) starts stages with the infinite "Perfect Shot" buff (+25% attack/crit at max) and gains 1 TP on kill, but the buff evaporates on taking any damage, so it is weak in raids like Cerberus/Diene where you cannot kill and cannot avoid damage; lowest raise priority since Root Hacker has better damage-amp and a separate class tree.

Core skills & Ultimate:

  • Override Field: TP 1, gain the unremovable 2-turn buff granting type-advantage vs all enemies (+50% damage dealt, -50% damage taken), plus a follow-up action; cooldown 2. Called a "balance-breaking" skill.
  • Terminal Protocol (main damage skill 1): TP 2, 1 bullet, 140% + 20% per enemy buff, ignores cover/escort, banishes if it fails to kill; cooldown 3. Great with Entropy Ghost (can follow up).
  • Astral Blade: TP 3, 3x5 area, 90% electric damage + Shock + 1 random debuff; cooldown 3. Does not consume bullets (so no Magic-Bullet/Collapse-Bullet amp), random debuffs, but wide range helps his poor mobility; good class-passive synergy for Chaos Blinger (applies 2 debuffs).
  • Null Spread (main damage skill 2): TP 2, empties all bullets and reloads Magic Bullets to max, then fires down a straight line at 30% per shot (each shot's real multiplier becomes 55% with Magic Bullet, so ~155% start), repeating until bullets run out or enemy dies; cooldown 3. With L.T.K. + exclusive weapon (6-bullet magazine, full awakening) the peak reaches 430%; but only ~220% on other classes with a stand-in weapon, sometimes weaker than Terminal Protocol. Cannot fire Collapse Bullets via Null Spread.
  • Tactical Maneuver: TP 1, move to farthest empty cell within 4 front cells, steal TP from passed enemies (if 0 stolen, refunded and cooldown -1), plus follow-up action; cooldown 3. Useful for his slow (movement 3) main classes; with TP steal, a 1-turn instant Blackhole is possible if 3 enemies are lined up.
  • Unreal Blackhole (Ultimate): dark area attack targeting within 4 cells; on use lose 50% of remaining HP and gain +15% attack until next turn start, then deal 100%~120% damage to enemies within 3 rings around the target and pull them to center (position control). Wide range, all passives apply, first-strike boost. Risky (halves HP, blocks passive-trigger condition), so normally use carefully; but firing while in Entropy Ghost is near no-risk since invincible, and after obtaining the manifestation skill he can fire it freely; full-manifestation drops the passive condition to 50% so HP loss barely matters. Grows in power/convenience the more you pay.
  • Manifestation skill Entropy Ghost: no TP, pays 10% HP to grant self Entropy Ghost; triggers Sequence Break for a re-action. First enhance adds debuff removal, second enhance adds 1 TP charge; cooldown 4. Effectively pays HP for 1-turn invincible + crit rate, lasting until end of the next turn's action (up to 2 Ghost attacks) plus a re-action. In PVP (as of mid-2025) the only counter to a Manifestation-1+ Heidelloon is another Manifestation-1+ Heidelloon, so this skill is considered essential for active PVP players.

Exclusive weapon & recommended gear: Weapon is Ex Nihilo; extra stats attack + crit, max bullets +1~2.

  • Kill Order: applied to a random hit enemy after attacking; received crit damage +10~20%; if that enemy takes lethal damage, the dealer gains 1 TP and Kill Order is removed; lasts 2 turns. 1 target, becomes 2 from 5-awakening.
  • Aftershock (Heidelloon-exclusive): on action end, fire at the lowest-HP enemy in range for 50~80% damage; triggers even without bullets; if it kills, Aftershock triggers once more (no cap on repeats, theoretically can wipe all enemies in 1 turn). Essentially mandatory; awakening's crit-multiplier rise contributes greatly to firepower. Note: Aftershock is not an "extra attack," so whetstones are meaningless.
  • Armor: Ignore survival entirely, go full-burst; unlike Christian there is no crit benefit and most is attack-multiplier, so pile everything into attack. Best Legendary armor is the Assassin 4-set; One-Man-Army 4-set + physical penetration can counter PVP crit-damage-reduction tank meta. Heroic: Chivalrous 4-set, or Chivalrous 2 + Slayer 2 for attack 20%. The self-themed "System Heretic" set (2-set: physical pen 15% + crit 15%; 4-set: attack +30% on enemy attack, plus once-per-turn 2-turn shield of 50% max HP on dealing lethal damage) is a broken paid-item set worth considering if wealthy.
  • Accessories: attack-focused, so the standard Ancient Hero's Ring is recommended (though it slightly raises survival, making passive-trigger a bit harder). Arrowhead valid if crit + Terminal Protocol focus, but note it does not apply to Null Spread. Heroic: Wound-Worsening Charm for attack, or Golden Dice for crit. Fang usable for attack but physical pen applies only to combat attacks, so unsuitable if Astral Blade/Null Spread are main; whetstones meaningless since Aftershock is not an extra attack.

KR Creator Guide

Character review of Heidelun by 하막군의게임튜브 · 2026-08-04

The second Outer One, released for the 1.5-year anniversary, and the reviewer thinks Heidelun came out stronger than Black Prince. His assessment is unusually stark: at the time of filming his guild had found essentially no answer to him. If Heidelun moves first, walks in and opens fire, the team dies — the only counters he can name are a Mingming Sanctuary or playing around revives. (19:04)

Entropy Ghost is the whole survival plan

Above 75% HP, a single lethal hit leaves him at 1 HP in Entropy Ghost instead of killing him. Banishment does the same. Ghost behaves like Banish except he can still move and attack, and because Ghost is itself an Action Control effect it automatically grants Sequence Break — so he gets an extra action out of it. (1:00)

That gives you the one reliable way to kill him: multi-hit skills. Because the trigger needs a single lethal blow from above 75% HP, anything that chips him down in several instances never qualifies, and he dies normally. Suppressing Fire and similar multi-hit attacks are what he recommends. (2:34)

The Outer One skill Entropy Ghost runs on its own cooldown, separate from the passive trigger, so using one doesn't lock out the other. (3:05)

Class: Chaos Blinger or L.T.K to attack, Root Hacker to grind

Root Hacker loads Magic Bullets and applies Paradox Worm on hit: -5% ATK and M-ATK per stack and +10% penetration damage taken per stack, up to 10 — so up to +100%. Shooting applies two stacks at a time because the shot and the round both land. (3:36)

The important quirk, and he demonstrates it on camera: Paradox Worm counts as a buff, despite showing a debuff icon. Black Prince's Atomic Blast+1 more → wipes it, and because Atomic Blast also blocks buff acquisition, shooting afterwards doesn't restack it. (4:38)

Chaos Blinger loads Collapse rounds instead — 25% fixed damage in a ring around each affected target, so several tagged enemies detonate together. It raises damage taken by 15% per debuff on the target until his own turn ends, which the reviewer confirms means it boosts his weapon's Aftershock chain but nothing your other units do. Attacking with an area skill also copies debuffs outward across the field. (5:36)

He expects most attacking players to run Chaos Blinger or L.T.K+1 more →. (7:10)

Gear

The System Heretic set, or Black Crow without it. One Against a Thousand is also fine if you want the Physical Penetration. Accessory: his exclusive if you have it, otherwise Black Banner is plenty. (13:11)

The weapon is mandatory

Aftershock is the reason. After attacking he fires again; if that kill lands, he fires again; and again, until nothing dies. He initially read the text as one extra shot and confirms in play that it is unbounded. It scales from 50% of ATK at base to 80% at full awakening, and he says guild-battle Heidelun simply kills whole teams with it.

★3 raises his ammo count to 2, ★5 applies Kill Order to two targets rather than one. Take at least one copy of the weapon — this is not optional. (13:52)

Runes

He runs a full Time set. Without it, Chaos and Ultimate mixed is fine — most players just run Time. (15:25)

Manifestation: 5 is the number

2 adds ATK. 4 adds ATK and, notably, resets the two longest cooldowns when he is hit by a Skill Control or cooldown effect — he calls this the direct answer to Este Dode decks. 6 pushes ATK to +30% and drops Entropy Ghost's cooldown to 2, i.e. a revive every other turn.

But 5 is the breakpoint that changes how he plays: Entropy Ghost starts returning 1 TP. Override Field then Entropy Ghost puts him back to 3 TP, which funds an Astral Blade in the same turn. (15:56)

Artifact: the exclusive, and it isn't close

Overload Loader+4 more →, Initial Armor-Piercing Magazine+2 more → and Wind Step+10 more → are all filler in his view — Overload Loader is what most non-full-manifestation players will use.

Sunset-Light Pendant is the real one: +30% Max HP on a character with a very low health pool, Physical Penetration and a cover state while shielded, and a Safety Barrier worth his Max HP at turn start below 50%. That barrier is what pays for the ultimate's self-damage, and it also refills him the turn after Entropy Ghost drops him to 1 HP. It cannot be removed. (17:29)
