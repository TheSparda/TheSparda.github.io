Signature Gear

Guide

The following is subjective NamuWiki community opinion (KR server).

Overall: Called a near-typeless heal-tank. As the first Dark-attribute legendary healer (prior legendary healers were only blue/red/light), he is typeless except against Light and can revive allies, so usefulness is self-evident. Blood Priest gives HP-based Spell Power and Spell Power-based defense, so raising specs makes HP and defense skyrocket; the ultimate grants a 1-turn invincible state, and TP payback keeps TP overflowing so ults come up very often. In sum: a semi-tank with typelessness + huge HP + huge Spell Power + huge defense + an undying skill. In PVP, said to be very hard to defeat unless you prepare counters like a def-ignore dealer or a buff-stealer (e.g. Silver). Low investment difficulty since finishing just up to Blood Priest gives all core healer skills. "Strength befitting the leader of the 13 Dark Gods." (Aside: "what happens when Prios comes out?")

Performance: Base class Acolyte; brings new classes from Tier 1. Tier 2 has unique class Blood Cultist; Tier 3 is all unique (Blood Priest / Abyss Priest / Omen Sage), a feat matched only by Sharkbari. Ultimate is Dark Blood Force Field. Unique passive Primordial Dark Blood grants a non-dispellable revive buff to an ally lacking a death-prevention effect and gives self -10% dmg taken; greatly boosts squishy dealers' stability. Recipient is random, so to target a specific ally, every other member must hold a death-prevention effect; more revive-holders exist after 1 year of service. Deimos himself has no revive but the passive adds lifesteal to basic attacks, so survival is fine if he does not die. The periodic extra action requires not attacking / not applying a debuff (i.e., basically play him as a pure healer); since counting starts turn 1, making the first action of turn 1 something other than attack/debuff guarantees the extra action, so 2 actions turn 1 is usually possible.

Classes:

  • Blood Priest: learns Life Tap; flat +30% max HP, HP-based Spell Power, and blood-magic passive. "End-game class for a healer"; raising HP raises Spell Power which raises defense, making him absurdly tanky. This tree also grants 2 key exclusive skills, so it is the top priority raise. Note: basic attack Life Drain is blood-magic but counts as a basic attack, so it does NOT trigger the passive.
  • Abyss Priest: learns Mental Collapse; on dark-skill use gain Revelation of the Abyss; ally skill removes 2 debuffs, enemy skill removes 2 buffs. All Deimos skills are dark magic so it is easy to use, but it is the only Tier-3 class with no survival on the passive.
  • Omen Sage: learns exclusive Curse of Incapacitation; unconditional up to +20% Spell Power and -20% dmg taken; debuffing removes 2 target buffs and applies Curse of Pain; curse skills cost -1 TP and grant extra move. Debuff-focused; only downside is Deimos's sole curse is Curse of Incapacitation, and it is a solo tree so raise priority is low.

Exclusive skills & Ultimate:

  • Transfusion: TP 1, heal ally 80% Spell Power + Transfusion buff, Acolyte, blood magic, cooldown 1. Low hurdle; superior to plain heals since the buff heals combat attrition.
  • Boiling Blood: TP 1, 2-turn Boiling Blood buff + Recklessness debuff (def -20%), Blood Cultist, blood magic, cooldown 1. High-risk high-return buff; caution: Recklessness counts as a debuff, so using it as the first skill loses the passive extra action.
  • Life Tap: TP 2, AoE heal 80% Spell Power + Life Tap buff, Blood Priest, blood magic, cooldown 3. Massive healing; only flaw is the 3-turn cooldown.
  • Mental Collapse: TP 3, 70% dark dmg to target + 50% to adjacent, removes Ward, random-moves and applies action disable, dmg taken +10%, 1 turn, Abyss Priest, cooldown 3. Locks up to 5 enemies for 1 turn; the only exclusive costing 3 TP with no blood-magic payback, but Deimos overflows TP. Strong in 5v5 PVP; can also target objects to hit enemies behind fences.
  • Curse of Incapacitation: TP 2, AoE, removes Ward and applies Curse of Incapacitation, Omen Sage, Weaken, cooldown 3. Excellent low-TP AoE debuff; pairs superbly with Dominator-class Sharkbari. Note: not blood-magic.
  • ultimate: move within 3 tiles, AoE 100%~130% dmg, 20% Fear; if no Dark Blood buff, gain HP cannot drop below 1 for 1 turn. Lets Deimos be used as a 1-turn sandbag thrown into enemy lines; compared to Veradin's Hexblade, strips the extras but adds Fear and teleport. Since he is a healer, survival after Immortality ends is also good. TP overflows, so it comes up very often.

Recommended Weapon & Gear: Exclusive weapon Chaos Blood, legendary rod; extra stats Spell Power and Max HP. After a skill: ally gets def/res +10%15% buff, enemy gets def/res -10%15% debuff; for Deimos, ally buff adds crit rate +510% and enemy debuff adds crit rate -510%; from 5th awakening, Deimos-only +1 skill range. Armor: Cardinal 4-set for HP + TP based on Blood Priest's HP-scaling Spell Power, OR (since skills are low-TP so 4-set is iffy) Cardinal 2 + Chancellor 2 to add flat Spell Power; a Devotion 4-set also works. Accessories: healer staples legendary and hero are solid.

Awakening/Manifestation progression: Two parallel tracks per stage: Inner Cube (left) and Awakening/right. - Stage 1: Ultimate Enhance 1 / Stat Enhance 1 - Stage 2: Unique Passive Enhance 1 / Stat Enhance 2 - Stage 3: Ultimate Enhance 2 / Gain Artifact - Stage 4: Unique Passive Enhance 2 / Artifact Enhance 1 - Stage 5: Ultimate Enhance 3 / Artifact Enhance 2 - Stage 6: Unique Passive Enhance 3 / Artifact Enhance 3 Note: exact per-stage numeric deltas for ult/passive are not itemized in this section. The passive scales as follows across awakening tiers (from the passive listing): extra-action cooldown 4turn (base) to 3turn (Max Awakening) to 2turn; Dark Blood revive HP 25% to 50% to 100% (+2 TP on revive); Blood Communion dmg taken -10% to -15%; Dark Infusion Spell Power +10%/+20%/+40%, Radiance Resistance +20%/+25%/+30%; Max Manifestation also gives +20% max HP. Ultimate scales 100%/120%/150% dmg, Fear chance 20%/30%/50%, and Max Manifestation adds +2 TP after cast.

KR Creator Guide

Character review of Deimos of Chaos by 하막군의게임튜브 · 2026-08-04

The reviewer opens by saying he didn't want to pull for a scruffy bearded man, and then immediately concedes the point: Deimos is, in his words, an absurdly broken character. His closing advice is to pull him even though Heidelun is next — because Deimos makes an entire team better just by existing, and he will still be good later. (23:17)

Dark Blood is a free revive for someone else

At game start he hands Dark Blood to a random ally that has no death-prevention effect of its own — a revive, given away. If every ally already has one, he keeps it himself. In exchange he takes Damage Empathy, which reduces his own damage taken. (1:01)

At full manifestation Dark Blood also raises the recipient's Max HP by 20%. Stack that with Life Tap's 20% and a Shakvari buff and the reviewer describes an Isabel Ripine sitting on 15-16,000 health that also revives. His own reaction: "just thinking about it is horrible." (21:15)

His passive also grants an extra attack or skill use every few turns, but only if he did not attack and did not apply a debuff that turn. Buffing allies is fine; attacking is not. (2:34)

Class: Blood Priest, and it isn't close

Blood Priest gives +30% Max HP, M-ATK scaling from Max HP, and — on any blood-magic skill — Blood Petrification (DEF worth 50% of M-ATK) plus Focus.

Two things make this dominant. Almost every skill Deimos owns is blood magic, so it is effectively always on; the reviewer shows 2,500 DEF at 6 copies alone. And Focus refunds TP, which on his many 1 TP skills means casting them for free. He describes TP as simply not being a constraint on this character. (3:05)

Abyss Priest adds M-ATK and magic penetration on any dark skill — again, everything — and strips buffs. Worth knowing that the strip resolves after the damage, so a shield still absorbs the hit before it comes off. (6:44)

Omen Sage+1 more → cuts curse-skill TP cost and applies Curse of Agony, which the reviewer tested: it deals 50% damage the moment it lands, before any of the on-buff or end-of-turn triggers. But Deimos has exactly one curse skill, so the class has little to work with. (7:47)

Life Tap is the reason he's broken

Life Tap heals allies within 2 tiles for 80% of M-ATK and applies Life Tap for two turns: +20% Max HP, and 60% of the caster's M-ATK restored after every combat.

The critical detail he demonstrates: "after combat" includes combat where the enemy attacked. So every single hit an ally takes heals them for 60% of Deimos's M-ATK — around 2,000 at his own modest rank — with no limit on how many times per turn. He shows it firing over and over on camera. (11:53)

The rest of the kit

Transfusion is a straightforward 80% heal with a 40% heal-over-time. (9:19)

Boiling Blood gives an ally +20% ATK and M-ATK, growing by 20% each turn to 60%, plus a TP refund at end of turn. Two subtleties: it counts as applying a debuff, so it blocks the passive's extra action — but at 1 TP with a 1-turn cooldown you can cast it repeatedly, and recasting it while it's still running extends the duration without resetting the stacks. He rates it a raid tool. (9:51)

Mental Collapse does 70% plus 50% fixed to neighbours, removes the Immunity effect (Esmeralda's), then scatters the targets randomly and applies Fear. Land it on a cross of clustered enemies and the whole group is displaced and unable to act. (14:08)

Curse of Incapacitation is a smaller Crackdown: +30% damage taken and -30% ATK/M-ATK over a 2-tile area, also clearing Immunity. (15:00)

The ultimate moves him in, hits everything within 3 tiles for 120-150%, absorbs all their buffs, and grants Immortal if he isn't carrying Dark Blood — he cannot drop below 1 HP. So he dives into the enemy formation and soaks whatever they have. (16:02)

Gear

Two pieces of the Cardinal set with two of Chancellor of Darkness — health is what matters, and the Chancellor cast bonus does little on him.

Accessory: Stopwatch, because TP is never the constraint and more cooldown turnover is. Cursed Eye is a reasonable alternative for survivability. (18:40)

The weapon is skippable

Unless you take it to ★5 for the Range +1, it only gives modest DEF/RES and Crit adjustments. Diablo's or Morgaina Schyren's weapon does more for him. (19:12)

Runes

Not health, and not defence — he already has enormous amounts of both. Go Crit-damage-taken reduction and let him grind. (17:38)

Manifestation

3 and 5 add TP recovery to the ultimate. Combined with Blood Priest's Focus and the artifact below, casting the ultimate refunds most of its cost — his estimate is around 4 TP back, though he flags that he hasn't been able to test whether two Focus effects stack. Full manifestation drops the passive's cooldown to 2 turns and adds the Max HP rider to Dark Blood. (20:14)

Artifacts

3. Elemental Mastery: Dark is the one he would take: every dark skill grants Focus, and every skill he has is dark, so it's a TP refund every cast.

The two Forbidden Symbol fragments shield the strongest ally and the dying ally respectively. Reasonable, but with TP already overflowing he thinks the Focus artifact is still better. (21:45)
