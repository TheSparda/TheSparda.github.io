Unique Passive

Two Faces of the Moon Passive

First Acquisition: At the start of the game, gain one of Will of the Moon 1 or Dark Side of the Moon 1. When you take lethal damage, revive with 35% health and remove all debuffs. This effect triggers 1 time per game. On revival, if the state before dying was Will of the Moon 1, instead gain Dark Side of the Moon 1; if it was Dark Side of the Moon 1, gain Will of the Moon 1.

  • Will of the Moon 1 Cannot be removed: At the end of the character's turn, grant 1 random effect to 1 ally within 3 tiles nearby with 60% chance. Cannot be removed.
  • Dark Side of the Moon 1 Cannot be removed: At the end of the character's turn, grant 1 random debuff to 1 enemy within 2 tiles nearby with 60% chance. Cannot be removed.

Max Awakening: At the start of the game, gain one of Will of the Moon 4 or Dark Side of the Moon 4. When you take lethal damage, revive with 35% health and remove all debuffs. This effect triggers 1 time per game. On revival, if the state before dying was Will of the Moon 4, instead gain Dark Side of the Moon 4; if it was Dark Side of the Moon 4, gain Will of the Moon 4.

  • Will of the Moon 4 Cannot be removed: Healing effect +30%. At the end of the character's turn, grant 1 random effect to 1 ally within 3 tiles nearby. Cannot be removed.
  • Dark Side of the Moon 4 Cannot be removed: At the end of the character's turn, grant 1 random debuff to 1 enemy within 2 tiles nearby. Cannot be removed.

Max Manifestation: Spell power and health increase by 30%. At the start of the game, gain one of Will of the Moon 7 or Dark Side of the Moon 7. Once per game, when you take lethal damage, revive with 100% health and remove all debuffs. On revival, recover all TP and immediately, if the prior state was Will of the Moon 7, instead gain Dark Side of the Moon 7; if it was Dark Side of the Moon 7, gain Will of the Moon 7.

  • Will of the Moon 7 Cannot be removed: Healing effect +60%. Damage taken -30%. At the end of the character's turn, grant 1 random effect to 1 ally within 3 tiles nearby.
  • Dark Side of the Moon 7 Cannot be removed: Crit chance / magic penetration +30%. At the end of the character's turn, grant 1 random debuff to 1 enemy within 2 tiles nearby.

Guide

Note: The following is NamuWiki community assessment (KR server), subjective community opinion. No explicit S/A/B tier rating is present in the text.

Performance:

  • Among Legendary characters, she is one of only three owners of the "11-character tree", along with Gishne and Irene. Uniquely, like Irene, she doubles as both healer and mage, so her Tier 3 classes are two: High Priest and Dark Wizard. Switching to the mage-side class also changes the class icon.
  • Whether Will or Reverse is applied is up to chance. Up through 4th awakening even the effect is very much a chance game, and it is not even a strong effect, making it a bizarre passive. At 4th awakening or above, a guaranteed healing effect attaches to Moon's Will, so it seems weighted more toward the healer side, but if you want to heal and only the Reverse side keeps getting applied, you are stuck with a bad outcome. That she has a revive skill is rated highly, at least.
  • She has no ultimate skill.

Evaluation:

  • A character where, aside from the surprise of a healer also using attack magic, it is unclear what they were trying to do. There are no attack-related buffs, and the passive is better suited to being a healer, so you end up using her as a healer, but the passive's randomness is too strong, making her hard to utilize. On top of that, she oddly learns Triple Elemental Blast, the signature move of the Pandragon mage corps, a truly confusing character.
  • Still, since she learns Heal and Heal Wind, she can be used as a healer if you want to. In World Boss raids, if a red/light attribute ban is in effect and there is no Deimos, there is room to field her as a Legendary-grade healer. She can also learn the Mage's basic 3 skills plus Electric Wave and Triple Elemental Blast, and she holds an ultra-rare revive skill, so either way she is usable if you want. Slap on a healer icon and use Triple Elemental Blast to surprise your opponent in the arena. The moment you decide to use this character, you have decided not to care about efficiency, so there is no need to weigh her power.
  • Savor your opponent's praise for your persistence.
  • She received an Inner Break, but she should still be praised.

Awakening/Manifestation progression: - Stage 1: Stat enhancement 1 - Stage 2: Unique Passive enhancement 1, Stat enhancement 2 - Stage 3: Artifact acquisition - Stage 4: Unique Passive enhancement 2, Artifact enhancement 1 - Stage 5: Artifact enhancement 2 - Stage 6: Unique Passive enhancement 3, Artifact enhancement 3
