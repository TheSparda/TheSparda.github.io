Guide (community assessment)

Note: the following is NamuWiki community assessment (KR server), subjective community opinion. No explicit S/A/B tier rating is stated in the text.

Overall evaluation: A melee burst-DPS armed with a revive and a heal on his base passive, plus AoE skills for varied situations and a debuff-reflect. Individually each piece feels slightly "off," but high awakening/high spec performance and exclusive gear fill the gaps and are designed to synergize, so his ceiling is considered very high. In PvP he permanently carries debuff-reflect and kill-triggered extra actions; the "Sprint Cyrano" build can recreate the infamous "first-turn Hyderun" as a melee dealer, and at max awakening he can even revive on turn 1 and recover TP 2, turning around to eat an Asura-Pacheonmu himself, so he is rated the number 1 threat to watch when on the enemy side. With a 3-star exclusive weapon, on turn 2 (when recovering Ectoplasm) he can recover up to TP 2 via Soul Drain to Ectoplasm-recovery to Soul Drain; with 4th-awakening passive enhancement in a raid, sample loops:

Turn

LvEffect
1Ectoplasm to Soul Drain (TP5) to Asura-Pacheonmu (TP0).
2(TP1 from turn passing) to Chaos Veil (TP0) to Soul Drain (TP2) to Gong-a-yeonseom (TP0) to Ectoplasm-recovery re-action to Soul Drain (TP1).

The problem: the ceiling is high, but reaching it needs heavy spending, requiring high-awakening weapon, high-awakening exclusive armor, and high-awakening exclusive accessory to assemble the parts (jokes compared it to Exodia). Also, whereas earlier Outer Ones boast absurd performance, Cyrano's survivability lags and he has no way to overcome attribute matchups, so both survival and expected firepower fall short, giving him assorted critical weaknesses. Even in PvP, though he has debuff-reflect and kill-triggered extra actions, those very traits make him the top-priority target.

Much of this bad rap owes to the current PvP environment. Cyrano is blue attribute: strong vs red, weak vs green, no matchup vs light/dark. As of early 2026, major PvP characters include GGS and Isabel (green) and the Black Prince, Beramode, Cheonma Cheon Yeoun (dark), so a high share of awkward matchups. The key character Hyderun is red, but this matters little because: he is a ranged dealer so Cyrano must punch through the front line to reach him; Override Field ignores attribute so Cyrano is more often at a matchup disadvantage; Entropy Ghost gives an invincible state so normal attacks do not land; a burst dealer like Cyrano would one-shot Hyderun, but Hyderun then enters Entropy Ghost instead of dying, effectively handing over an invincible state; and a defense-drained Hyderun is a "water body" easy to kill anyway, so Cyrano's matchup edge is hard to feel. Cost-efficiency pick: a 1-copy exclusive weapon at 3-star (securing Ectoplasm and Soul Drain); securing a 2nd copy adds the large-monster-attack extra-action passive, making him a useful main dealer in raids.

Performance highlights: Base class Fighter. Tier 2 has the exclusive class Dark Blade; Tier 3 is Grand Master / Sword Master / Abyss Blade, all exclusive classes. Ultimate is Asura-Pacheonmu. Unique passive "Knight of the Grey Cloak": unconditional ATK +20%30%; ATK up by 40% of lost-HP ratio; crit rate up by 10% of lost-HP ratio; extra action on killing a player-controlled enemy or summon (cooldown 32); at 2nd awakening passive-enhance, "when attacking a large monster" is added to the extra-action condition, cooldown 1; at 4th awakening, that condition also recovers TP 1. Unremovable buff "Critical Regen" (added by patch): once per turn after combat, if HP under 50%, recover HP by 30% of ATK but not above 50% of max HP; on the 1 lethal hit per game, revive at 60%~80% HP; at 5-star recover TP 1 on revive; at 6-star gain "Will of Devotion": cooldown-control immunity, unremovable, and instantly removes mind-control from Este Dode. The community calls this a very aggressive passive; the patched base heal + one-time revive fit his in-story arc of being cast to the bottom then reviving. 2nd-awakening large-monster extra-action makes him a strong raid burst dealer; 4th awakening adds TP 1 on that condition, so he gains TP each boss hit / each PvP kill. With a 3-star exclusive weapon's Soul Drain that becomes TP 1 steal + TP 1 on kill; at 5th awakening, using a fully-upgraded Ectoplasm from TP 0 yields Ectoplasm TP 2 + TP 1 steal + TP 1 on kill, snapping back to TP 4, letting him run wild in PvP. The 6-star Will of Devotion's cooldown-control immunity may have niche PvP use; the Este Dode mind-control bounce is more of an original-work nod than practical (Este's only mind-control skill is her ult Hedonic Grab). Base heal and revive give enough survivability, but unlike the lifesteal-Asura Black Prince (dark, near attribute-agnostic) or matchup-ignoring, fully-invincible-awakening Hyderun, Cyrano is blue so green attribute exploits his weakness, and his awakening skill Ectoplasm costs 50% HP, so play with survivability in mind.

Classes:

  • Grand Master: exclusive class that learns Vacuum Asura Blade; unconditional ATK/DEF/RES/crit rate +2030%, damage taken -1520%, additional -5% damage taken per enemy within 3 tiles (max -15~20%), caps enemy damage-reduction at 50% on attack/counter, has first-strike. Good balance leaning toward crit, but defense drops as nearby enemy count falls; recommended for early leveling since it teaches the main skill Vacuum Asura Blade, but not favored at the top end because the damage-reduction cap is computed per-source (not on the final result), so it only works against the Black Prince.
  • Sword Master: learns Gong-a-yeonseom; on combat attack ATK up to +50%, crit +15%, damage taken -50%, and on crit in combat recover up to 40% of ATK as HP (cooldown 1). Everything is centered on combat attacks, but among his exclusive skills only Gong-a-yeonseom has the combat-attack property, so an exclusive-skill-focused build can't leverage the passive; damage reduction is combat-attack only, so AoE hits land in full, a minus for survival-reliant Cyrano.
  • Abyss Blade: exclusive class that learns Gale Demon-Shadow Slash, following the Dark Blade to Abyss Blade tree. Unconditional ATK/RES up to +50%, damage taken up to -20%, and before attacking applies "Abyss Erosion" to enemies within 2 tiles and gains the "Aura of Control" reducing damage from outside-aura attacks by 20%; this class effect cannot be sealed (e.g. by Arc Knight's Suppression). Abyssal Erosion: ATK/spell -40%, self-heal received -50%, dark resist -30%, unremovable, permanent, heal-block. Highest unconditional flat-ATK gain, base damage reduction, survivability boost, and uniquely ignores class-passive sealing, so it is most recommended as the finished endgame class.

Key skills:

  • Chaos Veil: TP 1, cooldown 3. Gain the Chaos Veil effect (max 3 stacks, 2 turns; damage taken / crit damage taken -10%; hides all other buffs/debuffs; stacks up when hit by a player-controlled enemy and clears at max stack; on clear, gain "Chaos Guard" shield ATK 50% shield, 1 turn and copy all your debuffs to enemies within 3 tiles then remove all your own debuffs; if no debuffs, apply a random Chaos debuff). Chaos debuffs: Chaos Weakening ATK/spell -25%; Chaos Rift DEF/RES -25%; Chaos Destruction damage taken +10%; Balance Collapse crit rate taken +10% and crit damage taken +10%. The hide is a system-level effect that also ignores buff/debuff-count-based effects and buff-removal effects, praised highly. Near a debuff-immunity + reflect in PvP. On clear it dumps your debuffs onto surrounding enemies while you come out clean, like a near-rainbow-reflect; forced to trigger even through stun (a stunned Cyrano makes it an AoE stun). Clear conditions: 2 turns elapsed, or being hit 3 times by player-controlled enemies (PvP only); PvE relies on the 2-turn timer.
  • Vacuum Asura Blade: TP 2, cooldown 2. Directional sword-energy AoE; choose Horizontal Cut or Vertical Cut. Horizontal: 3x4 range, 120% ATK, -10% per target over 1 (max -30%), additional -10% per 2-tile distance, banish-state-attack. Vertical: 1x4 range, 160% ATK, -20% per target over 1 (max -60%), additional -20% per 2-tile distance, banish-state-attack. Effectively a single-target skill that can also hit AoE; max range 4 reaches ranged foes. Innate banish-state-attack synergizes with banish-inducing attacks; cannot hit Este Dode's Phase Leap (not a banish property).
  • Gong-a-yeonseom: TP 2, cooldown 2. Gain Fighting Spirit; ATK/spell +20%, 2 turns and deal 160% ATK to 1 target. Simple, strong single-target with a self-buff; take it to raids.
  • Gale Demon-Shadow Slash: TP 2, cooldown 3. 3x4 range, 90% ATK with movement; removes TP 1 from hit enemies. Provides mobility Cyrano otherwise lacks, but lower priority since Vacuum Asura Blade covers AoE and Ectoplasm doubles as movement; niche in a Sprint-Cyrano PvP build to start all enemies at TP -2 while charging in alone.
  • Sprint: TP 1, cooldown 2. Gain extra action immediately and crit rate +15% this turn. Key because awakened Cyrano can move 11 tiles on turn 1 (move 3 to Ectoplasm teleport 2 to move 3 to Sprint to move 3), reaching the enemy at start and opening with Vacuum Asura Blade or Gale Slash; a kill grants immediate re-action to chain into Soul Drain, letting him wipe light-armor characters and neutralize a healer early.

Ultimate: Asura-Pacheonmu: Hits enemies within 3 rings for 150%~165% ATK and gains Momentum of Ruin: physical lifesteal 10%, after attack/counter deal 20% ATK defense-ignoring dark damage, 2 turns, unremovable. Even fully upgraded at 5th awakening the ratio is a relatively low 180%, but the post-use lifesteal + added dark damage buff is the point; awakening raises lifesteal to 30%, added dark ratio to 40%, and adds movement, so high-awakening Cyrano uses it far more often with stronger Momentum of Ruin.

Ectoplasm: Teleport up to 2 tiles and leave an Ectoplasm summon where you were; gain re-action, split HP in half with it, it shares 35% of damage taken and is removed if the caster dies, same stats, cannot act, revive-immune, mind-control-immune, unremovable. Caster can use Thought Absorb / Thought Return before the next turn ends (each grants re-action); auto-recovered at turn end for an extra turn. Thought-Body Recovery removes the summon, absorbs its HP and buffs, clears all caster debuffs, removes banish state. Ignores cooldown control and works while silenced. Community view: a utility/teleport/tanking tool that also enables guaranteed re-action, debuff clear, and heal, but the tanking boost comes only after halving your HP, which helps little in the current inflated PvP; the summon itself doesn't deal damage and can even get in the way, so it is a poorly-rated awakening skill. Nuance worth studying: Thought Absorption / Thought Return grant "re-action," but turn-end auto-recovery grants an "extra turn," which ignores re-action-blocked status and shortens skill cooldown by 1 turn.

Exclusive weapon and recommended gear: Weapon: Old Excalibur, reddened by ultimate grima when master Iyolin took the Black Prince's life; added stats ATK +20% and physical penetration +20%. Base: pre-combat ATK up by 100% of AoE-damage increase, gain Blood Craving; turn-start take 5% remaining-HP physical fixed damage, unremovable, on skill use gain Dark Advent; phys pen/AoE damage +5%, 2 turns, max 2 stacks, unremovable, AoE damage taken -10%. Cyrano-exclusive: same pre-combat ATK; Soul Craving; turn-start take 6% remaining-HP physical fixed damage and Ruin Advent; phys pen/AoE damage +10%, 2 turns, max 2 stacks; 3-star: summon-attack target damage taken +30%; 4-star: radiance resist +10%; 3-star Ruin Advent adds Soul Drain; Soul Drain: 130% dark physical damage, applies Weakening; at level 2, ATK/spell -15%, crit rate taken +15%, crit damage taken +15%, TP 1 steal, range 1-3, ranged/blood-magic/TP-remove/combat properties; 5-star: at max Ruin Advent, apply Ruin's Call; damage taken +10%, healing received -50%, 2 turns, heal-block. Community calls the weapon so strong it is treated as his real body; Cyrano's damage heavily depends on it, so 3-star (for Soul Drain, the 1-3 range basic attack that also gets both basic-attack and skill buffs) is recommended, ideally toward 5-star. The downside is the Asura-like fixed-HP-damage penalty with no self-heal (eased once the patch made revive/heal his base passive). Soul Drain is Cyrano-only; other characters cannot use it. Armor: hard to ignore attribute matchup, so "kill before you die" and stack ATK; with Abyss Blade (ATK-only) as the finished class, ATK+phys pen is preferred over crit; the paid Successor exclusive armor gives ATK, phys pen, and ATK-based shield, ideal if obtained (only downside is being paid). Accessories: ATK accessories preferred; crit damage taken -10%, crit-hit HP recovery is the standard, the new Seopung Guardian's Amulet also usable, Marksman's Magic Bullet / Wolf Pine for offense; the specialized phys pen, max HP, shield-received increase, and shield-gain ATK/DEF/RES is ideal but only from a gacha event (its self-shield-on-crit needs 5-star), which added to user anger over its heavy paywall.

KR Creator Guide

Character review of Cyrano Bernstein by 하막군의게임튜브 · 2026-08-04

The third Outer One, and the one the reviewer is most conflicted about. Cyrano Bernstein hits enormously hard and is genuinely difficult to fight — but his power is spread across four separate purchases, and if you don't have all of them he plays like half a character. He states the floor explicitly: 4 manifestations, weapon at ★3 or better, and the Inheritor armour set. Below that line he is weak, and the set matters most. (28:24)

He also notes the developers responded to complaints about low-investment Cyrano by pushing several full-manifestation effects down to the lower tiers, so the early ranks are considerably better than at launch. (1:03)

The passive and what changed

ATK +30%, plus another 40% of the percentage of HP lost — and since Ectoplasm cuts his health in half by design, that bonus is close to permanent. Crit Rate also scales with HP lost.

Killing an enemy player or summon grants an extra action. Note the wording: only players and summons count, so farming NPCs in PvE does not trigger it. (1:33)

Critical Regeneration heals him for 30% of ATK below 50% HP, and he now revives once per match with TP restored, both available from a single copy. Devoted Will blocks mind control — specifically Este Dode's Hedonic Grab. (2:34)

Class: Abyss Blade, essentially always

Abyss Blade gives +50% ATK and RES, -20% damage taken, immunity to class passive lockout — so Black Prince's Morale Loss and Arch Knight-style suppression don't shut him off — and a Control Aura before attacking.

The aura is the payload: Abyssal Erosion on enemies within 2 tiles, cutting ATK and M-ATK by 40%, reducing self-healing and shredding Dark resistance. Since his basic attack converts to Dark damage, that resistance cut multiplies everything he does. Damage taken from outside the aura is reduced another 20%, stacking to 40%.

The critical timing detail: the aura clears at the start of his turn, and it is granted before attacking — so he has to attack every turn to have it at all. (5:39)

Grand Master is the situational alternative: +30% across the board, damage reduction scaling with nearby enemies to 40%, and a hard rule that enemy damage reduction is capped at 50% before he attacks or counters — aimed squarely at the 95-100% reduction stacks now in circulation. It also grants unconditional Preemptive Attack, which matters because his ATK is high enough that his counterattack can kill your own unit outright. (3:34)

Sword Master+10 more → he dismisses: its bonus is combat-only, and almost everything Cyrano casts is an area skill. (7:12)

Veil of Chaos, and how to break it

Veil of Chaos hides every buff and debuff on him. That is not cosmetic — Heidelun's Root Hacker and Chaos Blinger scale off counting the target's buffs and debuffs, and hidden ones don't count, so a veiled Cyrano takes very little from her. Once it drops, he takes a great deal. (7:44)

Breaking it requires three attacks from players — summons do not count, and he confirms multi-hit skills like Null Spread do not stack it multiple times. It also expires on its own after two turns, which is why "Cyrano is weak on turn 3" is a real pattern. (8:45)

Be careful how you break it. When it drops he gains a shield worth 50% of ATK, and every debuff on him is copied to enemies within 3 tiles before his own are cleared. Stun him and then pop the veil and you hand the stun to your own front line.

The attacks

Vacuum Asura Blade picks between a wide sweep (3x4, 120%, falling to 90% when several targets are hit) and a narrow one (160% over 4 tiles ahead). Crucially it can attack Banished targets — including Heidelun in Entropy Ghost — so nothing hides from him. (10:19)

Gale Phantom Slash is a 90% charge that passes through, stripping all TP. Kill something with it and the extra action lets him keep going, which is how he ends up deep in the enemy formation. (11:44)

The ultimate is what upgrades him: 170% in 3 rings, and it grants Ruinous Momentum for three turns — 20% physical lifesteal and 30% of ATK as DEF-ignoring Dark damage after every attack and counter. Under Abyss Blade's resistance shred that is a large number. Getting the ultimate out early is a real objective. (12:24)

Ectoplasm is a mobility and buff-laundering tool

Ectoplasm leaves a copy behind and moves him forward, splitting his health and sharing 35% of incoming damage with the copy. Absorbing or recalling it grants an extra action, and he takes back its HP and buffs.

So the advanced line is: send Cyrano forward alone, have your supports buff the copy standing safely at the back, then absorb it next turn and collect everything at once. At its simplest it is a 2-tile move plus an extra action for 3 more. (13:56)

Gear: the Inheritor set is not optional

He is unusually firm here. Without the Inheritor set Cyrano loses roughly half his performance, because the set's shield below 50% HP is what keeps him alive after Ectoplasm halves him. Without that shield he simply dies.

Failing that, Envoy of the Storm, then One Against a Thousand — but he describes both as making do. Accessory: Chronicle of the Genesis War at ★5, because that is where its shield appears; otherwise Black Banner. (15:57)

Old Excalibur: ★3 minimum, and mandatory

Before combat it converts your area-damage bonus into ATK at 100%, which is the quiet reason to stack area damage on him: it raises area damage and ordinary combat damage.

Casting a skill or killing grants Advent of Doom (Physical Penetration and area damage, 2 stacks), and at full stacks it applies Call of Doom to enemies: +20% damage taken and reduced healing received.

★3 is the floor because that is where Soul Craving appears — with 1 TP or less, his basic attack becomes Soul Drain: steals 1 TP, deals 130% of ATK as Dark physical damage, and applies a Weaken. Since Ectoplasm leaves him at low TP, Soul Drain is effectively his normal attack. ★4 adds area resistance, ★5 adds Call of Doom. (20:07)

Runes

He runs two Emperor runes in slots 1 and 2 for the area-skill damage, with Inheritor runes filling the rest. Without either, his recommendation is Chaos, Sun, Sun, Ultimate, Ultimate, Chaos — slot 5's Ultimate carries shield increase, which suits him, and slot 2's Sun is percentage ATK. (21:38)

Manifestation

2 drops the kill-triggered extra action to a 1-turn cooldown and grants Critical Regeneration 3 with TP on revive — both of which used to require full manifestation.

4 is the value line: killing a player or summon returns TP. Combined with Soul Drain stealing one, that is how he reaches 5 TP and fires the ultimate on turn one. Full manifestation only moves numbers, and adds a TP refund on Ectoplasm. (24:14)

Artifacts

Warrior's Essence+10 more → wants melee skills and almost everything he casts is ranged area — even Soul Drain is ranged. Only Gale Phantom Slash qualifies. Chain Crest he calls meaningless.

Flash Combo Secret Manual+4 more → is the practical pick: ATK, Physical Penetration and Crit, with Focus granted on that same melee area skill.

Grey Afterimage, his exclusive, is what you use at full manifestation — Crit damage reduction, fixed-damage resistance (which is what lets him survive Black Prince's Ultimate Grima), +10% Max HP, Counter Range +1, and debuff clear whenever he gains a shield, which he gains constantly. (25:17)
