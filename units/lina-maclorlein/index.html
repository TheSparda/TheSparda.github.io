Signature Gear

Guide

Performance:

  • Base class Sister; Tier 3 classes are Returner / Mater / Oracle; ultimate is Divine Eos. Reworked into a staff character so she is no longer a martial artist, and all of the original's melee/fighting skills were cut.
  • Unique passive "Mediating Hand": on single-target skill to an ally, dispel 1-3 debuffs and grant the 3-4 turn buff; on turn end if no ally holds the buff, grant it to the lowest-HP ally; per ally holding it, heal +5-10% and Divine Eos TP cost -1 each. Praised as excellent: strong debuff cleansing plus easy access to the powerful full-team heal Divine Eos.
  • Tier 3 classes: Returner (Radiance-specialized), Mater (has revive), Oracle (specialized in "wifi heal").
  • Returner learns exclusive skill "Recall"; spell power up to +40% on Radiance skills, 80% chance to gain a beneficial effect on Radiance-cast turns; rarely triggers Sunlight (100% spell-power damage + remove 2 buffs to enemies, 100% heal + remove 2 debuffs to allies) but too low-chance to rely on. Useful utility but random and requires the separate Priest-Returner tree, so lowest priority.
  • Mater: once-per-stage revive at up to 100% HP; learns the main AoE heal Abydos Circle plus revive, recommended as the endgame class. Lack of spell-power boost is a shame but she isn't a dealer anyway, so compensate with armor/accessories.
  • Oracle: on action end, heal allies adjacent to an enemy for up to 40% spell power and remove 1 debuff (wifi heal). More fragile than Mater but the wifi heal is so useful it is recommended for raids.
  • Core skills: Heal, Warrant Barrier, Abydos Circle. Exclusive skills: Returner's Recall and Oracle's Trance.
  • Heal (TP 1, cooldown 1): with her passive debuff removal, performs at Esmeralda's Recovery tier.
  • Warrant Barrier (TP 2, cooldown 3): 120% spell-power shield for 2 turns +1 TP; single-target so it can also trigger her passive debuff removal. Excellent utility.
  • Abydos Circle (TP 2): AoE instant heal 50% spell power + 2-turn "Abydos Light" dot heal (20% HP on turn end) and -20% crit-received. Her core heal; flawless except it can't remove debuffs (AoE).
  • Recall (TP 2, cooldown 2): teleport an ally + heal 80% spell power. Good utility for gimmick solving.
  • Trance (TP 1, cooldown 4): wifi heal, self immune to action-control, allies healed 50% spell power on their turn end regardless of range; with Oracle passive, adjacent allies heal 90%. Very useful but its 4-turn cooldown (her longest) is a drawback.
  • Divine Eos (ultimate): heals all allies 90% spell power and removes 1 debuff each with no range limit; allies at or below 50% HP heal +45%. Costs 5 TP but the passive can reduce it.

Recommended weapon and gear:

  • Exclusive weapon "Sacred Guide" grants a DEF/RES "Sacred Wooden Sword" buff and, as a Lina-only effect, +1 healing skill range. Very useful since her move is only 3 and heal range is short; at 5-awakening the buff also adds ATK/Spell power for combat support.
  • Armor: Light's Devotion set (Dahlia Igrit costume motif, gives healing buff), or Cardinal set (Rubens Heisenberg pope costume motif, chance to gain TP). Pope set's random cooldown cut is iffy since most skills have short cooldowns, but good if maining Oracle + Trance (cooldown 4).
  • Accessories: healer staples Jewel of Echo (Legendary) and Chalice of Devotion (Hero).

Evaluation:

  • Counted alongside Esmeralda and Chaos Deimos as one of the "Three Generals," one of Genesis War Mobile's top healers. Has everything a healer needs: single-target heal, AoE heal, debuff removal, wifi heal; Light attribute so no weakness; even has a revive. She was somewhat overshadowed by Esmeralda (who came with re-action and an AoE-barrier exclusive weapon and is strong at combat support), but Lina still has the irreplaceable Recall, better debuff handling, and the matchup-neutral Light attribute, so she is not without her own edges.
  • No explicit S/A/B tier letter rating is present in the text.

Awakening/Manifestation progression: - Stage 1: Ultimate Enhancement 1; Stat Enhancement 1 - Stage 2: Unique Passive Enhancement 1; Stat Enhancement 2 - Stage 3: Ultimate Enhancement 2; Acquire Artifact - Stage 4: Unique Passive Enhancement 2; Artifact Enhancement 1 - Stage 5: Ultimate Enhancement 3; Artifact Enhancement 2 - Stage 6: Unique Passive Enhancement 3; Artifact Enhancement 3 Reference for what the enhancements upgrade (from the passive/ultimate sections): - Unique Passive "Mediating Hand": scales from base (dispel 1 debuff, 3 turns, +5% heal per ally with the buff, TP reduction) at Initial, to dispel 3 / 4 turns / +10% heal / TP -1 at Max Awakening, to "Mediating Hand 7" at Max Awakening: dispel 3, 4 turns, +10% heal, TP -1, plus targets gain "Vow of Recovery 3" for 2 turns; self-heal on turn end rises to 65% of spell power (from 20% -> 50% -> 65%). "Vow of Recovery 3" (lasts 2): when hit by a combat attack, heal 30% of caster spell power after combat. - Ultimate "Divine Eos": allies heal 70% -> 100% -> 115% of spell power, dispel 1 debuff each; allies at or below 50% HP get extra heal 35% -> 50% -> 65%. At Max Awakening it also grants "Blessing Incantation 3": ATK/Crit rate/DEF/RES +15%, incoming crit rate -15%.
