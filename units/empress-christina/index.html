Guide

No explicit S/A/B tier rating is present in the text.

Performance:

  • Base class Gunner; Tier 3 classes are Ace/Specialist/Empress Gunner; ultimate is Inferno Support Bombing.
  • Unique passive "Empress's Seal" is described as an upgraded active-type turret: it now shoots after an ally is hit (earlier than the old "enemy ends turn" timing), and at Manifestation Stage 2 it even fires before the ally is hit, plus gains buff-removal immunity. Range-damage reduction makes it strong against AoE. The buff-removal immunity at Manifestation 2 is widely seen as a sniper counter to Black Prince's buff-removing AoE "Atomic Blast," since buff removal fails and range damage is reduced, greatly cutting Atomic's damage. Self-heal based on ATK at 6-awakening is called a nice touch since Gunners are squishy.
  • Classes: Ace goes all-in on crit rate (max bullets 3; +30% crit on bullet-consuming attacks per its passive) and learns Bullseye; near-always applies crit boost since only the ultimate does not consume bullets, and learns two strong nukes (Rapid Fire and Bullseye). Specialist learns "Tactical Round Reload," max bullets 4, gets Cover Fire at stage start, up to +25% crit; described as nearly a strict upgrade over Ace (loses 5% crit but gains ATK buff and half-cover). Empress Gunner learns "Empress's Banner"; on a basic-attack kill gains extra attack/skill, on non-kill gains 1 TP, and skill use grants the 2-turn "Imperial Secret Arts" buff (preemptive strike, physical penetration +30%, ATK +30%).
  • Key skills: all gun attacks +25% damage; multi-shot skills greatly boosted. Bullseye. electric/blazing/cold rounds; pairing cold round with Double Down can instantly freeze; Bullseye's 3-shot can stack 3 debuffs. TP refund on kill-fail, low usage burden. cooldown reduction + ranged-dealer buff area zone, also useful as a self-buff.
  • Ultimate Inferno Support Bombardment: tile-targeted AoE (very wide, easy to use), 80%~95% of ATK to enemies within 4 tiles of the chosen tile within 5, 30%~35% chance of +40% ATK, plus 2-turn move -2, no extra move/action/escort. AoE move -2 nearly turns enemies into sluggards; escort-cutting can neutralize escort-tank formations. Noted as not yet needed in PvE (no maps stack escort tanks), but useful in PvP against strong tank lines like Dunamis and Durant. Comment: her switch to blue attribute was for sniping Dunamis.

Recommended gear:

  • Signature weapon Royal Vermilion: pistol with bonus ATK stat; weapon skill +1 max bullets and 40%~100% chance of an extra hit for 30% of ATK after combat attacks; Empress-exclusive effect grants "Imperial Privilege" buff (+5%~10% crit rate/crit damage on dealing damage), stacks to 3, easy to max, no other effects so not mandatory.
  • Armor: since most skills scale on ATK, ATK sets like Chivalrous or Assassin/Slayer are suitable; with a high-awakening signature weapon and Ace/Specialist going crit, Assassin or Crow sets are also viable.
  • Accessory: Wolf Fang for ATK is suitable; a legendary Goyeongban could be given, but Gunners are too squishy to survive-and-heal a crit, so it is a bit wasteful just to patch survivability.

Evaluation:

  • A flexible position-swapping character: can be a supporter (cooldown reduction, range-damage reduction, ranged-dealer buffs for allies; move-down and escort-ban debuffs on enemies), a dealer, or a dedicated debuffer. ATK-based self-heal at high awakening helps in world-boss raids where all attacks are AoE.
  • At full Manifestation, with range-damage reduction and buff-removal immunity, she can be used as a Black Prince counter; shooting before allies are hit also helps protect them.
  • As a ranged dealer she is squishy, but her lover Albatini later released as an escort-capable Fighter, and Empress Christina came with a passive that always escorts him, so pairing them patches this weakness. Albatini also gains a revive on his passive when Christina is present, so their synergy is excellent; Albatini is also light attribute, making them well suited to combine.

Awakening/Manifestation progression: Two tracks per stage: Manifestation and Inner Cube. - Stage 1: Ultimate Enhancement 1 / Stat Enhancement 1. - Stage 2: Unique Passive Enhancement 1 / Stat Enhancement 2. - Stage 3: Ultimate Enhancement 2 / Artifact Acquired. - Stage 4: Unique Passive Enhancement 2 / Artifact Enhancement 1. - Stage 5: Ultimate Enhancement 3 / Artifact Enhancement 2. - Stage 6: Unique Passive Enhancement 3 / Artifact Enhancement 3. Numeric effects referenced by these enhancements (from passive/ult text): - Passive "Empress's Seal" scales First Acquisition -> Max Awakening -> Max Manifestation: gun hit 40% -> 55% -> 80% of ATK; ranged damage taken reduced 20% -> 35% -> 50%; buff on random ally Empress's Seal 1 -> 4 -> 7 (2 turns); healing added at 30% -> 80% of ATK; crit rate -50% and buff-removal immunity added at Max Manifestation. - Ultimate "Inferno Support Bombardment": 80% -> 95% -> 120% of ATK; extra-hit chance 30% -> 35% -> 50% for 40% -> 45% -> 50% of ATK.

KR Creator Guide

Character review of Empress Christina by 하막군의게임튜브 · 2026-08-04

The reviewer calls Empress Christina an ultimate-first character, and the ultimate is what caused all the argument when she was announced. Everything else — the banner, the self-buffs, the free extra attacks — is real but gets overshadowed. Her ATK is decent for a gunner and her follow-up damage adds up, so she isn't only a one-trick unit. (12:43)

His expectation was that she would appear on a lot of defence teams, because the opening play is simply to walk her forward and fire the ultimate on turn one or two. (13:31)

The ultimate has absurd range

Inferno Support Fire designates a tile up to 5 away and hits everything within 4 tiles of it. Combined with 3 Movement, the reviewer works it out: she can reach a target 12 tiles away in a single action. In guild battle, standing her at the front means bombarding the enemy's whole formation from where she starts.

The damage is not small either — 120% plus a 50% chance of more at full manifestation, so up to 170%. At his own 3 copies it already kills most 1,000-point characters outright. (5:49)

Because it's a tile target with that footprint, there is no dodging it. His counterplay suggestions are to eat it behind an Isabel Ripine wall or a Horn of Glory and clear the debuffs afterwards. (14:32)

Daze is the reason the ultimate hurts so much

Daze is Movement -2 plus no extra moves, no extra actions, and — new — cannot be guarded. The reviewer tested the guard clause carefully: it has to be applied to the tank. A protected ally carrying Daze can still be guarded; it's the guardian who must be dazed for the protection to fail. (3:11)

So the line is: open with the ultimate, freeze the enemy group with Daze, and have the rest of your team clean up. (6:51)

Class: Empress Gunner

Empress Gunner is straightforward and good. Killing with a basic attack grants an extra attack or skill use; failing to kill returns 1 TP instead. Casting any skill grants Imperial Secret for two turns: Preemptive Attack, +30% ATK and +30% Physical Penetration.

He flags the important bit: the kill-triggered extra attack has no cap. Keep killing with basic attacks and she keeps going. (2:08)

Empress's Banner

Empress's Banner cuts the cooldowns of all normal skills for allies within 2 tiles and plants a flag on her tile, granting her an extra move.

The flag grants allies inside it +5% Crit Rate and Crit damage per point of their attack range. He works an example: Erica, with 4 range, gains +20% of each just by standing near it.

Two things worth knowing: the flag persists until Christina re-casts it elsewhere or an enemy steps onto it — and it survives her death. (3:41)

Gear

Black Crow for the armour. Accessory: Serrated Arrowhead, since everything she does bar the ultimate is a combat attack; Ancient Hero's Ring if her Crit isn't yet where it needs to be. (7:31)

The weapon wants full awakening

+1 max ammo, Royal Privilege stacking +5% Crit Rate and Crit damage up to 3, and a 25% chance of a 30% follow-up after a combat attack with ammo remaining. That follow-up chance climbs to 100% at ★6.

Royal Privilege stacks very easily — a multi-target skill applies all 3 at once, and even a basic attack banks 2 because the shot and the round both count. But his verdict is honest: excellent at ★6, and not really needed below that. (7:52)

Runes

All Chaos. Ultimate in slots 3 and 4 if you feel short on health. (9:55)

Manifestation

2 adds buff-removal immunity — the most useful single step. 4 adds Crit-Rate-taken reduction, rising to 50% at 6. His pick for general use is 2; 4 if you want everything. (10:25)

Artifact: Meticulous Preparation, and it isn't close

Meticulous Preparation+11 more → starts her at 4 TP with +30% ATK — which means the ultimate goes off on turn one. Given how much of her value is in that ultimate, he expects nearly everyone to run it. (11:57)

Overload Loader+5 more → adds penetration and ammo. Initial Armor-Piercing Magazine+3 more → starts her with armour-piercing rounds, which is a real tactical option since firing all three costs 3 TP. Both lose to opening with the ultimate. (11:27)
