Signature Gear

Guide

Note: The following is NamuWiki community assessment (KR server), subjective community opinion.

Performance: Base class is Archer; Tier 3 classes are Eagle / Bowmaster / Sniper. The ultimate is Firebrand as in the original, and his exclusive weapon is "Hellfire Arcus", shared with his child. His unique passive is "Curtis's Divine Archer" - critical damage rises 10~20% with no conditions, and at the end of the character's action he gains a "Finishing Shot" buff until the start of his next turn. This buff grants critical rate of 10%~40% depending on awakening level with no conditions, and if there is an enemy within range that ended its turn at 30% HP or below, he fires an arrow once per turn dealing 80%~100% of attack as damage. From awakening level 5, if the target does not die, it adds a "Slow" debuff that reduces movement by 2 and blocks extra movement and extra actions for 1 turn.

The ultimate is Firebrand. It designates 1 enemy within 2~4 tiles, deals attack-scaled damage to enemies within 3 tiles around it, then creates a fire zone within the area at 20% chance lasting 2 turns. A style that compensates for its low power with fire-zone creation.

Hellfire Arcus is a Legend bow that, before a battle attack, has a chance to apply the "Fire Brand" debuff which increases damage the enemy takes. When Ariana uses it, the debuff duration increases to 2 turns. At low awakening the RNG is heavy, but once it lands the damage steadily increases, so it is a well-regarded bow.

Evaluation: Nicknamed "Bow-grandpa". Although an extremely early character, the "One-Strike Shot" learned in the Sniper class is powerful, so he is still well-used in Knightclan Wars and Kraken Raids as a sharpshooter. Because even now, over a year after launch, there are only 4 archer-type characters (only 7 even counting gunners), long-range snipers hold a high status. For gear, thanks to the passive he already has high base crit rate and crit damage, so the standard is to go crit-focused such as the Assassin 4-set; for accessories, the Arrowhead is good.

Awakening2025-04-01

Expanded Inner Break awakening

  • Added Inner Break awakening. Stages 1/3/5 enhance ultimate 'Flame Blade': at max, deals 120% ATK to target and enemies within 3 tiles, with a 35% chance to create a 3-turn Flame Zone.
  • Flame Zone: enemies inside take 10% of Max HP as damage at turn end (capped by the caster's M-ATK).

  • Stage 3 instantly equips the artifact; stages 4/5/6 upgrade artifacts 'Elemental Overload' (advantageous-element damage +10% to +25%) and 'Optimal Condition' (Crit Damage taken -20% at full HP).
