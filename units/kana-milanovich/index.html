Guide

Note: The following is NamuWiki community assessment (KR server), subjective community opinion.

Performance overview: Base job is Gunner. Tier 2 has the exclusive class "Insurgent"; Tier 3 classes are Spitfire / Specialist / Demolition. Ultimate is "Crimson Inferno".

Unique passive "Victory Idol": Each time all bullets are emptied, gain the "Victory Idol" buff (ult TP cost -1, up to 4 stacks, removed on use), and when an ally kills an enemy she damaged this turn she gains "Tension Up". From Awakening 4 onward, gaining Tension Up grants an extra action. The extra-action cooldown is 4 turns by default, drops to 3 from Awakening 5, and with Awakening opened it disappears at Stage 4 (1/turn) and becomes 2/turn at Stage 6, a big upgrade.

  • Tension Up: Attack/crit chance +515%; magic damage taken -515%; max 2 stacks, removed after attacking an enemy; at Stage 6: Movement +1.
  • A passive that grants an extra action and makes the ultimate easier to use when an ally kills an enemy she damaged. Best to hit as many enemies as possible, though the extra action's long cooldown is a shame.

Classes:

  • Spitfire learns exclusive skill "Spray Fire", max 4 bullets, passive gives Attack/crit +20% when bullets are 0 at turn start. Damage boost is excellent but has the strictest conditions. Spray Fire is a decent skill; Kana has Heat Finale though, so it's not essential.
  • Specialist learns "Tactical Round Load", max 4 bullets, gains "Cover Fire" buff at stage start plus up to +25% crit on bullet-consuming attacks. Cover Fire: Attack +5% and treated as "Half Cover" (no crit from enemy directional attacks, damage -25%). Class Jane Shore brought out first; Kana can do Cold Round double-tap stun via Double Down, but importance is low since she supplies Blaze Rounds via her exclusive weapon and Heat Finale.
  • Demolition is the exclusive Insurgent-Demolition tree class, learns "Heat Finale", max 3 bullets. Reduces enemy fire resistance up to 80% for 2 turns and applies "Boiling Rage" debuff; if the enemy takes lethal damage the ally who dealt it gains TP 1. Kana also gains +50% physical penetration fighting debuffed enemies. Fire-support specialist class; recommended as 1st priority to raise since Insurgent and Demolition teach Kana's main exclusive skills. Note: for Blaze Round only the post-hit 30% magic damage counts as fire.

Core skills and ultimate:

  • No Thanks!: TP 1. Applies "No Thanks!" debuff to 1 target within 2-4 tiles for 1 turn. Up to 4 stacks: per stack received crit chance +10%, damage taken +5%; taking damage adds stacks and duration. Cooldown 3. Effectively a 4-turn debuff of +40% crit-taken / +20% damage-taken. (Needs verification, but) a Gunner attack counts as 2 hits, so applying No Thanks then a basic attack instantly reaches 3 stacks, and Double Down reaches 4.
  • Spray Fire: TP 3, all bullets. 80% AoE damage in a direction, pushes 1 tile; hits limited by bullet count. Gunner's main AoE; with a magic-bullet-filled magazine the ratio rises to 105%. Less important since Kana has Heat Finale.
  • Tactical Round Load: TP 1, cooldown 3. Empties bullets then loads Shock/Blaze/Cold round to max. Shock Round: +35% extra magic damage and "Shock" debuff. Blaze Round: +30% extra magic damage and "Blaze" debuff. Cold Round: +20% extra magic damage and "Cold Slow" debuff. Jane Shore brought this out first; comboing with Double Down lands 2 debuffs at once (e.g. Cold Round then Double Down for instant freeze). Blaze counts as fire so pairs well with Demolition, but low priority since Kana supplies Blaze via exclusive weapon and Heat Finale.
  • Heat Finale: TP 1, 1 bullet, cooldown 3. Throws a bomb and fires: 80% damage to target, 30% fire damage to enemies within 2 tiles around it, then dumps remaining bullets and loads Blaze Rounds to max. Cannot be countered or guarded. Kana's main AoE; wide range and impact-type AoE make it easy to use, and it fills the magazine with Demolition-synergy Blaze Rounds for a good post-action state.
  • Crimson Inferno (ultimate): Targets 1 enemy within 2-5 tiles for 130~145% damage plus 75% to enemies within 3 rings around the target; a 7x7 AoE. Creates a Hellfire Zone lasting 2 turns at 20% chance. Hellfire Zone: entering or ending an action on that tile takes 10% of total HP as damage (not exceeding caster's attack). Fire damage with a huge 7x7 range makes synergy with the passive/Demolition excellent. Kana can cut its TP cost to 1 via passive and gets TP support from Boiling Rage, so it's easy to use, even easier with the exclusive weapon. Note Boiling Rage lands after Inferno detonates, so hard to boost Inferno with it; reduce TP cost and fire Crimson Inferno after Heat Finale.

Exclusive weapon and recommended gear:

  • Crimson Revolution: added stats Attack and crit chance. At turn end, 6085% chance to load 1 Blaze Round and gain "Bullet Delivery" buff. Kana raises load chance +10% and Bullet Delivery becomes "Lucky Draw"; if already holding Lucky Draw, gains "Lucky Kana" for 2 turns. Bullet Delivery: preemptive attack, crit damage +1020%, load 1 Blaze Round on kill (removed when bullets reach 0). Lucky Draw: preemptive attack, crit damage +15~30%, load 1 Blaze Round on kill; when bullets reach 0 gain Lucky Kana for 2 turns (unlimited duration). Lucky Kana: received crit chance -90%, 90% chance to resist debuffs, 90% chance for TP +1 at ally turn start; 2 turns. A rare Gunner exclusive weapon not requiring high awakening; luck-dependent but usually procs about once per 2 turns, milder than Christian's M.C Valentine or Jane Shore's Specter's Nightmare. Note Lucky Kana is a survival/sustain buff and lacks Lucky Draw's crit-damage boost.
  • Armor: like fellow Gunners Christian/Jane her defense is paper-thin, so ignore survival and stack offense. Passive gives crit 515%, No Thanks! 40%, exclusive weapon 510%, so crit-focused suits her. Legendary: Assassin set or Crow; Hero grade: Assassin or Xiake. Note crit gives no special return for Kana and Hellfire Zone damage has an attack cap, so leaning into attack power is also viable.
  • Accessory: Wolf Fang for attack; Fire Crest for debuff-focus (also gives crit and enhances ally support). If no Fire Crest use the Hero-grade minor version Flame Crystal. Both require a crit to land the debuff, so secure some crit.

Evaluation:

  • A Light-attribute support Gunner who backs up allies with strong damage-taken debuffs, fire-specialized buffs, and TP generation. "No Thanks!" boosts single-target damage; the -80% fire resistance from Demolition massively boosts Gunners' Blaze Rounds, Mages' Flame Ball, Elizabeth's Yeonhongseom, Ophelia's Prominence and Flare Meteor, Cordelia's special explosive arrows / cute traps / Napalm Blossom, and Cals's Cheonjipayeolmu. Being weakness-free Light, she's good in any composition. The first Light-attribute ranged position since Erica Myers, differentiated by debuff-based ally support as her main role.

No explicit S/A/B tier rating present in the text.

KR Creator Guide

Character review of Kana Milanovich by 하막군의게임튜브 · 2026-08-04

Kana Milanovich is, in the reviewer's judgement, a raid character first. Her two signature debuffs both scale with a long fight, and her extra-action condition is awkward in PvP. He rates the artwork among the best in the game and notes people in his guild pulled on the illustration alone. (15:38)

In PvP her use is as a debuffer for a fire team — see Boiling Rage below.

The extra action needs an ally to land the kill

Victory Idol grants Tension Up and an extra action when an enemy she damaged this turn is killed — but he tests it and the condition is strict: she cannot be the one who kills it. An ally has to finish the target.

Worse for raids, the wording is "defeats", not "deals lethal damage" — so killing something that revives doesn't count either. (1:32, 15:38)

At 2 manifestations the extra action loses its cooldown entirely; at full manifestation she can take two per turn. (12:00, 13:02)

Boiling Rage is an 80% Fire resistance shred

Demolition applies Boiling Rage to anything she damages: -80% Fire resistance, +50% Physical Penetration for her against it, and 1 TP on a kill.

The reviewer tested how resistance shred actually applies and it is not a modest adjustment — it multiplies final damage. He demonstrates it: Elizabeth Pandragon's fire skill hits a Boiling Rage target for 6,464 and an untouched one for 3,591. Roughly 1.8×. (3:07, 4:43)

It is trivially easy to apply — anything she damages gets it, so Suppressing Fire+5 more → tags a whole group at once. That makes her a buffer in disguise for Clausewitz's Flame Ball, Elizabeth Pandragon's fire skills, Beradin's Triple Elemental Blast and any fire ultimate. (4:12)

No Way! never expires

No Way! applies a stacking -10% Crit-taken and -5% damage dealt, to 4 stacks — and here is the part that matters: every subsequent hit extends its duration, from anyone.

He demonstrates it climbing from 4 turns to 5 to 6 as different units attack the same target. In a raid it simply never falls off. He calls it close to the strongest raid skill in the game. (5:45, 6:47)

Heat Finale needs the debuff first

Heat Finale deals 80% to the target and 30% around it, which is weak on its own. It's a fire skill, so Boiling Rage nearly doubles it — apply the debuff first, always.

One limitation: it's tagged a combat attack, not an area attack, so it does nothing against Joan Kartright, Emilio and others who blunt combat attacks specifically. (7:48)

The weapon: one copy is enough

Crimson Revolution loads a round at end of turn with a 60% chance and grants Lucky Draw; Lucky Kana follows, giving -90% Crit-taken, a 90% debuff resist and a 90% chance of 1 TP at turn start.

He found the loop easier to sustain than expected: extra actions re-roll Lucky Draw, the loaded round gets spent on a basic attack, hitting 0 ammo re-grants Lucky Kana, and round it goes.

Awakening isn't needed — a single copy is fine. Jane Shore's weapon is a good alternative, or a Hero-grade gun. (8:50, 10:22)

Gear

Black Crow with Ancient Hero's Ring or Serrated Arrowhead. (10:56)

Runes

All Chaos. Conspiracy in slots 1 and 6 if you're short on Crit. Nothing else to tune. (10:56)

Manifestation

The ultimate gains Stun at the first step, then cooldown reduction on a kill — which he rates as near-pointless, since TP is the limiting factor and you can't chain it anyway.

The passive is where the value is: 2 removes the extra action's cooldown and adds Fiery Cheer (area-skill damage, Movement, cheaper fire skills) — though Fiery Cheer needs the same ally-kill condition, so it rarely appears in PvP. Full manifestation allows two extra actions a turn, which is a PvE clearing tool. (11:29, 12:32)

Artifacts

Meticulous Preparation+10 more → is his pick: 4 TP at game start with +30% ATK, so she opens harder and is more likely to set up a kill for an ally.

Overload Loader+4 more →'s 60% Physical Penetration is genuinely large but still loses to opening strong. Wind Step+10 more → he expects nobody to use. (13:32)
