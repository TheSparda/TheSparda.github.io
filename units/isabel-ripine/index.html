Signature Gear

Guide

  • Performance overview: Base class Soldier. Tier 3 classes are Warlord / Lanceward / Iron Guardian, all unique classes, a big deal since she is the first spear-and-shield unit in a long while (since Vincent Bernstein). Ultimate "Heavenly Sky Single Slash" comes from the Longinus spear; her exclusive weapon is the Longinus armament (spear/shield). Unique passive "Woman of Steel" unconditionally raises DEF and RES, guards adjacent allies with reduced damage while guarding, very simple but solid. From 5-awakening, counterattack range +1.

  • Classes: All three Tier 3 classes create control zones granting cover to adjacent tiles, and all are unique classes.

  • Warlord: gains the main exclusive skill 'Battle Command'; has an OP passive (taunt immunity, +assist damage, ATK/DEF/RES +30% in combat when allies nearby, on Battle Command use cooldown reduction + TP 1 recovery). "Basically built to be the main pick." Raise Warlord first, no question.

  • Lanceward: gains 'Ground Crush'; unconditionally gains ATK-up and applies ATK-down to enemies within 3 tiles. Isabel goes all-in on DEF so the class itself is mediocre, but Ground Crush is a DEF-based AoE that debuffs ATK/spell power, worth securing.

  • Iron Guardian: follows the Guardian to Iron Guardian tree, gains 'Iron Wall Advance'; passive unconditionally gives DEF-up, physical damage reduction, and stun + skill-control immunity. A top-tier defensive class. If you only want a guard-tank, this class alone is enough.

  • Key skills & ultimate: Battle Command is the "flower" of Isabel, a powerful main skill choosing one of three effects (like three skills merged into one), costing only TP 2 with cooldown 2; with Warlord it becomes TP 1 / cooldown 1 usable every turn. Must-have.

  • Inspire: to one non-self ally, ATK +20% buff, removes/immunizes taunt, and reduces the longest-cooldown skill by 1 turn; removed after combat.

  • Secure Retreat: to one non-self ally, DEF/RES +30% and grants 'Withdraw' (extra move before action ends after a combat attack).

  • Focused Combat: to one enemy, physical damage taken +10%; when the enemy is attacked it gains Weakness Exposure (+10% damage taken, +10% crit chance) and the attacker gains 'Concentration' (TP 1 on action end).

  • Ground Crush: 3x4 AoE ignoring cover, 110% of DEF as physical fixed damage, -20% ATK/spell power and 'Neutralize' debuff (move -1, no extra move/action). Deals 3 to installations, excellent for destroying them. TP 2, cooldown 3.

  • Iron Wall Advance: move to a tile within range 3; gain 'Iron Wall Advance' buff (physical damage taken -10% for 3 turns, per adjacent ally at destination) and grant 'Guardian Formation' (AoE damage taken -15% for 2 turns) to allies within 2 tiles; also guards allies within 2 tiles for 2 turns. A defensive skill expanding guard range to 2 tiles and reducing AoE damage.

  • Ultimate Heavenly Sky Single Slash: charge in a chosen direction, ATK-based + DEF-based damage, stunning hit enemies for 1 turn; excellent guaranteed AoE 1-turn stun. Since Isabel stacks DEF, the ATK-based component feels like a balance adjustment, but the exclusive weapon's DEF-based ATK-up gives good synergy.

  • Exclusive weapon & recommended gear: Longinus armament, the first spear/shield exclusive weapon since Bernstein, appearing past the 1-year service mark. Originally the Longinus spear in the source but renamed because it now includes a shield. Weapon skill: DEF-proportional ATK-up and post-combat ATK-based physical damage; with Isabel the multiplier rises and the ATK-based physical damage timing shifts from post-combat to pre-combat. Extra stats are HP and damage-reduction (good for tanks), though HP-increase instead of DEF feels like a strong balance adjustment since her kit is mostly DEF-based. Armor: go all-in on DEF. Consider the "Lord of Fierce Strike" set (Dycus Rockwood motif) for DEF and DEF-proportional ATK-up, or the "Commander of Reversal" set (Vincent Bernstein motif) for some RES. Accessories: hero/legendary greaves for DEF, censer for HP recovery, or Cursed Eye for ATK-down; any tank accessory works.

  • Evaluation: A guard-tank specialized in nullifying physical damage with strong buff/debuff skills. Because the 'Focused Combat' debuff from Battle Command provides both damage-up and TP supply, she is described as performing at the level of Landam Kelvins and Gishne Driford combined; since she shares Landam's attribute she can fully replace him. Both were once meta-defining characters, so "combining the two" means truly insane performance.

  • As of mid-2026, she is rated one of the tank "Big Three" alongside Dunamis Rembrandt and Commander Siren Woodville. A well-built Isabel is considered nearly essential to clear the hardest current stage, Hero's Grave Challenge Floor 65; on selective banners it is said to invest heavily in Isabel beyond just securing her (needing high awakening / high manifestation).

  • After the "Investment-Return Battle" implementation she was used only in PvE for a while, but the June 16, 2026 update buffed her overall so she can be used in PvP again. Battle Command's TP cost was removed and cooldown reduced to 1, so it can be used every turn even outside Warlord. As of mid-2026, the Lanceward class she shares with Riddel gives cooldown reduction and Concentration on Ground Crush/Smash use, enabling spamming, and the Iron Guardian class became extremely durable.

  • (No explicit S/A/B tier letter present; community frames her as tank "Big Three" tier.)

KR Creator Guide

Character review of Isabel Ripine by 하막군의게임튜브 · 2026-08-04

Isabel Ripine is a tank whose offence keeps surprising the reviewer. She scales attack off defence, her weapon fires lightning before every fight, and she deals a lot of fixed damage — so attacking her is genuinely unpleasant. He rates her good at everything: raids, PvP and tanking. His one reservation is that she doesn't yet displace Dunamis Rembrandt at the top, and like all tanks she is softer against strong mages. (18:54)

How to fight her, and how not to

The counter-intuitive part first. Her passive reduces damage taken by up to 30% while guarding, so hitting a protected ally to force the guard is the worst way to attack her.

He demonstrates the difference on camera: forcing a guard lands about 2,616 damage, while hitting Isabel Ripine directly lands 3,120. If you have to remove her, hit her. (1:35)

Class: Warlord, and Combat Command is the point

Warlord brings taunt immunity, more flanking damage, +30% ATK/DEF/RES with allies within 3 tiles, and — the part that matters — reduces Combat Command's cooldown and returns 1 TP when it is used. (2:37)

Combat Command offers three options:

  • Encourage — cooldown reduction on the longest skill, ATK up, taunt-clear immunity
  • Secure Retreat — DEF/RES up and Withdraw after attacking. Note: Withdraw only triggers if she actually makes a combat attack afterwards
  • Focused Combat — the one to know. Applied to an enemy: more physical damage taken, Weak Point Exposure, and Focus for the attacker

Focused Combat is why raid players are talking about her. Weak Point Exposure stacks each time the target is hit, and every ally who attacks that target gets 1 TP. Because Combat Command also grants an extra attack or skill afterwards, she can loop command → attack → command → attack indefinitely, holding the debuff up and feeding the whole team TP. He says she is being discussed as a Landam replacement for raids. (3:39, 5:12)

Lance Warden is the debuff-stacking alternative: Combat Alert cuts enemy ATK/M-ATK within 3 tiles, and Ground Crush — 110% of defence as fixed physical damage in a 3x4 — adds Incapacitate on top. Between the two he shows an enemy's ATK falling from 2,046 to 1,600. Worth taking when you specifically need the enemy made weak. (6:15, 8:19)

Iron Guardian is the pure-defence route: +10% physical damage reduction plus Stun and Skill Control immunity, and Ironwall Advance stacks another 10% physical reduction per nearby ally, up to 30%. That is 40% physical reduction before anything else — and stacked with Clausewitz's Solid Front, he says physical attacks essentially stop working. (9:22)

He also flags a probable bug: Ironwall Advance's 2-tile guard range is written as lasting two turns and in practice never expires. (10:04)

The ultimate

A 3x4 charge for 70% of ATK plus 70% of DEF, stunning everything it hits and dealing heavy damage to installations. One geometry note: Ground Crush's area starts on her own tile, the ultimate's starts on the tile in front — a one-tile difference that matters when lining it up. (10:55)

The weapon does a lot

It grants ATK worth 15% of DEF and a 50% chance before combat of 50% of ATK as fixed physical damage. At ★5 that becomes a guaranteed proc plus a buff strip; at ★6 it strips two.

Because "before combat" applies whether she attacks or is attacked, the lightning fires and the buffs come off every single fight. (12:28)

A timing detail he tested, for anyone who wondered: the resolution order is in-combat → before-combat → after-combat. So Silver's buff theft resolves first, then Isabel Ripine's weapon strips buffs, then the actual fight happens. (13:32)

Gear

Personal taste more than anything — he runs a defence-oriented set, and notes others build purely for maximum DEF. Accessory: Healing Censer, because she has no self-healing at all; a defence accessory if you want to push her armour higher instead. (14:05)

Runes

Boar, Boar, Ultimate, Ultimate, Boar, Boar is the baseline. All-Boar or a Boar/Conspiracy mix both work if you want to maximise defence. (15:10)

Manifestation

2 adds Steel Penetration — ATK and Physical Penetration. 4 improves the penetration. 6 is the big one: her guard range extends to 2 tiles. (15:43)

Artifacts

Oath of Retribution+2 more → is his expectation for most players: Action Control immunity when hit in melee, plus a large counterattack damage increase.

Peak Condition+12 more → he doesn't expect to see used. Resonance of the Shield reduces skill damage per nearby ally, which is good in isolation — but she already has so much damage reduction stacked that he questions spending the slot on more. (16:47)

Balance2026-06-16

Adjusted the Iron Lady tiered effects and active-skill usage

  • Unique passive 'Steel Lady' reworked: now guards adjacent ally players (within 2 tiles at rank 12) and reduces damage taken while guarding by 10/15/20% (down from 15/20/30% across ranks). Guard cannot be dispelled.
  • DEF/RES bonus retuned to +20/25% (rank 3-4), +30% (5-6), +35% (8), +40% (10), +45% (12); adds Max HP +10% at rank 10 and +20% at rank 12.
  • Counter range +1 from rank 5-6 onward.
  • Steel Penetration reworked to a dispel-immune stacking buff (2 turns): ATK +10%, Physical Penetration +10%, gained on skill cast / basic attack / counter (was combat-only ATK + DEF Penetration).
  • Class passive 'Battle Commander' buffed: now grants ATK/DEF/RES/Flanking damage +15/30% AND damage taken −10/20%, with combat command skill range +2 (replacing the old cooldown −1 / TP recovery); still immune to Taunt and Fear.
