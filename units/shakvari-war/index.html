Guide

Note: the following is NamuWiki community assessment (KR server), subjective community opinion.

  • Base class Fighter. Tier 2 has unique class Warbone; Tier 3 is Slasher / Dominator / Unbroken, all unique classes, fitting the God of War. Ultimate is Storm Sword, evoking her Genesis 3 Part 2 era.
  • Passive God of War: builds 2 stacks, -15% dmg, consumes 1 stack when hit at game start; gains Battle Fervor, un-dispellable, up to 8 stacks, buffs ATK/DEF/RES before skill use / before attacking / before being attacked / after gaining a buff. If no Battle Fervor was gained last turn or she is under action-control, stacks drop by 2 at turn start. On kill, gains an extra action once every 3~1 turns. Strong scaling passive: grows the longer combat lasts, gives extra action every turn at 6-star, and at 5-awaken+ supports the whole team via War's Authority once per game.
  • Slasher: learns Omega Swing; +15% ATK unconditionally, +30% crit chance on attack, applies 3-turn Bleed on slash/pierce crit. All-in on damage; bleed DoT also triggers off allies' attacks so contributes significant damage. High firepower and teaches the main skill Omega Swing, so it is generally recommended as the 1st priority build.
  • Dominator: learns Cry of Threat; ATK buff scales with target's debuff state (no debuff +30% / has debuff +50% / has Enfeeble-type debuff +80%); 40% chance to apply Fear: cannot act, +10% crit taken at turn start to enfeebled enemies within 2 tiles; gains extra action + Focus + -20% dmg 2 turns after using a shout. Reaches up to +80% ATK and can self-apply the required debuff. Caveat: only one shout skill in the tree, and unlike Medici there is no TP-return passive, so TP consumption is heavy; alternatively just use Cursed Eye accessory and settle for the 50% tier. At launch, enfeeble-appliers to pair with were only dark-attr Bernstein or G.S (neither top-tier), so reception was lukewarm; by mid-2025, dark-attr healer Deimos and fire-attr assassin Omega, given out free enable the full +80% ATK swings.
  • Unbroken: learns Heavy Slam; +25% ATK unconditionally, -20% dmg taken, additional -40% dmg on combat attacks, heals 50% of ATK at end of turn if debuffed, heals 50% of ATK once/turn when under action-control. Survival-focused but its ATK boost is not small and conditions are not harsh; relatively underrated only because the other classes' ATK boosts are so strong.
  • Skills: Omega Swing. Cry of Threat. Battle Stance. Heavy Slam. Ultimate Storm Sword: AoE 100% electric magic damage within 3 tiles + no-counter debuff; combos with Christian's Satellite Attack (which stuns electrocuted enemies) for an AoE stun.
  • Dedicated weapon greatsword: +ATK/+crit, on combat-attack crit triggers Lightning Strike for electric damage + Vortex Rush/Storm Surge buff (phys pen up, crit taken down). For Shakvari, Storm Surge becomes Vortex Rush (stronger, 2-turn duration, guaranteed lightning; +1 move at 3-star+). Good color match and gives needed phys pen/crit/ATK, but only Heavy Slam among her main skills is a combat-attack and Vortex Rush needs warmup, so the weapon itself is rated weak; Albatini's Imperial Armada is often given instead for its reliable phys pen.
  • Recommended gear: crit-focused is essential (Slasher passive, Heavy Slam, dedicated weapon all reward crits). Armor: Assassin set at Hero grade; Crow/Raven set at Legendary. Accessory: Ancient Hero's Ring; arrowhead has limited use since only Heavy Slam is a combat attack; Hero grade Golden Dice.
  • Evaluation: A slash dealer requiring a choice between AoE (Slasher + Bleed + Omega Swing) and single-target, or defensive (Unbroken + Omega Swing + Battle Stance). But going survival cuts the already-mediocre firepower and cannot match all-rounder Albatini; going single-target loses to Karls. Main skills only scale 120%~140%, which is seen as the problem. Still, passive extra-action-on-kill plus Battle Stance vigor makes trash-clearing strong; in raids the bleed debuff gives solid damage contribution; at 5-awaken+ she can support the whole team with a once-per-game buff+heal. Versatile rather than specialized, with a stable and reliable strength and a dependable floor. In PVP, picking Unbroken means clumsy attacks or status effects actually heal her a lot, making opponents wary. No verbatim S/A/B tier rating is present in this article.

KR Creator Guide

Character review of Shakvari of War by 하막군의게임튜브 · 2026-08-04

Shakvari of War is the first genuinely new character — not a returning face from the source games — and as the god with the highest combat power in the setting, expectations were high. The reviewer says she lives up to them: a jack-of-all-trades who can be built as a bleed dealer, a lane-blocker, or a straight damage-reduction wall. (21:12)

Same caveat as several others: the AI plays her badly, so she's an attack-team unit rather than a defence-team one.

War Fervour builds faster than it looks

She gains a stack of War Fervour before using a skill, before attacking, before being attacked, and after gaining a buff — four separate triggers, so a single action often banks two or three at once.

He demonstrates it: Omega Swing grants one for the skill, one for the attack, and one for the Inertia buff it applies — three stacks from one cast. Then Battle Stance+1 more → adds two more (skill, plus the buff it grants itself). (3:08)

At 8 stacks she applies Authority of War to the entire team: ATK and Max HP up, HP restored, TP restored — permanent and unremovable. Crucially, reaching 8 doesn't consume the stacks, so she keeps the whole stack bonus as well. (5:15)

Stacks drop by 2 if she goes a turn without gaining any, or if she's hit by Action Control. (1:01)

Armored Suits is a counter, not a percentage

There was an argument at announcement that 3 stacks of 30% meant 90% damage reduction. The reviewer tested it: it is 20-30% reduction applied a set number of times, not a stacked percentage. Two stacks means the reduction fires twice, then it's gone. Much more reasonable than it read. (2:37)

Class

Slasher is the bleed build: +15% ATK, +30% Crit Rate on attack, and Slash or Pierce criticals apply Bleed (30% of ATK at end of turn).

The finding he demonstrates: the text implies Bleed lands first and Excessive Bleeding needs a second critical hit later. In practice both fire on the same hit — the crit applies Bleed and immediately counts as a critical against a bleeding target — so an ordinary attack effectively lands at about 130%. (7:16)

Dominator is the control build. Threatening Roar applies Fear-Tremor with the Weaken tag, and her passive scales damage by the target's debuff state: +30% with none, +50% with any debuff, +80% if that debuff is a Weaken. Casting a shout also grants Overwhelming Defence, an extra action and Focus (TP back at end of turn).

He went looking for other Weaken-tagged debuffs and found exactly one: Incapacitate, from Isabel Ripine's Ground Crush+1 more →. He asks viewers to comment if they know of others. (11:25, 12:26)

Unbroken+2 more → is the defensive build: +25% ATK, -20% damage taken, and a further 40% reduction "when making a combat attack" — which he clarifies means counterattack damage she receives is cut, demonstrating 617 versus 912. (13:27)

Gear

Black Crow with Ancient Hero's Ring — the standard Slash-dealer kit. He notes Crit matters on some of her classes and not others, so One Against a Thousand is also fine. (17:32)

Weapon: ★3 and stop

Critical hits on combat attacks call down lightning (the same effect as Stormbringer) and grant Vortex Rush. ★3 adds Movement +1, which is the reason to take it — and there is no reason to go past ★3. Below ★3 he says Stormbringer is about as good. (18:05)

Runes

Chaos, Chaos, Ultimate, Ultimate, Conspiracy, Conspiracy. All-Chaos or Conspiracy in slots 1-2 for Crit are both fine. (17:32)

Manifestation

2 adds Max HP. 4 raises Armored Suits to 30% reduction. 6 takes it to 3 stacks with more Max HP. (19:38)

Artifacts

Warrior's Essence+10 more → is what most players will run — ATK on melee skills.

Chalice of Blood+1 more → is the bleed-dealer option: +50% bleed damage, which he estimates puts Bleed around 45% of ATK, though he flags he hasn't tested the exact interaction. Peak Condition+12 more → is the survivability pick. (20:09)
