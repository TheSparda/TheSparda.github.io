Guide

Note: The following is NamuWiki community opinion (KR server), subjective.

  • Core identity: a high-awakening-essential character who lives and dies by debuffs and can apply "Healing Reversal". To perform, she needs passive-buff stacks; to stack them she must attack debuffed enemies; to do that she must apply debuffs first, so she needs warm-up, yet her survivability is not outstanding. Aim for at least 5-star awakening.
  • Class notes: Base job Ranger; Tier 2 unique class "Dominant"; Tier 3 = Hi-Master / Python / Thorns Queen.
  • Hi-Master: applies "Insight" (ATK/Magic/DEF -25%, +30% crit-taken for 2 turns to 2 random enemies within 3 tiles). Fits her debuff synergy but can only use "sword" type, so it cannot equip the dedicated weapon; lowest priority.
  • Python: teaches "Hwasarangchu"; applies "Weakening" and Mary deals +50% damage to Weakened enemies; steals buffs equal to debuff count. High damage potential, good weapon synergy; recommended for early growth and as an endgame class. Its "Weakening" can supply 80% ATK-up to War's Sharkbari (Dominator class).
  • Thorns Queen: teaches "Erotic Scar"; +30% ATK on attack and applies "Wheel of Pain" (unremovable debuff, random debuff if HP not 100% at turn end); reflects 30% of first hit as fixed physical damage.
  • Dedicated skills: Pyeoninmu (Dominant), Hwasarangchu (Python), Erotic Scar (Thorns Queen). Often either all three form the endgame set, or Erotic Scar (off-tree) is dropped for "Jesikyeon" as damage + self-heal.
  • Pyeoninmu (TP 2): pick Gyeon-pyeoninmu (pull enemy) or Tan-pyeoninmu (advance), the key skill that applies "Healing Reversal"; but multiplier is only 120% and extra damage/effect requires bringing the enemy within 2 tiles.
  • Hwasarangchu (TP 2): 80% + 80% extra-hit (160% total), cannot be counterattacked, range 1-2; her main damage skill; whetstone boosts the extra hit.
  • Erotic Scar (TP 1): deals 1 damage to an ally but gives +1 movement and a one-time guaranteed crit; useful as self-buff for Hwasarangchu burst, but does not apply to AoE and cannot beat Valkyrie's parry.
  • Ultimate "Call Me Queen": front 3x4 AoE, 100%-115% damage, aligns enemies in a line and applies 2-turn "Thorn Bind" (immobilize); doubles power vs a single target. Downside: with a dedicated-skill set she then has no AoE at all.
  • Dedicated weapon "Lepine Bleu": adds "Enthralling Violation" (15-40% AoE within 2 tiles, cooldown 1) after attacking an enemy with a healing-block effect, and a shield (25-50% ATK for 2 turns) if she took no damage last turn; at 4-star it also applies "Weakening" (ATK/Magic -25%). Since sub-tier-hero sabers are weak, obtaining the dedicated weapon is recommended; but it only performs well from 4-star, so it is considered mediocre at 3-star or below.
  • Recommended gear: survival-oriented armor since her position is awkward. Popular sets: Red Wind (Alsion motif) or Emissary (G.S motif; its ATK-up only applies to Hwasarangchu since Pyeoninmu/Call Me Queen are not "combat attacks"). Underworld 2 + Emissary 2 (Kasim motif) is a solid ATK+HP choice. Best accessory (legendary): Cursed Eye (applies debuff in combat, stacks passive fast + aids survival); at hero grade a whetstone helps damage, but note Mary is a debuffer, not a dealer.
  • Overall verdict: a difficult character who needs warm-up but is not tanky. Long seen as niche (mainly countering "Third Great-Granduncle" and Sarah Randgrid, then sidelined). But "Healing Reversal" is so strong that her value keeps rising as the 2025 meta inflates survivability; "just survive and spread debuffs and she does one member's worth of work." Newer main deities added in Q2 2025 (War's Sharkbari, Justice's Diene) grow stronger when her debuffs are present, giving her a niche as a main-deity supporter and increasing her employability.
  • No explicit S/A/B tier letter is stated in the source.

Awakening2025-04-15

Expanded Inner Break awakening

  • Added Inner Break awakening; stages 1/3/5 enhance ultimate 'Call Me Queen'.
  • Ultimate 'Call Me Queen' (max awakening): deals 140% ATK to enemies in a 3x4 frontal area and pulls them into a line, applying Thorn Bind (cannot move) and Healing Reversal (received healing converts to damage equal to 50% of the heal) for 2 turns; double damage if only one enemy is in range.
