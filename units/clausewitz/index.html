Signature Gear

Guide

Community opinion, translate faithfully. No explicit S/A/B letter tier given; described as broken/OP-tier mage.

Overall: A broken-tier mage whose summons are powerful, whose Spell-Power-based damage is excellent, who is tanky, and who can even gain extra actions. Counted as top-tier among all mages (green attribute and Beradin, Kun, Gishne, Diablo, Este Dode, etc.). Strong when run summon-focused, but also pulls his weight as a battle-mage with Ice Missile and Magic Spear.

Passive: Unconditional +20% Spell Power note: passive text lists +10% initial, and at end of character turn picks one of 3 buffs; buff power scales with awakening.

  • Sturdy Frontline: buff on Heavy Armor allies, reduces first damage taken for 2 turns. Useful with heavy-armor tanks; the Mystic Soldiers summoned by Dragon Strike are Heavy Armor, so AI usually picks this after Dragon Strike.
  • Battlefield Flag: buff on allies within 3 tiles; if using ranged skill without moving, cooldown -3 and crit rate up. Quite strong but the "don't move" condition is awkward, so rarely used.
  • Magic Veil: self buff; Spell-Power-scaled shield for 2 turns; while shield holds, chance to be crit and AoE damage taken drop. No special condition, and since Clausewitz is a light-armor mage it patches his low defense; his all-in Spell Power makes the shield very strong. At 5-Awakening cooldown drops to 2 so Magic Veil never gaps, so rushing 5-Awaken is recommended.

Classes (7.1): All three Tier-3 options cap Spell Power at +50%, so ceiling is equal; acquisition and extra effects differ.

  • 1st recommended: Skimmer. Strong signature skills sit on the Tactician-Skimmer tree; the +50% Spell Power condition ("on attack, target adjacent to an ally") is very easy, and kills leave a Mystic Tactics Book granting a free extra action with no cap on repeats. Can solo-sweep enemies. "If you're not using Skimmer, why use Clausewitz."
  • 2nd recommended: Wind Wilder. Learns Genie summon, fitting his summoner nature. +50% via 25% + 25% but conditions are strict, so less recommended despite the TP payback.
  • Archmage: after a "combat attack" if enemy survives, steal 1 TP and +50% Spell Power for 1 turn. Learns Thunderbolt; TP theft applies to basic attacks so TP is plentiful. Doesn't apply to AoE, so poor synergy with Dragon Strike; sometimes run as a battle-mage with Ice Missile + Magic Spear instead.

Signature skills & Ultimate (7.2):

  • Dragon Strike: 3 TP, 3x3 area for 60% Spell Power, summons 3 dark/slash/heavy in a line for 2 turns. Summons have no turn and can't move but create a control zone, ignore movement-hinder and position-control, and attack for 80% attack when an enemy ends action within 8 surrounding tiles; also give adjacent allies cover. His core skill; can push enemies and break formation; a melee enemy attacking the center soldier eats 100% counter plus 80%x3 end-of-action hits, theoretically up to 340% of the soldier's attack. Summons also assist-attack, enabling encirclement. Being Heavy Armor they benefit from Solid Front; AI almost always uses Solid Front after Dragon Strike.
  • Plane Shift: swaps a target enemy within 3 tiles with an ally within 3 tiles and deals 100% Spell Power. Tricky, used situationally (pull enemies, obstacle gimmicks).
  • Genie Summon: summons 1 Genie (dark/magic/medium) within 2 tiles. Specialized in hindering enemy movement: applies movement-down debuff to enemies ending action within 2 tiles, and +30% magic damage to movement-controlled enemies. Can't use the enemy Genie's whirlwind summon, but has line, 3 tiles, 90% Spell Power, 2-turn movement-down. Versatile despite lacking the whirlwind.
  • Ultimate Lightning of Thundermare: pick a spot within 3 tiles, hit enemies within 3 tiles for Spell-Power damage; adjacent enemies take extra 50% Spell Power lightning and get counter-blocking Electrocute. His skills have high TP cost / cooldowns / fixed uses, so gaps let TP build; in long fights it sees surprising use.

Recommended gear (7.3):

  • Weapon: dedicated. Bonus stat is magic penetration (great fit). Weapon skill grants Dynamic Magic at turn start (stacking Spell Power, stacks drop on skill-kill); Clausewitz-only effect disperses 80% of combat damage taken to summons within 2 tiles at 6-star, and he can summon up to 4, making him extremely tanky.
  • Armor: Chancellor set (Beradin-motif) recommended, all-in Spell Power plus penetration.
  • Accessory: Necronomicon most recommended; at full awaken raises summon level by 5 and buffs Spell Power 10%.

Balance history: Buffed on 2026-04-21; in PvP, combos with Altair, Beramode of Conspiracy, Esmeralda, full-build Karim/Silver, etc. let a duplicated "Jebi" keep pulling ultra-stat Genies for "insane damage," so a temporary measure was applied via the emergency maintenance on 2026-06-26.

Awakening/Manifestation progression: Two tracks per stage: manifestation and Inner Cube. - Stage 1: Ultimate Enhancement 1 / Stat Enhancement 1 - Stage 2: Unique Passive Enhancement 1 / Stat Enhancement 2 - Stage 3: Ultimate Enhancement 2 / Artifact Acquired - Stage 4: Unique Passive Enhancement 2 / Artifact Enhancement 1 - Stage 5: Ultimate Enhancement 3 / Artifact Enhancement 2 - Stage 6: Unique Passive Enhancement 3 / Artifact Enhancement 3 Related passive scaling: Initial = Spell Power +10%; Max Awakening = +25%; Max Manifestation = +50% (cooldown drops 3 to 2). Ultimate: Initial 100% > Max Awakening 115% > Max Manifestation 140% Spell Power lightning damage.

Balance2026-04-21

Full rework of unique passive, ultimate, class, exclusive weapon, active skills, and artifact

  • Unique passive Genius Strategist reworked; all three summoned skills (Hardened Front, Battle Flag, Magic Veil) now cannot be dispelled.
  • Awakening 3-6: cooldown standardized to 3 (was 3/3/2/2). Hardened Front now also heals 5% Max HP once per turn. Battle Flag now grants Crit Rate and Crit Damage +10/15/20/25% (was Crit Rate only 5/15/20/30%).
  • Manifest 2/4: M-ATK now +30/40% and adds Max HP +10/20% (was flat M-ATK +30/40). Cooldown standardized to 2. Hardened Front 5/6 heals 10% Max HP/turn. Battle Flag 5/6 grants Crit Rate and Crit Damage +30/35% (was Crit Rate only 40/45%). Magic Veil 5/6 damage reduction raised to 55/60% (was 50/50%). Effects now grant immunity instead of TP removal.
  • Manifest 6: M-ATK now +50% and adds Max HP +30% (was flat M-ATK +50). Hardened Front 7 heals 10% Max HP/turn. Magic Veil 7 now grants TP-removal immunity.
  • Ultimate, class passive, exclusive weapon, active skills, and artifacts also adjusted.

Awakening2025-02-04

Expanded Inner Break awakening

  • Added Inner Break awakening. Stages 1/3/5 enhance ultimate 'Thundermare's Lightning Strike': at max, target a tile and deal 140% M-ATK lightning damage to enemies within 3 tiles; adjacent enemies take an extra 50% M-ATK, and all hit enemies are shocked for 1 turn.
  • Stages 2/4/6 enhance unique passive 'Genius Strategist': at max, M-ATK +50%; at turn end choose one of Solid Front 7, Battle Flag 7, or Magic Veil 7 (Cooldown 2 turns), each granting an immunity (movement-block / skill-control / TP-removal).
  • Solid Front 7: grants heavy-armor allies a 2-turn effect reducing the first hit taken in combat by 70%.
  • Battle Flag 7: allies within 3 tiles get a 2-turn effect; while stationary, ranged skills gain -3 Cooldown and +50% Crit Rate.
  • Magic Veil 7: gain a 150% M-ATK shield for 2 turns; while it holds, Crit Rate taken and AoE damage taken are reduced 50%.
  • Stage 3 instantly equips the artifact; stages 4/5/6 upgrade artifacts (e.g. 'Simple Protection Charm' game-start shield 40% to 100% M-ATK).
