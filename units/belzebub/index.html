KR Creator Guide

Character review of Belzebub by 하막군의게임튜브 · 2026-08-03

Belzebub launched to a rough reception — the reviewer says players were only half-joking that he felt like a Hero rather than a Legendary. A run of bug fixes and some settled build knowledge pulled him back up. He is not overpowered, but he is genuinely usable, and the reviewer is careful about how he frames the one obvious complaint: Belzebub's damage is low, but he is a tank, so low damage isn't really a fault. (28:21)

Build him around Max HP

Everything in the kit routes through his health pool. Erebos Guard's Gatekeeper of Ruin converts 5% of Max HP into ATK, and Bio-Transmutation restores 10% of Max HP plus 25% of DEF whenever he drops below 90%. That restore is not healing, so Transmuted Body's -90% healing received doesn't touch it, and it fires a second time below 35% HP. The practical result the reviewer keeps coming back to: if Belzebub isn't killed outright, he tends to stabilise above half health on his own. (4:10)

Ether-Force Modification only exists at 6★

The Ether-Force Modification line on his unique passive does not appear at all below 6★. Of the two versions, Zeta is the one worth having — base stats +30% against Mu's flatter bump, plus Control Zone ignore and movement-debuff immunity.

It will not stack with Altair's Transcendence effect. Field both and only one fires; the reviewer's own testing put the upper-left unit first in the ordering, whichever of the two that happens to be. (7:42)

Class: Erebos Guard for PvP, Zeros Warden for raids

Erebos Guard is the default and the reviewer says it is hard to justify anything else in PvP — DEF/RES, damage reduction, ATK off Max HP, and Herald of Ruin applying Ruin Erosion on attack.

Zeros Warden is the raid class. Infinite Adaptation stacks quickly while you keep swinging, and at 15 stacks it turns into extra turns rather than extra actions, dragging cooldowns down and TP up with every one. His caveat is the usual one for anything that needs warming up: by the time it comes online in PvP, the fight is over. (8:13)

Void Deployment is the TP engine

Void Deployment strips two debuffs off allies within 3 tiles, strips two buffs off enemies, grants an extra action, and can be cast while Silenced. Chain it with the exclusive weapon's TP return and a TP accessory and the reviewer opens the game at full TP, walks into the enemy's face and fires the ultimate on turn one. (11:49)

Psychic Burst placement is a coin flip

Under Gatekeeper of Ruin, Demon-Slaying Chain Kill Slash triggers Psychic Burst before the attack, summoning a thought-form in a tile adjacent to the target. Adjacent means any free neighbouring tile, so whether you get a Flanking attack out of it is luck. The summon is worth having regardless: it takes 15% of Belzebub's incoming damage, and when it dies it detonates for 80% of ATK with an 80% chance of Fear. (14:23)

Gear

The reviewer runs Warrior of Restraint reforged toward HP, on the same Max HP logic as everything else. Worth flagging that this is his personal preference and differs from gmap's own recommendation, which ranks Judge of Condemnation first for the counterattack synergy. (22:38)

For the accessory, take Crystal Orb of Insight+1 more → if you want the turn-one ultimate — its Crit Rate is what gets you to the 75% breakpoint the weapon needs. Otherwise Void Core is the general pick: Max HP, Movement +1, and Void Barrier plus Null Field make him markedly harder to shift, with the reduction converting into ATK on his own turn. Killing Will looks like it should fit this playstyle and doesn't — the reviewer says skip it. (26:49)

Dark Blinger: ★2 is mandatory, then stop or go ★5

★1 is described as no better than nothing. ★2 adds Infinite Output — on any skill cast, a chance equal to Crit Rate to gain 1 TP, no cooldown, attack skills included — and that is what makes the turn-one ultimate work at all. ★5 adds Counter Range +1. Everything in between is filler. Take ★2, or ★5 if you are committing. (23:40)

One warning on the weapon's headline stat: a lot of players stacked Counter Damage because it converts into ATK, and the reviewer reports it applying strangely in practice — possibly a bug — and rates it well below what the numbers suggest. (23:09)

Runes

Infinite Output's TP return rolls off Crit Rate, so Crit comes first; with artifacts he lands around 75% at full investment. Sun runes for Crit, Ultimate runes for DEF, HP in whatever is left. (25:12)
