KR Creator Guide

Character review of Hellkite by 하막군의게임튜브 · 2026-08-31

Hellkite is a well-made, on-concept unit: very tanky, with damage that comes almost entirely from stacked fire damage-over-time rather than from any single big hit. The reviewer likes her, with two warnings — she turns to paper the moment Dragon Scale breaks, and he thinks Slash units historically have a short shelf life, with Xavier already announced as the next Ancient Dragon. (31:52)

He didn't pull her, and says so up front: this review is built from other players' testing rather than his own account. (1:01)

Heal Block immunity blanks Anemone

Way of the Annihilating Flame carries Heal Block, Silence and Skill Control immunity, so Anemone's Heal Block simply doesn't land on her. She also brings 40-50% fixed-damage resistance, which matters in the mirror because most of her own damage is fixed. (1:31)

Dragon's Blessing buffs the whole team by fire-skill count

It counts how many fire skills the recipient has equipped, ultimate included, and grants a tier accordingly — 10/20/30% base stats at full manifestation. It is an Authority, so it will not stack with other Authority effects.

At the top tier it also ignores extra-action denial, which the reviewer calls out approvingly given how many recent units block extra actions. (2:33)

Dragon Scale is a revive at 20% HP — and the crash after is real

It is not a death-prevention effect, which means she can carry a separate revive on top and effectively come back twice.

The cost is severe. Once Broken Scale replaces it, her -50/70% skill damage taken flips to +10/20% taken, and healing received goes from -60% to +20%. Before the break she is extremely hard to kill; after it, she folds. (3:37)

Dragon's Wrath stacks from anyone's fire skill

Taking damage, or any unit — ally or enemy — using a fire skill, gives her a stack, plus one per enemy hit when she attacks.

At 3 and 6 stacks: Movement +1 and Crit Rate +30% each. At 10, another +30%. That is Movement +2 and Crit +90%, so she crits essentially always, and the reviewer reports that reaching 10 isn't hard. (6:10)

The Blaze Aura wording trips people up

It reads as one clause but is two, and he spends a while on it. Enemies within 2 tiles take DEF-ignoring fire damage when they end their action; separately, allies within 2 tiles get an extra turn on a kill. At Blaze Aura 3 those allies also gain TP-removal immunity and Control Zone ignore. (7:43)

Class: Ignis Warden by default, Ash Lord for raids

Ignis Warden gives -35% damage taken, ATK scaling off HP lost, 20% of fire damage dealt returned as HP, and a Magma Zone under her whenever she is attacked.

Ash Lord trades that for +40% ATK, area and fire-crit damage, and two auras that each add 50% to fire damage-over-time — one on allies dealing it, one on enemies receiving it, which multiply out to roughly double. Enormous in raids, much frailer in PvP. (11:18)

Magma Zones do the heavy lifting

Enemies entering take DEF-ignoring fire damage and a melt debuff that cuts Fire RES and adds RES-ignoring fire magic damage per stack at end of turn, up to 5 stacks. Hellkite entering heals and gains a barrier reflecting 30% of damage taken as fixed damage.

The subtlety: the zone spawning under her counts as her entering it, so being attacked heals her — but only on the 2-turn cooldown. (12:21)

Physical Penetration is nearly a dead stat on her

Her passive grants 40% Physical Penetration and the weapon adds more, but almost everything she does is DEF-ignoring, RES-ignoring or fixed damage. Blaze Sword is one of very few skills penetration actually applies to. (13:24)

Pyrexia is Action Control, not Stun

The important detail, and the reason she gets through defences that should stop her. Pyrexia is not tagged Stun, Fear or Banish — it is plain Action Control, so Stun resistance and Fear resistance do nothing.

He names the only counters he knows: Dragon Slayer Zephyr's Ring of Purification+2 more →, de Medici's shout, and a mage shield artifact whose expiry grants Action Control resistance. (20:01)

Gear

Because penetration barely applies, there is no reason to run One Against a Thousand or Envoy of the Storm — an HP set or borrowed Outer One pieces are fine. Black Banner as the accessory.

On skills, only three fire skills are needed (the ultimate counts), so one of the three can be dropped for a movement skill. (23:39)

Weapon ★3 is the breakpoint, and the weapon is mandatory

★3 grants Fire Claw, which converts her basic attack and counter into 130% fire physical damage and applies the melt debuff on hit — much the cheapest way to hold melt at 5 stacks. ★5 adds Preemptive Attack; ★6 is numbers only.

The weapon's own turn-start self-damage is a bonus rather than a cost, since it keeps her below 100% HP and so keeps the Crit Rate rider live. (26:14)

Artifact: Heat Haze of Dragon Blood

Gladiator's Fighting Spirit+1 more → and Crest of Retribution are both dismissed.

Heat Haze of Dragon Blood gives +25% damage on fire attacks and Crimson Haze (+30% ATK) after one, upgrading to Shimmering Heat Wind on a second consecutive fire attack — which refunds TP on every non-ultimate fire skill. Since all her skills are fire, that is every turn. Three fire attacks in a row extends it to the whole team. (27:46)

That team-wide version is hard to use, because few allies cast fire skills. Theta is the exception he names: the plasma skill counts as fire, which makes them an obvious pairing. (30:51)

Investment

Four manifestations minimum. He adds, with some resignation, that Slash units don't really have a budget build — full manifestation or don't bother. The weapon can stop at ★3. (31:52)
