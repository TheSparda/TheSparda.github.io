Guide

Overall verdict, verbatim label "S/A/B tier": (no explicit letter tier stated). Summary: "A pretty piece of trash that was barely saved by an artifact."

Classes (7.1): Tier 3 options are Winter Sword, area, Arc Knight, all-round offense/defense, Frost Knight, ice + defense. Arc Knight's passive: +40% ATK before skill use and debuffs adjacent enemies (ATK/spell power/crit reduction + passive-nullify), so best-balanced; considered the safe, strongest pure-damage brainless pick. Winter Sword passive applies only to area skills; Frost Knight only to ice skills, so both have generality flaws. Frost Knight learns core skill Snow Tale Chaos Strike, gains "Frost Ice Wall" buff (physical dmg -40%, ice resist +40% after casting; deals 30% ATK ice dmg to enemies within 8 tiles and applies Chill debuff on being hit once), praised for good tanking. Winter Sword learns Ice Strike Flash and can stack Chill via the Double Snowflake technique - strong in that case, but lowest priority since it needs Snow Tale Chaos Strike.

Signature/Ultimate (7.2): Snow Tale Chaos Strike is the core skill - move within 3 tiles then hit 8 surrounding tiles, applying Ice Bind; 180% multiplier but dmg is split among those hit, so with only 1 target the full 180% lands (very strong). Core to the Double Snowflake technique. Ice Strike Flash: move to an empty tile 4 tiles away in a straight line, 70% ATK dmg along the path; low multiplier, acts as a supporting debuff skill - use combined with ice skills, not alone. Ultimate Pandragon royal signature move, identical to Crow: move within 3 tiles, AoE dmg to enemies within 3 tiles, applies Cold Slow; synergizes with ice-skill siblings (Lacid, Iyolin), himself, and also Veradin.

Recommended gear (7.3): Exclusive weapon has ATK% option, +dmg on ice skills, and TP payback of +1 TP on ice skill cast. Key are the awakening-added exclusive effects: 3-star adds extra attack/skill after ice skills; 6-star adds "Ice Crystal" buff. Enables the "Double Snowflake" technique: T1 fill 1 TP via Warrant Barrier, T2 at 5 TP cast Snow Tale Chaos Shadow Slash then use payback 1 TP for Snow Tale Chaos Strike; especially strong on Winter Sword. Heavily depends on high-star weapon skills. Armor: Crow set mainly recommended; based on Nangcheon for penetration; Haidellune set is best if shared - Haidellune + Frost Throne + Saint King Lacid's Pandragon faction buff yields huge dmg on Double Snowflake. Accessories: Golden Dice or Ancient Hero's Ring. Artifact: Frost Throne is essential.

Overall (8): Very popular fandom character; big attention from the pre-Black-Prince PV. But awkward design: an early-burst AoE character that only performs after killing 1 enemy, and if it fails to kill in time gets the Price of Arrogance debuff; needs full exclusive-weapon awakening to work. Went to the warehouse just 2 weeks after release when Inner Break was introduced with Black Prince. Fans waited for Ice's Inner Break, but it did not fix Price of Arrogance, and being an AoE character whose skills lack the Combat tag yet oddly having "+40% physical penetration in combat," plus an artifact structure that could not do Double Snowflake, blew up the community lounge. Developer stayed silent; finally in the 3rd week of Sept 2025 the new artifact Frost Throne was added, raising performance to usable/active-roster level. Combined with Saint King Lacid's Pandragon faction buff (base stats up to +30% and movement +1), it can finally function. Note: still cannot run raids due to Price of Arrogance, and Double Snowflake needs 1 turn of warm-up. For rush: T1 throw into enemy lines with Ice Strike Flash + Snow Tale Chaos Strike then combo with Lacid's Xavier Breath or ice magic; Frost Knight is better here for survivability, while Double Snowflake favors Winter Sword or Arknight. Full weapon awakening requirement means low raising priority unless you love Ice. (Community aside: "newer characters are better" is the trap.)

Awakening/Manifestation progression: Track: Manifestation / Inner Cube. - Stage 1: Ultimate enhance 1; Stat enhance 1. - Stage 2: Unique passive enhance 1; Stat enhance 2. - Stage 3: Ultimate enhance 2; Artifact acquired. - Stage 4: Unique passive enhance 2; Artifact enhance 1. - Stage 5: Ultimate enhance 3; Artifact enhance 2. - Stage 6: Unique passive enhance 3; Artifact enhance 3. Reference for what each level enhances (from passive/ult sections): - Passive Sword of Conviction: initial: at 100% HP, +15% ATK on area attacks; start with Sword of Conviction 1. Max Awakening: at 100% HP, +30% ATK on area attacks; start Sword of Conviction 4. Max Manifestation: Max HP +30%; at 70% HP, +40% ATK on area attacks and +40% physical penetration in combat; start Sword of Conviction 7. - Ultimate "Snow Tale Chaos Shadow Slash": First Acquisition: 110% ATK dmg, 20% chance to apply Cold Slow for 2 turns. Max Awakening: 125% ATK dmg, 35% chance. Max Manifestation: 140% ATK dmg, 100% chance.
