Guide

The following is subjective NamuWiki community opinion (KR server).

Performance: Base class is Sister; Tier 3 classes are Saint/Minister/Hyginist; ultimate is Abydos Ascension. Unique passive "Saint of Mercy" increases spell power based on number of other allies within 3 tiles; on death, once per battle, gives a 2-turn action-locked invincible state then revives at some HP. Higher awakening raises the spell-power gain, and from 5-star adds dispelling 1 debuff on revive.

Among Tier 3, Minister is the top priority. Its passive removes 1 debuff from the lowest-HP-ratio ally, averages their HP with Esmeralda's, then heals 3040% (a so-called "Wi-Fi heal"). Because it targets "lowest HP ratio ally" there is no range limit and no TP cost. It also learns "Second Wind" (heal 1 ally + grant re-action), so it is a flawless endgame class. Reaching Minister level 23 essentially finishes her build.

Hyginist focuses on spell-power amplification and debuff removal. Its passive debuff-defense buff only blocks damage-over-time and dark-attribute debuffs, so it is somewhat awkward. The up-to-40% spell-power amp is good, but being unable to block or cleanse silence/stun is critically limiting, making it nearly a Diablo-sniping pick. However the AoE HP-recovery + debuff-removal skill Sterilize learned here is excellent, so Hyginist ranks 2nd. The endgame build is considered: go Minister first, then use Opal to pull Sterilize from Hyginist, running Recovery/Sterilize/Second Wind.

Saint is a shared class specialized in recovery and debuff removal, but its Cure Light lags behind other classes, so it tends to be deprioritized.

Also notable is Recovery (learned from Healer): same heal amount as Heal but with debuff removal attached, making it a full upgrade of "Heal." Note its cooldown is a longer 2.

Signature skills: Second Wind (from Minister) and Sterilize (from Hyginist).

  • Second Wind: TP cost 2. Targets 1 ally within 2 tiles, heals 80% of spell power, and if the target has ended its turn, grants an extra action. Cooldown 3. This re-action-granting skill is her core; before her, ally re-action buffs were basically only Vigor Shout (extra attack/skill chance), so Second Wind's status is very high. This is why she is considered irreplaceable.
  • Sterilize: TP cost 2. Aim a direction; front 3x4 area deals 70% spell-power Radiance damage to enemies, while allies and herself get 70% spell-power heal and 1 debuff removed. Cooldown 3. Excellent AoE that also heals/cleanses Esmeralda herself even though she is outside the area.

Ultimate Abydos Ascension: restores 1 TP to all allies and grants a spell-power-scaling shield for 2 turns. It costs 5 TP yet the TP restore is chance-based, which is slightly disappointing. Still very useful: it shields all allies with no range limit, and while the shield holds gives +15% damage dealt / -15% damage taken.

Dedicated weapon and recommended gear: Weapon is Ribera Pretoria. It shaves 1% HP off adjacent allies in exchange for a 1-turn spell-power-scaling barrier, and grants the "Emergency Response" buff (heal-amount up + heal-skill range up) for 2 turns when an ally is below a certain HP. Excellent because it can shield right at start for the cost of 1% HP, preventing allies from being bursted down; a cross-formation with Esmeralda at center shields the whole team at start. Extra stat is spell power +10%, which is ideal. From 5-star the buff's HP condition disappears and it becomes a permanent passive, so high awakening is worth considering. The 1% HP loss is useful for triggering skills conditioned on being not at 100% HP; conversely, keep full-HP-conditioned characters non-adjacent.

Armor: sets aiding survival and skill use are recommended. Generally the Cardinal set (Van Dyke Cardinal outfit motif, aiming for TP recovery) is 1st priority, and the Pope set (Rubens Heisenberg Pope outfit motif, aiming for cooldown reduction) roughly 2nd. Since options include revival and most performance is spell-power dependent, focusing on spell power is recommended. As a variant, all-in spell power via Chancellor 2 (Beradin outfit motif) + Dark 2 (hero-grade outfit) combo exists.

Accessories: Stopwatch (Legendary, cooldown-reduction option), the healer staple Echo Gem (Legendary), and Chalice of Devotion (Hero) are recommended. Since Recovery, Second Wind, and Sterilize all have long cooldowns, Stopwatch's cooldown reduction helps considerably.

Evaluation: A perfect upgrade over Rina, the previous top healer; as of early 2025 considered a top-tier healer. Having an easy-to-use re-action skill already wins; even at "business-card" (no dupes) stage performance is decent, and enhancement efficiency is good, so at full-dupe/full-enhance no recovery unit could replace Esmeralda even into early 2025. Even people who used fully-invested Rina were stunned by Esmeralda's performance. Despite huge enhancement costs, she is considered a Top 3 "human-rights character", the kind you must full-dupe/full-enhance first at any cost if you keep playing this game.

No tier letter (S/A/B) rating is given in the text; wording used is "top-tier healer," "upgrade," and "Top 3 human-rights character."
