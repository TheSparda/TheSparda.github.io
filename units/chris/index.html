Signature Gear

Guide

This community write-up predates the 2026-03-24 (KR) rework of Whisper of Fate, Swan Song and the Gunblader / Requiem class passives, and still quotes the old numbers for them. The sections above carry the reworked text; the KR Creator Guide further down the page covers the reworked build.

Overview: Base class is Gunner; Tier 3 classes are Ace / Spitfire / Requiem; ultimate is Swansong. The unique passive "Whisper of Fate": when attacking an enemy not at 100% HP, Crit Rate +20% and take 40% less counter damage; landing a crit grants the unremovable 1-turn "Whisper of Fate" buff. Whisper of Fate: when an enemy ends its turn within Chris's range and Chris has ammo, deal 35~50% ATK damage and gain 1 TP at 40%~100% chance. This makes Chris an active turret; because Gunners have wide attack range, auto-damaging enemies that end turns inside it is a big merit. Below 5th awakening the TP-gain chance gambling is heavy, but gaining TP just from enemies ending turns in range is a strong bonus effect.

Classes: Tier 3 options: Ace (crit-focused), Spitfire (gains ATK and crit buffs while out of ammo), Requiem (counters melee attacks, gains extra movement if ammo remains). All classes reload Magic Rounds, +25% ATK bonus magic damage to max when ammo is 0 at the start of the ally turn.

  • Ace: learns Bullseye; max ammo 3; passive gives +30% crit on ammo-consuming attacks. Since Chris has almost no ammo-free actions, the crit boost applies nearly always; also learns two strong damage skills (Rapid Fire and Bullseye), so it is recommended as 1st-priority build and as an endgame class.
  • Spitfire: learns exclusive skill "Barrage"; max ammo 4; passive gives ATK and Crit +20% when ammo is 0 at turn start. Good firepower increase but the hardest condition, so not very recommended; Barrage is a good skill worth securing.
  • Requiem: learns "Deadly Scorch"; uses the Gun Blader-Requiem exclusive tree. Gains pre-emptive attack, counters adjacent melee attacks with sword for 40% damage reduction, and gains extra movement at turn end if ammo remains. Great mobility with good ammo management and improved survivability, and learns two useful exclusive skills; but lacks damage-boost or crit buffs so it is weakest for damage and its passive is hard to trigger, so usually recommended as 2nd-priority build.

Key skills:

  • AP Rounds Reload: TP 1. Empties remaining ammo then loads AP Rounds to max. Cooldown 3. AP Rounds: +25% ATK bonus physical damage and remove 1 TP. Buffs all gun attacks by +25% damage; multi-shot skills that trigger ammo effects at once are greatly strengthened.
  • Bullseye: TP 3, 3 ammo. 130% ATK damage, triggers all consumed ammo effects; on kill, empty remaining ammo and reload Magic Rounds to max. Cooldown 3. Gunner's main damage skill; after AP Rounds Reload, Bullseye becomes a fearsome 205% ATK multiplier.
  • Blade and Gun: TP 2, consume ammo if any. 110% ATK to adjacent enemy, strongly knock back and apply "Shock Stun" for 3 turns; if distance opened, fire gun for +50% ATK. 160% multiplier single-target; usable as a stun skill whenever crit lands. Wants to pair with Requiem's 40% adjacent damage reduction; but Chris is squishy so may be used as an escape skill.
  • Barrage: TP 3, all ammo. Directional, 80% ATK to enemies in range, push 1 tile each; targets limited to ammo count. Gunner's main AoE; with magazine full of Magic/AP Rounds the multiplier rises to 105%.
  • Deadly Scorch: TP 3, all ammo. Move to a chosen tile within 3 tiles, then 60% ATK to enemies within 2 tiles and load 1 Magic Round; double damage to enemies at 50% HP or below. Cooldown 3. Confirm-kill AoE with movement; multiplier low but can reach 85% with Magic/AP buffs; use to finish enemies whose HP was chipped by the passive's enemy-turn attack.
  • Swansong (ultimate): targets 1 unit within 13 tiles, AoE 100130% ATK centered on target; does not consume ammo. Cross-shape area (8 surrounding tiles plus 6 in each of front/back/left/right), like Cheonjipayeolmu. Simple pure-damage AoE with no utility; the animation shows Chris's subordinate unit delivering crossfire, explaining the no-ammo cost and cross shape.

Recommended gear: Exclusive weapon Sturmgehilter; bonus stat ATK increase; +12 ammo at stage start; if ammo full at turn start, +1525% crit damage for 1 turn (3-turn cooldown); after a combat attack that crits, applies 3-turn "Shock Stun" debuff (no counter, stuns at 2 stacks). Upgrades Blade and Gun into a stun skill; crit damage option is attractive for crit-focused Chris, but otherwise mediocre so not deemed essential. Armor: stack crit to enable the passive. Hero grade: Assassin set; Legendary: Crow set (motif of the character Crow); stack crit rate + crit damage, no room for survivability. Accessories: since crit is so important, Golden Dice (extra chance roll) or Balance Blade (trades damage for crit) are recommended.

Evaluation: An active turret that chips HP on the enemy turn and confirm-kills on the ally turn. As Genesis War Mobile's first-ever Gunner class character, operation is somewhat complex and a bit tricky, but ultimately if you load AP/Magic Rounds and shoot while securing crit to act as an "active defense system" turret on the enemy turn, it does its job. The game has very few ranged dealers (only Ariana and Chris in Green attribute), so deploy for Blue-attribute sniping.

No S/A/B tier rating stated in the source.

KR Creator Guide

Character review of Chris (filmed before his rework) by 하막군의게임튜브 · 2026-08-04

The reviewer doesn't normally cover older characters, and made an exception here because Chris was reworked and enough people asked. Everything below describes the reworked kit at full awakening, which is also how he frames it — an older character below full awakening, he says, is essentially unusable, so there's no point describing anything else. He got his own copy to 5★ and stopped. (0:01, 0:31, 1:01)

Whisper of Fate, and why crit resistance is the stat

At full awakening the passive gives +40% Crit Rate, -40% critical hit chance received, and +30% Max HP, and landing a critical hit grants Whisper of Fate. He is emphatic that the middle number is the important one — Crit Rate matters, but crit-chance-taken matters more.

Whisper of Fate shoots anything that ends its turn inside his range: 80% of ATK plus a 30% defence-ignoring bullet, so about 110% in practice, and it needs ammunition to fire. Its "range +1" is added on top of his own, giving five tiles rather than four. It also carries two riders:

  • Sharp Strike — after he attacks anything, an adjacent enemy takes 100% Slash damage and Bleed. Killing with it grants Vigor of Fate, an extra attack or skill — but carried into the next turn, not the current one.
  • Weak Point Defense — a chance to take 30% less damage when attacked, scaling somewhere between 30% and 80% with the attacker's Crit Rate and his own crit-chance reduction. There's no published formula; he estimates around 80% once the reduction is fully stacked.

(1:33, 2:04, 2:35, 3:05, 3:35, 4:07)

Class: Requiem, and nothing else

His words: don't even look at the others. Requiem gives Preemptive Strike, lets him counter an adjacent combat attack with the sword, +50% ATK when attacking, and -50% damage taken when attacked — that second clause on any attack, not just combat ones.

Both numbers decay with distance: -5% per tile beyond the first, to a maximum of -15%. So Chris is not a character you play at range; three tiles out he has already lost the whole bonus. Get adjacent.

It also grants an evasive move after attacking — a teleport anywhere within 3 tiles, terrain ignored. Attack, then hop next to someone, and Sharp Strike adds its 100% on top. Landing adjacent also grants Swift Evasion, carrying another 30% crit-chance reduction, which is what makes diving into the enemy line safe rather than suicidal. (4:39, 5:09, 5:39, 6:09, 6:39)

Gear

Chris does not hit hard. Outside Requiem's +50% there is almost no attack scaling in the kit, just 30% on an artifact. He looked at building physical penetration instead — it can be pushed to 100% — and decided survivability paid better, so it comes down to taste.

His own build is One Against a Thousand with Envoy of the Storm, which covers about 30% penetration between them. For the accessory he picks Black Banner: penetration, crit-chance reduction, attack, and damage reduction after being hit — in his view the best accessory Chris has right now. If you'd rather chase Crit Rate, Balanced Blade Necklace is the alternative. (8:12, 8:42, 9:13, 9:44)

The weapon needs ★3

Sturmgewehr raises maximum ammunition, and the part that matters is the stacking debuff: at two stacks it deals 40% resistance-ignoring damage and returns TP to him. The TP clause only exists from ★3, so ★3 is the floor; past that it's only numbers.

Reaching two stacks means hitting the same enemy twice, which is exactly why he runs Double Down+5 more → — a 2 TP skill that fires twice, refunds the TP through the weapon and ends up costing what a 1 TP skill would. Bullseye+3 more → does the same job by spending three rounds. (9:44, 10:15, 10:46)

There's a second pattern he describes: open with Rain of Fire to smear the weapon debuff across a group, then let Whisper of Fate shoot each of them as they come to you, refunding TP with every hit. That only works if the magazine refills mid-turn, which needs the Advanced Tactical Magazine. (11:17, 11:48)

Artifacts

Overload Loader+4 more → and Initial AP Magazine he waves off. The Advanced Tactical Magazine he takes almost entirely for its 40% physical penetration — he's dismissive of the mid-turn reload and the heal-on-empty as marginal. With 30% from gear, 20% from Medici's Zephyr Falcon buff and 40% here, penetration lands around 90% and the runes finish it.

Trigger of Freedom is his main pick and the one he rates highest: attack up, another 30% crit-chance reduction, and — the good part — taking a combat attack clears one debuff and heals him. Get stunned, and the stun comes straight back off. With two or more debuffs you don't choose which goes, but as a survival tool he calls it the strongest thing he has. (11:48, 12:18, 12:50, 13:20, 13:52)

Runes

He hasn't slotted his yet. His reasoning: crit-chance-taken is already at 120%, so don't spend runes there, and critical-damage-taken reduction is pointless when nothing crits him anyway. That frees slots 1 and 2 for attack, penetration or Crit Rate to taste, with Sun and Moon in the survivability slots.

His actual advice is that the runes follow the plan — turret with high Crit Rate to keep Whisper of Fate live, or penetration to punch through tanks, or pure bulk. (14:24, 14:56, 15:27, 15:57)

The arithmetic, and the verdict

He closes by adding it all up, assuming the Zephyr Falcon buff is running — and he is clear that without Medici, Chris is hard to use at all.

  • Health: +30% passive and +20% from Zephyr Falcon, so 1.5× the number on the roster screen.
  • Crit Rate: 40% from the passive alone, which he considers enough.
  • Crit chance taken: 40% passive, 30% class on approach, 10% boot reforge, 30% Trigger of Freedom, 10% Black Banner — 120% total. Against an attacker with over 40% Crit Rate that still leaves the mitigation roll around 80%.
  • Damage taken: a flat -50% from Requiem, plus the 30% roll on top, for up to -80% — comparable to a barrier field, except it never runs out.
  • Attack: 50% class and 30% artifact, about +80%, which by current standards he calls average.
  • Penetration: about 50% from gear and the faction buff, up to 90–100% if you build for it.

One correction he makes: the Zephyr Falcon +1 skill range and Whisper of Fate's range bonus are separate things and don't add up. The passive shot goes five tiles either way.

So: not a character who deletes anything, but one that chips constantly — shoot, teleport in, Slash, push with Blade and Gun, shoot again — while being very hard to kill. He describes taking hits from Cyrano Bernstein and surviving. If you're picking him up on a one-plus-one, he thinks it's worth doing now, with an eye on a Zephyr Falcon deck once a fourth deck slot arrives. (16:27, 16:59, 17:31, 18:01, 18:33, 19:04, 19:36, 20:08, 20:38, 21:09)

  • Stages 1/3/5 enhance ultimate 'Swan Song': at max, deals 150% ATK damage in an area around a target; consumes no ammo.
  • Stages 2/4/6 enhance unique passive 'Whisper of Fate': at max, when attacking an enemy below 100% HP, Crit Rate +50% and take 60% less counter damage; when attacked by an enemy below 100% HP, chance to be crit −50%. After landing a crit, gain Whisper of Fate 7 for 1 turn.
  • Whisper of Fate 7 (1 turn, cannot be dispelled): Crit Rate +30%, effect range +2; if an enemy ends its turn in range and Chris has ammo, fire for 80% ATK damage and gain 1 TP.
  • Artifact 'Overcharger': first awakening Physical Penetration +15% and max ammo +1; at max, Physical Penetration +60% and max ammo +1.
  • Artifact 'Initial Armor-Piercing Magazine': start the game loaded with Armor-Piercing Rounds.
