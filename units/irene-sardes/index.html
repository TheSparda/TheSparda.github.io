Guide (Performance / Evaluation)

The following is NamuWiki community assessment (KR server), subjective community opinion.

Performance: Among Legendary characters, she is one of only three who possess the "11-shaped" class tree. Like Morgaina, she has an unusual build that doubles as healer and mage, so her Tier 3 classes are two: Cavalist and Bloodmage. Switching to the mage-side class also changes the class icon. Her unique passive is "Woman Behind the Veil": when granting a beneficial effect to an ally via skill, she gives the target a shield equal to 25-40% of Spell Power for 2 turns; also, dealing damage to an enemy grants herself a shield equal to 25-50% of Spell Power for 2 turns. From 5th awakening, if she holds a shield, in combat her Defense and Resistance increase by 20% of Spell Power. This is the core passive of Irene the shield supporter. She has no ultimate. Her signature weapon is Chromatic Rod, a Legendary Rod; its bonus stat is healing. When she casts a spell on an ally, it grants a "Recovery" buff that heals based on the caster's Spell Power at turn start. When Irene uses it, the shield she grants allies is strengthened by an additional 20%. A very fitting weapon for Irene, who is both a healer and shield supporter; since Irene can also use Warrant Barrier, synergy is even better.

Evaluation: A utility-rich healer plus shield supporter: she can grant AoE shields and healing while also targeting herself, giving her strong self-survivability, and she can even provide silence support via Silent Cloud. Because she learns Heal and Heal Wind she is plenty useful as a healer, and since she learns Warrant Barrier she can become even stronger as a shield supporter. Her magic is debuff-focused, but she learns the debuff-camp staple AoE Silent Cloud in a base class, so just having that alone pulls its weight. Mastering just the two Tier 1 classes completes the Heal-Heal Wind-Silent Cloud skill set, so her low investment burden is another advantage. Morgaina exists in the same 11-shaped tree as a healer+mage with no ultimate, but Irene's passive is so excellent and her skill set so good that comparing them would be rude to Irene.

No S/A/B tier rating stated.
