Guide

Note: the following is subjective NamuWiki community opinion (KR server). No explicit S/A/B tier rating is present.

Base class Fighter; Tier 2 has the unique class Arbiter; Tier 3 is Paragon / Radiant Knight / Arc Knight.

Unique passive Balance Scale: crit damage taken reduced 15%~25%; on ally death gain the unremovable buff, and while it is held attack power +15%~25%. At 5-awakening, ally death adds 10%~15% HP recovery; at 6-awakening a reflect is added returning 25% of crit damage taken to the enemy. Focus is on crit-damage resistance and gaining attack up on ally death. "Ally" includes summons, so intentionally using them is appropriate. Since her signature Decision Fall and her ultimate are AoE, the reduced TP cost is a big benefit. From 6-awakening the reflect makes her hard to touch carelessly in PVP.

Classes: Paragon applies Supreme Brand debuff (up to +20% damage taken / crit chance to be hit vs Diene), recovers 1 TP on crit, and reduces damage 50% once per turn; focuses on Diene's own damage and adds survivability, and lets her learn 2 signature skills on the Arbiter-Paragon route; single-target, raid-specialized (great for world boss raids). Radiant Knight gets Holy Light Combo; when enemies within 3 tiles outnumber allies (excluding self), attack +30% and damage taken -30%; grants Covenant of Light buff; strong when outnumbered, PVE-focused. Arc Knight is a common class with Whirlwind Slash and Suppress passive (+40% attack before skill, and lowers adjacent enemies' attack/spell/crit and nullifies their passives); balanced all-rounder, chosen as an endgame class by many; pairs fine with Diene since Decision Fall lets her dive in.

Key skills: Decision Fall: movement + AoE formation-breaker; large demerit (theoretically up to 80% of HP can be lost after attacking), a double-edged sword, but useful to feed Holy Light Combo's lost-HP-ratio damage or to self-lower HP for Radiant Knight buffs. Zenith Blade: self-buff, +30% physical penetration and +1 range for 2 turns, TP 1 / cooldown 3; strong vs high-defense enemies and lets her hit sword infantry one-sidedly without counter. Holy Light Combo: single target 150% + follow-up 30% scaled by lost HP ratio (base 180% guaranteed), TP 2 / cooldown 3, her main damage skill, must-secure. Also Cry of Vigor from Knight is an AoE buff granting extra attacks, worth considering since it benefits from the passive's TP reduction.

Ultimate Nine Crusader: the same ult from Genesis War 3 Part 2; hits random enemies within 3 rings with 9 swords at 20% each (70% vs enemies at 100% HP). Community math: max damage 20%x9=180%; against a full-HP enemy 70%x1+20%x8=230%. Targets are random so damage scatters with more enemies in range; ideally keep only 1~2 enemies in range, but the range is uselessly wide and hard to control. Still usable in showdowns with one main enemy or world boss raids.

Dedicated weapon Justice Crusader: gun-slicer, +15%~30% phys pen; applies unremovable No Healing for 2 turns on hit, and vs enemies with a "healing-block" debuff, target takes +25%~50% damage and gets Powerless Seal, no attack / no counter for 2 turns. Very strong: makes revive-holders useless and effectively locks a target into only fleeing; even awakened Black Prince cannot cleanse it via Ultimate Grima since that is an attack-type skill blocked by Powerless Seal. Awakening only raises phys pen and damage (not essential), but useful to reach 100% phys pen without Wolf Fang and One Man Army. Note: currently only Diene uses gun-slicers, so versatility is worst, but the powerful debuffs make it a must-have.

Equipment: armor is all attack-power-scaled with almost no crit options; the one crit option (TP recovery on crit) is on the Paragon class. For Paragon, hero-grade Chivalrous Hero 2 Assassin 2 gives +10% attack and +10% crit; otherwise go pure attack. At legendary grade, One Man Army 4-set is best for attack (phys pen + attack); Netherworld set is good for survivability (HP + penetration). Accessory: Wolf Fang recommended, reaching ~65% phys pen with weapon + Zenith Blade, up to 85% with One Man Army 2-set or Netherworld 4-set, then use a rune for the last 15% to hit 100% (a big breakpoint). Other options: whetstone for Holy Light Combo follow-up, Yellow Bead for crit if Paragon. Legendary Ancient Shadow Plate suits damage dealers well and, combined with her crit-damage-reducing passive, lets her shrug off crit dealers (most of whom are medium armor, sliced up by the gun-slicer). Note: healing-reduction accessories are swallowed because Justice Crusader's No Healing lands first, so do not use them.

Overall: a physical-penetration specialist that devours medium-armor crit dealers. Weakness: the passive only activates on ally death, but summons can be sacrificed for it, so play with summons. Best partner is Clausewitz, who summons 3 at once, they all die 2 turns later, and shares the same attribute.

Awakening/Manifestation progression: Two parallel tracks: Inner Break and Inner Cube. - Stage 1: Ultimate enhancement 1 / Stat enhancement 1 - Stage 2: Unique Passive enhancement 1 / Stat enhancement 2 - Stage 3: Ultimate enhancement 2 / Artifact acquired - Stage 4: Unique Passive enhancement 2 / Artifact enhancement 1 - Stage 5: Ultimate enhancement 3 / Artifact enhancement 2 - Stage 6: Unique Passive enhancement 3 / Artifact enhancement 3

KR Creator Guide

Character review of Diene of Justice by 하막군의게임튜브 · 2026-08-04

Diene of Justice is the unit that introduced the Sever damage type, which is the single most important thing about her: Sever deals +25% into Medium Armour and -25% into Light Armour, and Medium Armour is very common, so the matchup is favourable more often than not. (17:04)

The reviewer's honest verdict is that she is awkward at low investment. Her classes want her surrounded by enemies, her area skill costs her own health, and her single-target skill has a 3-turn cooldown. At high investment, though, the exclusive weapon is — his words — an absurd piece of equipment, and it carries her. (19:11)

Balance Weight is built to be farmed

Whenever an ally dies she heals 15% and gains a stack of Balance Weight, up to 3: +25% ATK, more Max HP, and -1 TP on area skills per stack — with no "excluding the ultimate" clause, so the ultimate gets cheaper too. Once at 3 stacks it is permanent and unremovable for the rest of the match. (1:35)

"Ally" is generous. Summons and NPCs both count. On defence, park NPCs and let them die. On offence, the cleanest trick he shows is Zabi's Mystic Soldiers: they summon three at once and vanish after two turns, and vanishing counts as death — so you get all three stacks in one go. (2:35)

Class: Paragon for survivability, Radiant Knight for the aggressive line

Paragon applies the Supreme Brand on hit (up to 4 stacks, +80% damage and Crit Rate taken from Diene — big in raids), returns 1 TP on a critical hit, and halves incoming damage once per turn. That last part is what he prefers: it is the same shape as Crow's per-turn reduction and it is reliable. (4:11)

One tested limitation: hitting several enemies at once and critting on all of them still only refunds 1 TP, not one per crit. (4:41)

Radiant Knight grants +30% ATK and -30% damage taken while enemies within 3 tiles outnumber your own nearby allies — strictly more, not equal — and it re-evaluates as you move, flickering on and off in real time. It also hands Light Covenant to the ally with the lowest absolute HP (not lowest percentage), so it usually lands on a summon.

Because Light Covenant includes Diene herself, walking into the enemy line with Decision Fall means taking that first hit at roughly 80% reduction. (5:12)

His overall lean is Paragon, on the grounds that 30% reduction isn't enough to survive standing in the middle of an enemy formation. (18:12)

Gear

Physical Penetration is the stat to chase. With the weapon at full awakening she starts at 30%, Zenith Blade adds 30%, runes about 20% and One Against a Thousand another 20% — which is how you reach the 100% that current builds target. Without the full weapon, make up the difference with Wolf Fang Necklace or Marksman's Magic Bullet. Crit is not a priority on her. (11:52)

The weapon is the character

He calls it broken, and it is the reason to build her at all.

Attacking and dealing damage applies Heal Block — and because her skills are area attacks, that goes onto everything she hits. Attacking an enemy that already has Heal Block adds +30% damage dealt (a damage increase, not an ATK increase, so it is much larger than it looks) and applies Incapacitate.

Incapacitate stops the target attacking at all. Other skills still work, and a healer can cleanse it, but the reviewer's illustration is stark: alternating Heal Block onto one target and Incapacitate onto another wins a 2-versus-1 outright.

Mandatory. Full awakening ideally, a single copy at minimum. (13:14)

Runes

Wind runes to help reach 100% penetration, mixed with Chaos and Ultimate. His view is that the exact arrangement is taste — what matters is hitting 100% penetration between runes and gear. (14:58)

Manifestation

Mostly numbers, but full manifestation stacks +10% Max HP three times for +30% total, halves Crit damage taken and reflects 50% of it, which makes her considerably harder to remove. (15:31)

Artifacts

Eye of the Watcher, her own, is what most people will run: it marks the highest-value enemy within 4 tiles, cuts that target's ATK and M-ATK by 60% when it attacks her, and grants an extra action for killing the marked target.

Pulse of Life+3 more → pairs with Radiant Knight's fewer-allies clause for extra survivability. Will of the Fatal Strike+4 more → he rates poorly here — he tested the ultimate and found it less impressive than expected. (16:01)
