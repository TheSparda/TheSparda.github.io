Unique Passive

Special Tactics Officer

  • Attack Power and Crit Rate increase by 10%~20% with no conditions.
  • At game start, grants "Fire Support" effect to adjacent allies.
  • Fire Support: Attack Power and Spell Power +1012.5%, area damage taken reduced by 1012.5%. Cannot be dispelled. Authority attribute.
  • At 5th Awakening: On attacking and landing a critical hit, gain "Focus" effect (cooldown 1).
  • Focus: Gain 1 TP at end of action. Cannot be dispelled.
  • Manifestation upgrade: Adds the "Emergency Protection Kit" function. When another character ends their action, grants a shield up to 4 times (with cooldown 1 as a condition) to a player-controlled character or summon that meets all of these conditions:
  • Within 5 tiles (melee range) of Anne
  • Health higher than 1 and lower than 50%
  • Has no shield
  • Visible to enemies
  • Can receive buffs

Classes

Tier 3Assault Officer, Overwatch Officer, Breacher

Tier 3: Assault Officer - learns "Barrage Storm"; uses "Precision Rounds". Exclusive class.

  • Max ammo 4.
  • Attack Power and Crit Rate increase by up to 20% with no conditions.
  • Damage taken reduced by 20%, and damage from attacks within 2 tiles reduced by an additional 20%.
  • Has undispellable effect "Reload: Precision Rounds": at game start, load "Precision Rounds" to max. At turn start, reload if ammo fully depleted.
  • Precision Rounds: when consumed to deal damage, adds Attack Power's 30% defense-ignoring physical damage to the target.
  • Has undispellable effect "Chain Fire":
  • When ammo damage hits an enemy, grants undispellable effect "Chain Marking".
  • Chain Marking: Defense -15%. Removed when attacked by "Chain Fire" effect or at turn start. Duration 1 turn.
  • At end of character's turn, fires ammo at non-adjacent enemies within 4 tiles that have "Chain Marking", dealing Attack Power's 30% damage. Can crit. Consumes ammo; does not trigger if none.
  • All Tier 3 classes have "Melee Counter Fire": when hit by a melee attack, consume ammo to counter with a shot.

Tier 3: Overwatch Officer - learns "Overwatch Mode"; uses "Suppression Rounds". Exclusive class.

  • Max ammo 3.
  • Crit Rate and Crit Damage increase by up to 20% with no conditions.
  • Damage taken reduced by 20%, crit rate taken reduced by 30%, and damage taken from debuffed enemies reduced by an additional 10%.
  • Has undispellable effect "Reload: Suppression Rounds": at game start, load "Suppression Rounds" to max. At turn start, reload if ammo fully depleted.
  • Suppression Rounds: when consumed to deal damage, deals Attack Power's 20% physical damage and grants "Combat Suppression" effect. Dark attribute.
  • Combat Suppression: Attack Power, Spell Power, counter damage, Crit Rate -30%. Lasts 1 turn. Cannot be dispelled.
  • Has undispellable effect "Reaction Fire":
  • Before receiving a single-target attack from an enemy within 4 tiles, a certain chance to fire ammo dealing Attack Power's 30% damage.
  • The crit-rate-taken reduction amount is added to the trigger chance (30%~80%).
  • Does not trigger while unable to counter or against counter-immune attacks. Requires ammo to trigger.
  • Has "Melee Counter Fire" (all Tier 3).

Tier 3: Breacher - learns "High-Explosive Rocket"; uses "Detonation Rounds". Exclusive class; has Imperial Gunner-Breacher exclusive tree.

  • Max ammo 3.
  • Attack Power increases by up to 30% with no conditions.
  • Damage taken reduced by 30%. If a shield is present, damage taken reduced by an additional 10%.
  • When using a "Heavy Weapon" attribute skill, Attack Power increases by 30% and that skill's cooldown is reduced by 1.
  • Has undispellable effect "Reload: Detonation Rounds": at game start, load "Detonation Rounds" to max. At turn start, reload if ammo fully depleted.
  • Detonation Rounds: when consumed to deal damage, adds Attack Power's 15% resistance-ignoring fire magic damage to the target.
  • If target has no fire debuff: grants "Incineration" effect.
  • Incineration: Defense and Resistance -20%. At end of character's turn, resistance-ignoring magic damage equal to 20% of the higher of Attack Power or Spell Power. Duration 2 turns. Damage-over-time attribute. Fire attribute.
  • If target has a fire debuff: deals Attack Power's 40% resistance-ignoring magic damage to the target and 1 surrounding ring around it. Can crit.
  • Has "Melee Counter Fire" (all Tier 3).

Guide

The following is NamuWiki community assessment (KR server), subjective community opinion:

  • Overall: A gunner that is fairly sturdy thanks to her self-shield and passive, equipped with area firepower and excellent debuffs. She can be run as a melee-oriented crit dealer, but running her as a dot-damage + debuffer via the Brecher + heavy-weapon combo gives a more desirable impression. It is also notable that enhancing her passive with the 2nd awakening makes her a shield supporter.
  • Class priority (from Class section): Brecher is recommended to raise first (learns her core skills Fire Jet and High-Explosive Rocket, both heavy-weapon type; ultimate also has heavy-weapon type for synergy; less crit-dependent so also viable in PVP). Assault Officer is a melee-leaning dealer, usable but hard to fully utilize due to gunner fragility. Overwatch Officer has the lowest raising priority (counter/crit focused, needs heavy investment).
  • Gear (recommendations): Prioritize attack power (for firepower and shields) plus a bit more crit rate to guarantee crit procs. Impression leans more toward dot-damage/support than pure dealer. Hero-grade: Assassin 2 + Chivalrous 2 mix. Legendary-grade: Crow 4-set (secures 20% crit rate) as baseline; higher ceiling is the Assassin/Killer set (provides both ATK% and crit damage) if crit rate is secured. For ATK%-focused Brecher, Killer 2-set, ideally mixed with Phantom Thief 2-set (crit dmg +10%, crit rate +15%). Accessory: legendary-grade "Go-Young-Ban" is ideal for dealers; hero-grade uses Whetstone or Wolf Fang.
  • No S/A/B tier letter rating is stated in the source.

Awakening/Manifestation progression: No dedicated stage-by-stage awakening section is present in the source. KR Creator Guide

Character review of Anne Millennium by 하막군의게임튜브 · 2026-08-04

Anne Millennium is a support gunner, not a damage dealer. The reviewer is direct about it: even with Breacher's stacked +155% attack bonuses, her damage doesn't land as hard as the numbers suggest. Where she excels is propping up someone else — and specifically Cyrano Bernstein. (28:50)

Her passive is refreshingly short by current standards, which he remarks on more than once.

The Emergency Defence Kit is built for Cyrano's clone

At every unit's end of turn — anyone's, including summons — she gives an Emergency Barrier (40% shield) to the lowest-health player or summon within 5 tiles that is below 50% HP and has no shield. One per turn, and each use spends a charge; charges come back when an allied player or summon dies.

The reviewer immediately spots what that describes: Ectoplasm+1 more →. Cyrano runs forward, splits his health and leaves a clone under 50% with no shield, and the act of casting ends his action — so the barrier lands on the clone straight away. He recommends her on defence teams specifically to make that clone unkillable. (3:35)

Manifestation adds charges and range: 2 charges at 2 manifestations, 3 at 4, and 4 at full manifestation, which also gives the Fire Support authority to two allies instead of one. (4:39)

Ammunition is the whole class system

Every class has a counter-fire and its own round type, and the reviewer stresses that picking the right round is the character. One rule underpins all of it: rounds reload at turn start, and no ammunition means none of the reactive effects fire at all. Run dry and the class simply stops working. (7:44)

Assault Officer loads Precision Rounds (30% DEF-ignoring) and adds Chain Fire, marking a hit target and dealing another 30% at end of turn — which itself fires a round, adding another 30% DEF-ignoring. He works it out: a single basic attack becomes roughly 190% of ATK. One caveat he tested — the critical hit from Chain Fire does not return TP through the passive. (5:42)

Overwatch Officer loads Suppression Rounds, applying Combat Suppression: -30% ATK, M-ATK, counterattack damage and Crit Rate, unremovable. Its Reactive Fire triggers before she is attacked, on a chance scaling with her Crit-Rate-taken reduction — around 60% at full investment, though he notes there's no published formula. This is the "make the enemy weak" build. (8:16)

Breacher is the damage build and what most players run. +30% ATK, -30% damage taken, another -10% while shielded, and heavy-weapon skills gain another +30% ATK and a cooldown reduction. Its Detonation Round deals 15% — but against a target already carrying a fire debuff it instead detonates for 40% DEF-ignoring damage in a ring, and it can crit. For comparison, he notes Heidelun's Collapse is 25%. (11:22, 12:24)

Fire Jet is the main skill

SkillTagsTPRangeCDEffect
Fire Jet----covers a 3x4 for 90%, applies Incineration (the fire debuff Breacher's rounds need) and Slow, grants her a shield, clears a debuff under Breacher, and has a 40% chance to leave fire on the ground — which, though the tooltip doesn't say so, is a Control Zone, so it blocks a lane. Under Breacher its cooldown drops to 2. Fire Jet plus Breacher is effectively the character. (14:28) One oddity he reported: despite being magic damage, Fire Jet is affected by Veramode's Physical Interference. He treats it as a bug. (15:29)

Barrage is the one to skip

Barrage+2 more → is the only skill spending all four rounds, so in theory it's the Breacher detonation payoff — move up to 3 tiles and hit a random enemy within 2 tiles four times, with round damage halved.

Its flaw: "random enemy" includes barricades. He describes firing into a group and watching two of the four rounds go into the scenery. He considers it a bug and doesn't expect it to be fixed. (16:01, 18:03)

High-Explosive Rocket

150% over a wide 3-tile-radius area that knocks back and strips buffs. Its Doomsday rider fires the rocket when she dies — 180% under Breacher — so simply having it slotted is worth something.

Against large monsters it applies Siege Mark (+50% damage taken), which does not stack with Heidelun's Paradox Worm — making Anne the substitute when Heidelun isn't available. (19:15, 20:06)

The ultimate covers a 7x7 with Incineration and a chance of fire terrain — the same footprint and range as Kana Milanovich's. (21:06)

Loadout: Shock Shot, Fire Jet, High-Explosive Rocket, on Breacher. (22:38)

Gear

The Emperor set beats One Against a Thousand with Envoy of the Storm if you have it. Accessory: Time Compass is his pick; Ancient Hero's Ring otherwise, or a fully awakened Crack Sword for the Crit Rate, which feeds her TP recovery. (23:08)

The weapon: ★3 minimum

ATK and Max HP, a stacking ATK/penetration buff on skills, +2 max ammunition — which matters given how much the class depends on having rounds — and a Crit-Rate-taken reduction.

★3 adds Preemptive Attack, ★5 adds Weak Point Detection, ★6 adds physical lifesteal. Not strictly mandatory: Xenia's weapon is a fine substitute, though he notes it costs more than six copies of this one. (24:40)

Runes

Chaos throughout with Sun in slot 6 for health. One specific reason: Shock Shot has a 100% coefficient, and without skill-damage runes the AI prefers basic attacks over it — so skill-damage runes change what the AI chooses to do with her. (25:12)

Artifacts

Blast Padding is the pick, because it pairs directly with Breacher: +30% ATK, +20% shields given, and an unremovable turn-start shield that keeps Breacher's shielded-damage-reduction permanently active.

Advanced Tactical Magazine+1 more → is the situational alternative — it reloads immediately when she runs out rather than at turn start, which addresses the ammunition problem directly, and heals for 50% of ATK. Bane's Hammer is a raid artifact. (27:16)
