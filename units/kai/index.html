Unique Passive: Heart of Embers [Passive]

  • Call of the Flames: Immune to mind control. Ignores cooldown control. At game start, gain maximum stacks of Return of Flames. Before the end of each turn, if no young Hellkite is present, gain 1 stack of Return of Flames; if the young Hellkite is outside a 4-tile range, Return of Flames can be used to dismiss and resummon it on an adjacent tile.

Ember Revival

LvEffect
1When receiving fatal damage, recover 60% HP and gain 1 TP. Deal fixed damage equal to 40% of ATK to enemies within 1 ring. Remove all of your own debuffs and exiled status. Damage received decreases by 15% for 1 turn.
2When receiving fatal damage, recover 80% HP and gain 2 TP. Deal fixed damage equal to 50% of ATK to enemies within 1 ring. Remove all of your own debuffs and exiled status. Damage received decreases by 17.5% for 1 turn.
3When receiving fatal damage, recover 100% HP and gain 3 TP. Deal fixed damage equal to 60% of ATK to enemies within 1 ring. Remove all of your own debuffs and exiled status. Damage received decreases by 20% for 1 turn.

Ash Resonance

LvEffect
1Fire/Radiant resistance +15%. When attacking an enemy with 4 or more fire debuffs, gain one additional action per turn (cooldown 2).
2Fire/Radiant resistance +30%. When attacking an enemy with 3 or more fire debuffs, gain one additional action per turn (cooldown 1). When the Hellkite's Gaze artifact is equipped, reduce the required fire debuff count for the additional action by 1.

A close-range swordsman who uses Sprint to gain additional actions, delivers successive powerful strikes, and advances to the kill position to maintain pressure.
