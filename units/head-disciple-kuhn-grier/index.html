Guide

Overview / performance: Base class is Mage. Tier 2 has the unique class "Dual Magician"; Tier 3 classes are Frost Mage / Elemental Breaker / Polarity Tuner. Her ultimate, Double Elemental Blast, is a dual-element version of the "Tri-Elemental Blast" combining fire and ice magic. (The wiki jokingly asks "Medroa?")

Unique passive: Spell power +10%~20%. Fire skill/basic attack grants 1 stack Starlight; ice skill/basic attack grants 1 stack Moonlight. More fire attacks = more firepower but loses survival buffs; more ice attacks = more survival buffs but less firepower. At 5-dupe Kun starts with Starlight 1 / Moonlight 1. Using Star/Moon Cast in Dual Mode gives +1 element stack from the elemental attack plus +2 from Dual Mode, and the acquisition effect flips the opposite stack, so one of Starlight/Moonlight instantly hits 5 stacks while the other is fully lost, making the first-turn action extremely important. The unconditional spell power boost is nice but the passive is not easy to operate. With the signature weapon, thanks to Libra-Fire, even all-in fire is survivable via spell-power-based shields, greatly reducing the risk of committing to one side, so it is worth securing at least a copy of the exclusive weapon. Minor ally support via Libra buff is possible but hard to aim for. Note: like Witch Cameo, she cannot learn the ordinary Tri-Elemental Blast skill; unlike Cameo, this is because she is still in training.

Classes: Tier 3 has three: Frost Mage (revival passive), Elemental Breaker (adds resistance-down debuff on elemental attacks), Polarity Tuner (ice->fire order gives survival buff, fire->ice order gives firepower buff, and having both grants self-healing).

  • Frost Mage: a shared class that can learn Moonlight; has a guaranteed 1-time revival and Moonlight is quite strong, but has zero spell power buff so firepower is poor and it runs a lone unique tree; lowest raising priority for Kun (as with other mages).
  • Elemental Breaker: learns the exclusive "Dual Command"; applies resistance-down debuff on elemental attacks. Fire Chain: fire attack lowers fire resistance up to -35%, max 2 stacks, 3 turns. Ice Chain: ice attack lowers ice resistance up to -35%, max 2 stacks, 3 turns. No need to worry about juggling, since you can focus one element; the resistance-cut lasts 3 turns, leaving room to use the other element.
  • Polarity Tuner: learns the exclusive "Frost Fire"; gains a buff when using the element opposite to the previous action. Ice Resonance: from fire skill after previous ice skill, spell power +50%, 3 turns, undispellable. Fire Resonance: from ice skill after previous fire skill, defense/resistance +50%, 3 turns, undispellable. Having both: recover 30% spell power as HP at turn end. Strong reward for alternating elements; her exclusive skills are element-selectable or dual fire/ice, so she can always get the resonance she wants, but it is limited to "skills" so the Dual-Mode-changed basic attack cannot gain resonance and you must remember what element you used last action.

Core skills and ultimate:

  • Dual Mode: TP 1, cooldown 2. Like her master Witch Cameo, one skill with 3 options; cannot use lightning but has a fire+ice dual-element basic attack as the 3rd option. A key core skill.
  • Star Cast: +2 Starlight, basic attack becomes Fire Arrow (100% spell power, 2-turn Burn, +50% resistance-ignoring extra damage vs fire-debuffed enemies; Burn = 15% spell power fire fixed damage at turn end, 2 turns).
  • Moon Cast: +2 Moonlight, basic attack becomes Crystal Fist.
  • Dual Cast: +1 Starlight and +1 Moonlight, basic attack becomes Double Stellar.
  • Dual Command: TP 2, cooldown 3. Pick Fire Breath Mk.2 / Ice Breath Mk.2 / Double Breath. The AoE counterpart to Dual Mode.
  • Fire Breath Mk.2: 3x3 directional, 90% spell power fire, Burn + heal reduction.
  • Ice Breath Mk.2: 3x3 directional, 80% spell power ice, Cold Slow, 20% chance to create Ice Zone, control area, 2 turns.
  • Double Breath: 3x3 directional, 70% spell power fire+ice, Burn + Cold Slow; if enemy HP <=50%, +40% extra damage (so up to ~110% spell power on low-HP targets).
  • Frost Fire: TP 3, cooldown 3. Single target 140% spell power; +30% and Blaze: cannot attack, cannot counter, 2 turns if fire debuff present; +30% and Severe Cold: class passive cannot trigger, 2 turns if ice debuff present. Her main single-target damage; well built up it can deal ~200% spell power plus strong debuffs. But fire/ice debuffs can bounce off (action-control immunity etc.) so landing both is hard, enemies you can debuff first are basically raid bosses (who resist Blaze/Severe Cold), and in PvP debuffs get cleansed easily, so realizing her firepower potential is not easy.
  • Moon Light: TP 4, cooldown 3. 100% spell power ice + Freeze on target, plus 50% spell power ice to 3-tile area + Cold Slow. The highest-tier ice magic; the highest-multiplier AoE among her normal skills. Applies stun and area Cold Slow; on a single target it deals 150% spell power, making it stronger than Double Elemental Blast as a single-target skill.
  • Double Elemental Blast (ultimate): designate a tile within 3 tiles in a straight line, hit 5x3 area for 100%~120% spell power, apply Combustion Collapse: 25% spell power fire fixed damage at turn end, no healing, 2 turns and Ice Bind: cannot move, no extra move/action, 1 turn; consumes all Starlight/Moonlight stacks for +3% damage per stack; 15% chance (10%~30% by rank) to create a 1-turn Combustion Zone or Ice Zone tile. Damage is slightly low but the debuff DoT and effects are strong. Kun has high skill utility so it is not easy to use, but the return matches.

Recommended equipment: Exclusive weapon is Pyro Glacier; extra stats magic penetration and max HP. Provides firepower support and a powerful shield. At 3-dupe+ it adds TP recovery (20%~30% chance) but being probabilistic it is hard to leverage. Comet Veil: shield 3%~5% spell power per stack, 1 turn, max 10 stacks, undispellable.

  • Armor: all-in spell power solves both firepower and survival, so spell power stacking is essential. Since her survival performance is better than firepower, Cardinal 4-set (boosts HP, spell power, and TP) is appropriate. For firepower, Chancellor 4-set can also grab move/survival via the "Retreat" effect, but given her debuff/survival-weighted design it is only "usable," not ideal.
  • Accessory: Black Dice for survival feels appropriate, or go full survival with greaves-type accessories.

Evaluation: A tanky-style debuffer that disrupts enemies with debuffs and DoT while enduring via shields and defense. She does have some firepower, but the coefficients are somewhat mediocre, her spell power ceiling needs build-up, and extra damage is conditional on debuffs, so firepower needs warm-up time, giving a debuff-first impression. Above all her defensive performance is excellent, so treat her as having gone the survival route. Note 3: though never highlighted, considering she rose to Imperial Four Heavenly King despite being a Pandragon Mage Corps POW, ultimately abandoned both Gaishir and Pandragon yet survived past the Genesis War, married a magnate (her granddaughter becoming lord of Librebille) and lived a full life, she can fairly be called a survival specialist.

  • She is skill-dependent so TP consumption is high, but she has no TP support and her passive and class passives are even trickier to use than her master's, so she is a character with many operational difficulties and is not easy to run.

KR Creator Guide

Character review of Head Disciple Kuhn Grier by 하막군의게임튜브 · 2026-08-04

This is the most negative review in the series, and the reviewer says so up front: he posted to the community warning people off her before the video went up. He also thinks she is the best-looking character the game has produced — he's genuinely impressed by how far the illustration work has come — which is the whole tension of the review. (0:00, 0:30, 1:31, 2:03)

His benchmark: even played well, she comes out below Witch Cameo Beaufort, and below the original Koon Greer. (1:31)

Starlight Flame and Moonlight Ice

Fire skills grant Starlight, frost skills grant Moonlight, and she starts with one of each — but only from 5★. Below that she starts with nothing, so 5★ is the practical floor, and the free copies get you there. (2:34, 3:06)

One wording trap he flags: it reads "fire-attribute skill or basic attack", which he first read as "any basic attack". It isn't — it means a fire-attribute basic attack. (3:06, 3:38)

The numbers are where the review turns. Starlight is +2% damage dealt per stack, five stacks, so +10%. Moonlight is -3% damage taken per stack, so -15%. Both figures are identical at full manifestation. He repeats this several times because it keeps surprising him. (4:08, 4:40, 5:13)

Gaining one type converts the other across and leaves a Remnant — Flame Remnant or Ice Remnant, capped at five, one per conversion. Five conversions on a one-turn cooldown means five turns minimum, and only if every conversion goes the same way. (6:15, 6:46)

At the start of the turn she also hands Libra to a random ally within 2 tiles, keyed to whichever stack she holds more of: more Starlight gives attack and spell power, more Moonlight gives a shield, and an equal count gives Harmony — physical and magic penetration resistance, 12% at 5★ and 15% at full. (4:08, 6:46, 7:16, 7:46)

Class: Elemental Breaker, effectively by default

Elemental Breaker applies Fire Chain or Frost Chain when she hits a target that already carries a matching-element debuff, each cutting that resistance by 35%. He's clear that 35% is a real number — it multiplies the final damage — but two things blunt it. The debuff has to be there first, so either she spends a turn applying it or a partner like Kana Milanovich does. And applying both chains at once does not give 70%; it still resolves as 35%.

His read is that this class is waiting for a resistance-shred meta that hasn't arrived — the game is currently a penetration meta. (8:16, 8:48, 9:19, 9:50, 10:22)

Polarity Tuner grants Ice Resonance or Fire Resonance when the element she casts differs from her last one, and holding both heals her at end of turn. The +50% spell power is genuinely large — she has very little spell power scaling anywhere else — but alternating elements fights directly against the passive, which wants stacks piled on one side.

The way out is the dual-element skills, which count as both and grant both resonances at once. That, he says, is the only sane way to play the class. (10:22, 10:52, 11:23, 11:53)

The ultimate

Double Elemental Blast hits a 5×3 for 115% at 5★ and 140% at full manifestation, applying Burn and Ice Bind, then consumes every Starlight and Moonlight stack for +3% damage each — nine or ten stacks, so around +30%. It has a 20–30% chance to leave a Combustion or Frost Zone.

The +3% per stack, the zone chance and the zone effects are all unchanged by manifestation; only the base 115% → 140% moves. And because it eats every stack, you rebuild the whole passive afterwards. (17:29, 17:59, 18:29, 22:04, 22:35)

Gear

Chancellor of Darkness for armour and Jade Ornament for the accessory, though he adds that Book of the Sage does the same job and that it genuinely doesn't matter much here. (19:00)

The weapon is the reason she works at all

Pyro Glacier is the part of the kit he rates. Fire skills apply Wrath of the Sun to the target and frost skills apply Wrath of the Moon; between them they cut DEF and RES and attack and spell power. Cast a dual-element skill and both land at once, three stacks each, which at 6★ he puts at roughly -45% defence and -45% offence on the target.

At end of action she also gains Meteor Veil per buff she's holding, to ten stacks — and she holds a lot of buffs, so ten is easy.

★3 adds a 20% chance on any non-ultimate cast to refund TP and cut cooldowns (on its own 2-turn cooldown, which he finds weak). ★5 takes that cooldown to 1. ★6 raises the chance to 30% and adds a shield worth 50% of spell power.

His verdict: the weapon is mandatory and ★3 is the minimum. (19:00, 19:30, 20:01, 20:32, 21:03, 21:34)

Runes

The Pride and Devotion runes currently on the wheel work well paired up. Otherwise a plain mage setup — Moon and Lightning, or all Lightning. (21:34, 22:04)

Manifestation

The first stage finally adds the two effects the passive has been building toward: Eternal Flame at five Starlight, giving magic penetration and an extra action; and Momentary Glacier at five Moonlight, giving physical damage reduction and one hit taken as zero.

Their cooldowns are 2 and 3 turns, and both stay at 2 and 3 at full manifestation — which he says is why he wouldn't use them much even then. Stage 4 adds 10% Max HP and, in his words, that's really it. Stage 6 makes Libra go to two allies instead of one. Even the passive's spell power and Max HP figures don't move. (22:35, 23:05, 23:36, 24:07)

Artifacts

Resonance Stone of Magic+2 more → raises spell power and damage, but only after she attacks, so it's a turn late. Wall of Imagination+3 more → is the usual mage survivability pick. Craving for Warmth+1 more → is her own: +20% Max HP and an aura that cuts allies' debuff durations by 1 and restores 15% of Max HP at the start of the turn. (24:38, 25:09)

Verdict

That artifact is the tell — she is built as a supporter, not a dealer. The problem is that the support isn't strong either, and 5★ versus full manifestation is the difference between 12% and 15% penetration resistance on one ally.

The one thing he likes is that opening penetration-resistance buff, which does help a tank hold. It isn't a reason to field her.

His closing advice is to skip her, take the free copy from the event, and save for the next character. (25:40, 26:11, 26:43, 27:13)
