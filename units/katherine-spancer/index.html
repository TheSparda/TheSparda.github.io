Signature Gear

Guide

  • Base class is Fighter. Tier 2 has the exclusive class "Iron Maiden"; Tier 3 classes are Striker / Highlander / Dreadnought. Ultimate is the paladin-tradition Plasma Slash.
  • Unique passive "Self-Discipline Fighting Spirit": can share adjacent allies' damage, and when taking damage gains the unremovable buff "Self-Discipline Fighting Spirit" for 2 turns. Max 3 stacks; +25% ATK/HP per stack; at 3 stacks, after taking damage, once per turn recover 510% HP; at 5 awakening or higher, after taking damage the longest remaining active skill cooldown is reduced by 1; when attacking, applies "Weakness Exposure" debuff for 2 turns equal to stacks (Weakness Exposure: damage taken +10%, +10% chance to be crit, max 3 stacks). Designed to be run outright as a tank; the more she tanks, the stronger the debuffs she can apply on attack.
  • Tier 3 classes: Striker specializes in AoE and crit rate but is not much recommended since Catherine is tank-specialized rather than a dealer. Highlander gives up to +40% ATK on combat attacks only, +15% crit and 50%-of-ATK HP recovery when HP is 50% or below; lower priority since Catherine is not a dealer and her key skills/ultimate are not combat-attack judged, but Reverse Scale Flash is worth taking as it reaches 170% multiplier (high among TP 2 skills) and strongly raises the target's chance of being crit by 20% and reduces their damage dealt by 30%. Dreadnought is the exclusive class that learns "Crushing Vacuum Wave"; via the Iron Maiden-Dreadnought exclusive tree, DEF rises by adjacent enemy count x up to 25% and ATK rises by 30% of DEF, plus armor type changes to Heavy Armor. Generally the most recommended class since Catherine gets all her main skills in Iron Maiden-Dreadnought and wants Heavy Armor for DEF.
  • Exclusive skills / ultimate: Two exclusives: "Thorn Barrier" (learned in Iron Maiden) and "Crushing Vacuum Wave" (learned in Dreadnought). Thorn Barrier (TP 1): escorts allies within 2 tiles for 2 turns and grants the unremovable buff dealing 50%-of-DEF physical fixed damage to melee attackers; very essential for tank Catherine, with good damage reflect, but note it provides no DEF buff despite the name, and has no usage limit so a poorly-built Catherine using it can get pummeled and ascend first. Crushing Vacuum Wave (TP 3): jump to a chosen tile within 3, leap-attack down, deal 50% ATK to adjacent enemies and apply Taunt for 1 turn (taunted enemies are forced to basic-attack the caster, and Catherine takes 20% less damage from them); a move+taunt skill whose jump lets it cross terrain/obstacles; dive into enemy packs since Catherine benefits from more nearby enemies. Plasma Slash ultimate hits enemies within 5 tiles forward in a chosen direction; the effect is flashier but was nerfed to a single 1-line row instead of 3 rows like in Genesis 3: deals 50~80% ATK plus extra 50% of remaining HP, then applies "Chill Slow" and "Burn" debuffs for 2 turns; it gives fire and ice debuffs but no electric status at all (Plasma in name only), and there may be synergy chances since many Pandragon royals have ice-type skills.
  • Evaluation: A tank-type character where raising DEF solves everything. But because DEF must be raised well, it is hard to appreciate her value at low levels; equip proper armor sets and train her hard to bring out her true worth. In enemy-attribute raids she is sometimes deployed in place of Landam (off-attribute) to serve a debuffer + tank role.
  • No S/A/B tier rating stated.

Awakening/Manifestation progression: - Stage 1: Ultimate Enhancement 1, Stat Enhancement 1 - Stage 2: Unique Passive Enhancement 1, Stat Enhancement 2 - Stage 3: Ultimate Enhancement 2, Artifact acquired - Stage 4: Unique Passive Enhancement 2, Artifact Enhancement 1 - Stage 5: Ultimate Enhancement 3, Artifact Enhancement 2 - Stage 6: Unique Passive Enhancement 3, Artifact Enhancement 3
