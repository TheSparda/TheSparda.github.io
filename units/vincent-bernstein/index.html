Guide

The following is subjective NamuWiki community opinion (KR server):

His damage is not much to expect, but if set up well he is extremely tanky, so using Defensive Stance learned from Soldier he can be used as a tank protecting allies, or, like Landam who also overlaps two classes, as a kind of buffer. In that case the exclusive weapon, which grants an attack buff to adjacent allies, is useful.

Compared to that rival Landam, the biggest difference is Royal Guard vs Royal Lancer: Royal Lancer learns God-Slaying Spear, whereas Royal Guard at most has only Crushing Strike, a melee stun skill. Even though the stun skill is good, Landam can also act twice while Bernstein has nothing like that and no ultimate either, so calling him a Dark-attribute inferior version of Landam is not entirely wrong. On top of that, since he is not an escort tank but a tank that shares half the damage, mistakenly taking Shakhbari's Mugeuk Jingwangseom can cause a disaster where both ascend (die). Since high awakening only raises the numbers, there is not much merit, so overall he is not a recommended character. With even Landam falling out of the first team, the need to field Bernstein (who is worse than Landam) is really low. Like Irus, he was left neglected without an ultimate or promo video, and was in the same boat awaiting only a rework.

With the Inner Break awakening, Bernstein at 2+ Break has come to boast performance rivaling Isabel, at least in PvE. In the case of Engagement Order, which is exclusive to Inner Break, at full Break he can grant ATK / Spell power +30% held undispellable for 2 turns, so at full Break it also became possible to use him as a "cheer squad leader" in PvP.

The exclusive weapon Master's Dignity is a spear/shield weapon that provides DEF and damage-taken-reduction as additional stats, and has a weapon skill that raises DEF and RES when HP is above 50% while also raising the wearer's ATK. When Bernstein equips it, the exclusive effect provides the ATK increase not only to the wearer but also to adjacent allies, so it is considered an essential weapon if you intend to use Bernstein.
