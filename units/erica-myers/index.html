Unique Passive - Will of Silver

First acquired: At end of character's turn, if no enemy was attacked this turn, choose and trigger one of Sleep Explosion Trap 1 or Catch Breath 1. This choice and effect are not revealed to enemies and are not treated as a skill.

  • Sleep Explosion Trap 1: Designate tiles within 2 tiles in a straight line, placing up to 3 sleep explosion traps on empty tiles in range that last 1 turn. If an enemy steps on a sleep explosion trap, it explodes dealing physical fixed damage equal to 25% of attack power and inflicts Sleep for 1 turn.
  • Catch Breath 1: At start of next turn, recover an additional 1 TP and increase critical hit chance by 10% for 1 turn.
  • Sleep action control: Falls asleep, unable to act. When the effect expires and it wakes, gains Sleep Resistance for 2 turns. Lasts 1 turn.
  • Sleep Resistance: Cannot be inflicted with Sleep. Lasts 2 turns.

Max Awakening: At end of character's turn, if no enemy was attacked this turn, choose and trigger one of Sleep Explosion Trap 4 or Catch Breath 4. Not revealed to enemies, not treated as a skill.

  • Sleep Explosion Trap 4: Same as trap 1 but explosion deals physical fixed damage equal to 50% of attack power and inflicts Sleep for 1 turn.
  • Catch Breath 4: At start of next turn, recover an additional 1 TP and increase critical hit chance by 30% for 1 turn. Reduces the cooldown of the one skill with the longest remaining cooldown by 1.

Max Manifestation: Critical hit damage +30% and damage taken -30%. At end of character's turn, if no enemy was attacked this turn, choose and trigger one of Sleep Explosion Trap 7, Catch Breath 7, or Helping Hand 3. Not revealed to enemies, not treated as a skill.

  • Sleep Explosion Trap 7 cannot be removed: Same placement; explosion deals physical fixed damage equal to 70% of attack power and inflicts Sleep for 2 turns.
  • Catch Breath 7 cannot be removed: At start of next turn, recover an additional 1 TP and increase critical hit chance by 50% for 1 turn. Reduces the cooldown of the one skill with the longest remaining cooldown by 1.
  • Helping Hand 3 cannot be removed: Designate another ally within 3 tiles, granting Helping Hand 3 effect for 1 turn.
  • Helping Hand 3 (buff): Lasts 1 turn. Defense/Resistance +30%. When this effect is removed, clears all debuffs and recovers 1 TP.

Classes

Tier list:

  • Tier 1: Archer
  • Tier 2: Hunter, Lightwatcher
  • Tier 3: Eagle, Bowmaster, Purifier

Common classes:

  • Archer (Tier 1): Basic class using bow or crossbow to deal ranged physical damage.
  • Archery 1: +5% critical chance on combat attacks made without moving.
  • Archery 2: +10% critical chance on combat attacks made without moving.
  • Hunter (Tier 2): Skilled hunter who tracks and takes down enemies from range with bow/crossbow.
  • Hawk Eye 1: +15% critical chance on combat attacks made without moving; +20% attack power when attacking enemies afflicted with movement control effects.
  • Hawk Eye 2: +20% critical chance without moving; +30% attack power vs movement-controlled enemies.
  • Eagle (Tier 3): Master hunter who uses bow/crossbow and skills to lull enemies to sleep from range.
  • Hunting Master 1: +25% critical chance on combat attacks without moving; +40% attack power on combat attacks vs movement-controlled enemies, and recovers HP equal to 25% of attack power.
  • Hunting Master 2: +30% critical chance without moving; +50% attack power vs movement-controlled enemies, recovers HP equal to 30% of attack power.
  • Note: A fixed-turret class that must attack without moving. Characteristically deals bonus damage to enemies with movement-control debuffs.
  • Bowmaster (Tier 3): Ranged weapon expert who wields bow/crossbow with speed and precision.
  • Ambush Shot 1: +30% critical chance on combat attacks; 30% chance to trigger a follow-up attack dealing 30% of attack power.
  • Ambush Shot 2: +40% critical chance on combat attacks; 50% chance to trigger a follow-up attack dealing 30% of attack power.

Unique classes:

  • Lightwatcher: Light archer who destroys and purifies evil from range with bow/crossbow.
  • Inner Light 1: +10% attack power on combat attacks. After a combat attack, recovers HP equal to 15% of attack power and removes 1 of own debuffs.
  • Inner Light 2: +15% attack power on combat attacks. After a combat attack, recovers HP equal to 25% of attack power and removes 1 own debuff.
  • Purifier: Advanced class filled with firm conviction and burning passion, using bow/crossbow.
  • Burning Flash 1: +25% attack power on combat attacks. After a combat attack, recovers HP equal to 30% of attack power and removes 2 own debuffs.
  • Burning Flash 2: +30% attack power on combat attacks. After a combat attack, recovers HP equal to 40% of attack power and removes 2 own debuffs.

Signature Gear

Guide

NamuWiki community assessment, KR server; subjective community opinion.

Performance:

  • Base class Archer; Tier 3 classes Eagle/Bowmaster/Purifier; ultimate is Hoaeungjo.
  • Unique passive Will of Silver: at end of action, if no enemy was attacked this turn, chooses one of Sleep Explosion Trap or Catch Breath (hidden, not a skill).
  • Sleep Explosion Trap places up to 3 traps; stepping on one immediately inflicts Sleep, disabling for 1 turn, plus strong attack-scaling damage, making it a very strong passive. Good for survival on Erica, who is fragile as an archer. Note: traps only go on empty tiles, so they cannot be placed where allies or enemies stand.

Purifier class note: With the generous "combat attack" condition, it grants attack boost, healing, and debuff removal, an overpowered class; removing 2 debuffs via passive is a big merit in a game where debuff removal usually works by count. Bowmaster class note: +40% crit with the very generous combat-attack condition plus a follow-up attack makes it an excellent dealer class.

Key skills and ultimate:

  • Wind Circle Arrow: TP 1 cost; deals 50% attack-power damage, on kill grants an extra attack/skill, on failure to kill a follow-up deals 80% additional. Cooldown 3. A ranged version of Mei Nautilon's Gale Fang; only 1 TP but 130% total ratio (?) and an extra attack on kill. Since the 80% comes in as a follow-up, a whetstone can boost it to 170% max. Long 3-turn cooldown.
  • Light-Purge Round: TP 3, range 2-5; 70% attack-power damage, removes all target TP, deals (removed TP x 20% attack) radiance fixed damage to adjacent enemies, and applies Suppress (no TP gain) for 1 turn; ignores cover. Erica's main skill. Against an enemy with 5 TP it becomes a 170% single-target hit that also strips all TP. Cooldown 3.
  • Hoaeungjo (ultimate): range 2-7 single-target, 160-200% damage, cannot be countered or guarded. Because Light-Purge Round is so strong, the ult sees little use, but it is still a fine sniping tool. Graphically it looks like a straight-line AoE but it is not.

Recommended equipment:

  • The exclusive weapon's skill-damage boost is nice, but it triggers after casting and the chance-based heal is very iffy for an archer who dies when hit; not considered essential. The shared Legendary bow Palenote has strong crit-related options and is considered a good alternative.
  • Armor: since survival is low value for a ranged dealer, go full damage. At Hero grade use the Assassin set; at Legendary use the Crow-outfit-motif Raven set, stacking crit chance and crit damage.
  • Accessories: whetstone works given the high follow-up weight, or Golden Dice since crit matters; if crit is sufficient, High Spirit Orb is an option.

Evaluation:

  • Considered the best Dark-element counter: a Light-element ranged burst dealer. Almost all skills count as combat attacks, so the class passive triggers nearly 100% of the time for strong ranged damage; main skill Light-Purge Round strips all TP and applies Suppress, reducing an enemy to only basic attacks. With only 4 archer characters (7 including gunners) a year after launch, ranged snipers hold high status, and Erica's rare Light element makes her especially valuable.
  • Since top burst dealer Scharnhorst and the setting's strongest Black Prince are both Dark element, as of early 2025 Erica is actively used as a strong counter alongside Iyolin, Joan, and Kana. The devs stated they released her because a Light-element ranged dealer was needed, and that design intent worked very well.
