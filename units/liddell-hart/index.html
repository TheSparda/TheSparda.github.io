Unique Passive

Legacy of the Wild

  • Crit rate increases by up to 25%, and defense and resistance increase by up to 15% of attack power.
  • Also holds the 'Combat Instinct' and 'Call of the Wild' effects, and at the start of that game gains 'Return of the Pack' at maximum stacks.
  • From 5th awakening, holds the 'Will of the Wild' effect.

Combat Instinct: An unremovable effect that grants the 'Combat Sense' effect when the following conditions are met:

  • Gain stacks equal to the number of enemies hit after attacking
  • Gain a stack after being attacked
  • Gain 2 stacks after attacking a large monster if the target survives

Combat Sense:

  • Per stack: attack power up to +6%, defense and resistance up to +3%
  • At end of turn, if you did not attack an enemy this turn, stacks decrease
  • Max 10 stacks. Unremovable.

Call of the Wild:

  • Immune to mind control effects and stun effects
  • Gain 'Return of the Pack' at max stacks at game start
  • At end of turn, if there is no 'Wolf Kal', gain 1 stack of 'Return of the Pack'
  • Return of the Pack: At max stacks, if there is an adjacent empty tile, summons 'Wolf Kal' and is removed. This buff is not visible to enemies. Max 2 stacks. Unremovable.
  • If 'Wolf Kal' is outside a 4-tile range, you can use 'Return of the Wild' to un-summon it and re-summon it on an adjacent tile.

Will of the Wild: Gained from 5th awakening. Max HP +10%, fire/frost/lightning resistance +15%, cannot stack with other Authorities. Unremovable. Authority attribute.

Classes

All of Liddell Hart's classes generate a control zone that provides cover on adjacent tiles.

Juggernaut Lancer

Class that gains the main exclusive skill 'Crushing Charge'.

  • Unconditional stat boosts up to +50% attack power and +20% damage reduction
  • Physical/magic penetration resistance +10% each (base)
  • After using a movement-attack skill:
  • Gain 'Indomitable Endurance' at max stacks
  • 'Indomitable Endurance': damage reduction +10%, defense +10%, resistance +10%. Max 3 stacks. Duration 2 turns. Unremovable.
  • That skill's cooldown reduced by 1
  • Removes movement-control attribute debuffs

War Brute

Class that gains the exclusive skill 'Flesh Rending'.

  • Attack power and bleed damage increase unconditionally by up to 30%
  • Damage taken reduced by 15%
  • Gains 'Blood Iron Wall' under the following conditions:
  • Blood Iron Wall: Damage taken -10%. After being attacked, recover HP equal to 25% of attack power, then stacks decrease. Max 3 stacks. Lasts 2 turns.
  • Gain conditions:
  • At game start: 3 stacks
  • At turn start, or when another character within 4 tiles dies: 1 stack

Lanceworden

Class that gains 'Ground Smash'; has the Guard-Lanceworden exclusive tree.

  • Gains attack power unconditionally, and applies attack-down to enemies within a surrounding 3-tile range (passive).

Wolf 'Kal' - Summon

The wolf summon that appears alongside Liddell Hart via the passive. Acts after Liddell Hart finishes acting. Freely ignores control zones and traps when moving.

Summon stats: A summon with 70% of the summoner's base stats (excluding spell power), the summoner's physical penetration, and 100% of the summoner's crit rate.

Passive 'Wild Instinct':

  • Damage taken reduced 20%
  • Cannot be manually controlled
  • Damage taken reduced 10%
  • When it takes lethal damage, it is not killed but un-summoned instead
  • Shares 15% of damage with the summoner within 4 tiles
  • On attack, applies 'Weakness Exposure' to the target

Weakness Exposure:

  • Damage taken +10%. Chance of being crit +10%
  • Does not stack with other 'Exposure' attribute effects
  • Max 3 stacks. Duration 2 turns
  • Exposure attribute
  • Removed when the summoner dies

Wolf Kal's skills:

  • Bite: TP cost 1. 130% of attack power as damage. Applies 'Bond Seal' effect. Cooldown 2 turns.
  • Bond Seal: When hit by a physical-damage attack from a playable character or 'Wolf Kal', gain stacks. At max stacks, grants 'Bond Vigor' to Liddell Hart and resets stacks. Cannot stack with 'Resonance Seal' effect.
  • Bond Vigor: Grants an extra action before end of turn and reduces stacks. Duration infinite. Unremovable.
  • Wolf Howl: TP cost 2. Removes 2 debuffs from allies within a surrounding 3-tile range and grants the 'Fighting Spirit' effect. Cooldown 3 turns.
  • Fighting Spirit: Attack power and spell power +20%. Duration 2 turns.
  • Wild Dash: TP cost 1. Immediately after using the skill, gain an extra action. During that turn, crit rate +15%. Cooldown 2 turns.

Ultimate

Howling Hellebarde

Ultimate

A single-target skill that designates 1 enemy, deals 170%~200% of attack power as damage, applies the 'Bleed' and 'Brand of Slaughter' effects, and re-summons Wolf Kal.

  • Bleed: At end of turn, deals defense-ignoring damage equal to 30% of the caster's attack power. If hit by a slash- or pierce-attribute critical attack, an excessive-bleed effect triggers dealing defense-ignoring damage equal to 30% of attack power. Damage-over-time, bleed attribute. Duration 2 turns.
  • Brand of Slaughter: Damage-over-time taken increased by 50%. Unremovable. Duration 3 turns.

Guide

Note: NamuWiki community assessment (KR server), subjective community opinion. No explicit S/A/B tier rating present.

  • A "deal-porter" (dealer + supporter) able to both deal and support, through strong bleed damage plus DoT combined with the "Roar of the Wild" support, and commanding a deal-porter summon that outclasses most low-rarity characters.
  • The wolf "Kal" is a powerful summon doing more than one hero's worth of work, and since it auto-resummons 2 turns after death, it can be used as a body/tank without burden.
  • In raids, combine DoT/tick-damage dealers such as Omega, Sharkbari, and Dycus, plus Medici (reborn as a Zephyr Falcon supporter), and push through the damage ceiling with DoT.
  • Caveat: it takes time to build up, since it needs to stack to perform its role.

KR Creator Guide

Character review of Liddell Hart by 하막군의게임튜브 · 2026-08-04

Liddell Hart is an attack-scaling tank, and that phrasing carries the whole problem: he wants ATK, but he wears heavy armour, where ATK is hard to find. The reviewer describes him as awkward for that reason and says players who own him don't rate him as overpowered.

Two things carry him anyway: the shout, which is enormous, and the wolf, which is genuinely useful. (22:35)

His other complaint is blunt — the AI won't cast the shout, so he is unusable on defence teams until that's fixed. (23:38)

Wild Roar is the reason to own him

The reviewer says he called Liddell Hart broken on the strength of this one skill alone, before seeing anything else.

It applies Savagery to every allied player on the map — no range limit at all — granting +20% ATK, Crit Rate, DEF and RES, Movement +1 for anyone at 3 Movement or less, and 1 TP back after attacking below 1 TP. It clears a debuff from all of them, removes mind control, and grants him an extra action afterwards.

At 2 TP on a 3-turn cooldown that's already good; the weapon cuts it to 1 TP on a 2-turn cooldown, so it goes out every other turn. (11:16)

Pair it with the Crown of the Fallen accessory and the shout clears two debuffs from the entire team. (18:30)

The wolf

The wolf inherits 70% of his base stats and 100% of his Physical Penetration, shares 15% of his incoming damage while within 4 tiles, and applies Weak Point Exposure on its attacks. You cannot control it — it wanders and acts on its own, attacking, buffing and clearing debuffs.

The detail that matters defensively: when the wolf takes lethal damage it is unsummoned rather than killed. So Cyrano Bernstein does not get his kill-triggered extra action from removing it. (4:05)

Re-summoning has a rhythm worth knowing. Pack Return needs 2 stacks; it starts at 2, so a wolf is out from turn one. When the wolf dies you gain 1 stack at end of turn, meaning a full turn passes before a replacement appears. If the wolf is more than 4 tiles away you can dismiss and re-summon it next to you at end of turn. (1:32)

Class: Juggernaut Lancer is the default

The reviewer expects most players on Juggernaut Lancer: +50% ATK — which matters most on an ATK-scaling tank — and -20% damage taken, plus Indomitable Endurance at max stacks after a move-attack for another 30%.

Stack that with the wolf's 15% share and he reports roughly 65% total damage reduction. (8:22)

War Brute raises ATK and bleed damage and opens with 3 stacks of Damage Bulwark (45% reduction), decaying as he is hit. Because bleed is a raid staple, he calls this the raid class. One quirk: the wolf being unsummoned doesn't count as a unit dying, so it doesn't restore a stack. (9:44)

Lance Warden+1 more → was quietly improved — it now grants Positional Guard at end of turn for a flat 20% damage reduction that wasn't there before. (10:45)

Gear

Heavy sets — he recommends the Vigor set, and passes on a report from a player with a full Immortal Conqueror set including its Juggernaut buff that Vigor still came out ahead.

Accessory: Crown of the Fallen, and it's close to mandatory. ATK, DEF and RES, the second debuff clear on the shout, and from ★3 a TP refund on move-attacks. (18:00)

The weapon is required at ★4

Not for its damage — for the shout. ★3 cuts the shout's TP cost; ★4 adds the cooldown reduction as well, which is what turns it into an every-other-turn effect. ★6 adds healing.

It also grants Reaper's Blade after casting a skill: since the shout grants an extra action, you cast it and then basic-attack with boosted damage and a heal. (19:30)

Runes

All Chaos is the standard; all Sun is a reasonable alternative given he wants ATK. (21:02)

Artifacts

Wind of Recovery+3 more → is the all-round pick — ATK, Physical Penetration, DEF and RES, plus damage reduction when Action-Controlled.

Horn of Valhalla is the buffer option: the shout grants +20% ATK to the entire team, since the shout itself has no range limit. Chalice of Blood+2 more → raises bleed damage and belongs in raids. (21:32)
