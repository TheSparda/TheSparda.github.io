Guide

The following is subjective NamuWiki community opinion (KR server). No explicit S/A/B tier rating is stated.

Performance: Base job is Fighter. Tier 2 has the exclusive class "Musa (Warrior)"; Tier 3 classes are Swordmaster / Highmaster / Mushin (War God). His ultimate is Annihilating Star Heaven Dance. The unique passive "Eastern Swift Star" grants +20% crit chance above a health threshold and pre-emptive strike (attacks first when attacked). At turn start it applies "Duel" to the strongest-attack enemy within 3 rings. That status does nothing by itself, but killing that enemy (revives are ok) grants an extra action and the "Culmination" buff, which stacks 3 to 6 times depending on awakening level. The buff is an attack buff plus attack-scaled health recovery, quite useful for Nangcheon since his crit boost is health-conditional.

Class: Among Tier 3, Swordmaster has high attack boost, Highmaster focuses on entering enemy formations, and Mushin combines mobility and attack when no debuff is present but is useless with a debuff. The exclusive Mushin follows the Musa-Mushin tree, which holds all exclusive-skill learning and best fits Nangcheon's style, so it is the most recommended; take it as first pick without hesitation. Its passive gives up to 90% chance to gain 1 TP on basic attack, and +1 movement / +40% attack when debuff-free. The condition is very simple ("no debuff"), but this game has many debuff-wielding enemies, so it needs careful management. Swordmaster is a shared class (from CW2) that learns "Fang Flash"; its passive gives attack/crit-chance/damage-reduction and self-heal but only in combat, and is weak to AoE. Fang Flash is a strong single-target skill (170% at +30% crit) that grants the "Gale" buff, synergizing well with Nangcheon; if Swordmaster is unlocked it is worth running over Shadowless Sword. Highmaster's passive ignores control zones and applies the "Mind's Eye" debuff (up to -25% atk/magic/def and +30% received crit chance) to 2 random enemies within 3 tiles at turn end; feels more Ranger than swordsman, awkward in large battles but useful in low-count raids. Its 100% + 2-turn heal-block is decent but not enough to replace Meteor Annihilating Flash.

Exclusive skills: from Musa is Nangcheon's core skill Meteor Annihilating Flash: 60% damage to enemies within 4 tiles forward, moving to the farthest empty tile; if a crit lands it spends 1 TP to cast again with no cap, so with the initial 2 TP cost it can trigger up to 3 extra times, theoretically up to 240% on the same enemy. from Mushin is another main skill Shadowless Sword: 150% single-target; on kill it sweeps the surrounding 8 tiles for 30%, and on non-kill it recovers 50% attack as health after combat, usable as self-heal, good for raids. Since the rival Fang Flash has a crit buff and higher multiplier, pick Fang Flash for more damage and Shadowless Sword to secure health. The ultimate Annihilating Star Heaven Dance is an AoE hitting enemies within 3 tiles around self, granting the Culmination buff on use and again on kill, and can serve as a way to gain Culmination.

Recommended Gear: Exclusive weapon is the Meteor Sword. This is fairly bland compared to the era when a meteor triggered on crit; despite synergy with the passive's Duel and Meteor Annihilating Flash, it is only a crit-damage boost and gives nothing on the stage's first attack or when no kill occurs, so Stormblinger is rated better for spec convenience and Durandal better for damage. For armor: at Hero grade the crit-providing Assassin set is recommended; at Legendary grade the Crow-themed "Raven" set (also crit) is standard. His own signature One Against a Thousand set lacks a crit option and is not well recommended. For accessories, since crit is so important, the Golden Dice (extra probability roll), together with Karls Brandt, is considered essentially required.

Evaluation: A pure crit-dependent dealer, living and dying by crits. At full health and debuff-free his crits land well and he hunts "Duel"-marked enemies with extra actions and Meteor Annihilating Flash volleys, making him a reaper against blue-attribute foes, but a single debuff wipes out his +1 movement and +40% attack, and low health removes 20% crit chance, halving his performance. Outside Highmaster he has no defensive tools, and most core mechanics trigger off crits, so the spec gap between having and lacking crit is large. Overall a fragile glass cannon needing careful management, which is why his auto-battle vs manual-battle power gap is said to be enormous. Early on he rewarded delicate handling, but that is hard and getting harder: past the 1-year service mark, unremovable debuffs like Iolyn's Revenge have increased, and by year 2 unremovable debuffs are everywhere with heavy power inflation. The remaining uses are PVE-only, or giving up Mushin to reclass into Highmaster as a Mind's Eye applier, or going Swordmaster for original-work recreation.

Awakening/Manifestation progression: The awakening/dupe progression. Each stage lists the "Manifestation" reward and the "Inner Cube" reward. - Stage 1: Ultimate Enhance 1 / Stat Enhance 1 - Stage 2: Unique Passive Enhance 1 / Stat Enhance 2 - Stage 3: Ultimate Enhance 2 / Artifact Acquired - Stage 4: Unique Passive Enhance 2 / Artifact Enhance 1 - Stage 5: Ultimate Enhance 3 / Artifact Enhance 2 - Stage 6: Unique Passive Enhance 3 / Artifact Enhance 3 (Numeric values behind the "Ultimate Enhance" and "Unique Passive Enhance" ranks appear in the ultimate/passive sections, which are extracted separately.)
