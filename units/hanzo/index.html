Signature Gear

Guide

  • Performance/Evaluation: For over a year after service launch he was the only legendary-grade assassin. A disruption-type character specialized in damage negation. If you try to attack, he's hidden and can't be hit; if you use an AoE to strip stealth, the passive makes damage land as 0; if you strip stealth and deal damage, his clone just wastes your turn. So survivability is excellent. Rather than dealing damage, the basic playstyle is to trust Dark Veil and Phantom Clone, throw him into the enemy lines, survive, and next turn spread the Black Shadow Slay Formation debuff while healing HP. In PVP he can be used to absorb Black Prince's Dark Fate Combo Blade or Asura Heaven-Splitting Dance to protect allies. Going Shadowkiller to leverage a high crit ceiling, he can also land no-counter 180% damage with Shin-yeon-sal-seom on Black-Shadow-Slay-marked enemies, but showing off damage potential in front of Sharn or Black Prince is a stretch.
  • After the rework, from 5th awakening Black Moon Mist was added granting extra actions, plus he gained an ultimate, making him stronger. But the extra-action condition (first take AoE damage to remove Dark Veil, then kill an enemy) is very demanding, so it will be quite hard to use.
  • The standard operation has some difficulty, so if you switch from Sakiski to Hanzo you may be flustered, but keep in mind he's a fundamentally different character.
  • Currently he shows top performance as a decoy/dummy against Black Prince. Thanks to Dark Veil he can survive Atomic Blast and Ultimate Grima, and the clone can absorb the Dark Fate Combo Blade.
  • Since Inner Break was introduced he has shown considerable performance: debuff immunity is now 100%, he gains good artifacts like Assassin's Mark, and reaching 100% physical penetration became easy too. Notably, this 100% debuff immunity means Hanzo no longer takes the Hero's Grave debuff.
  • Additionally, comboing with full-awakened Gishne: turn 1 use Whirling Eight Blades spending 3 TP, get 2 TP back from Gishne and gain Ultimate Inheritance 3, then next turn lay down Illusion Slay Formation to fill the field with traps.
  • Since Cyrano's release (Empire 7 Heroes' Last Bastion), his deployment frequency rose greatly. Thanks to Dark Veil held from game start, he takes no damage from turn-1 Cyrano AoE skills and can bait attacks; then using Phantom Clone he can also absorb Heidellune's attack. If he survives to turn 2 he can spread Black Shadow Slay Formation to block re-actions, making him a cost-effective character for clearing the "Si-Ha-Cheon-De" defense deck (Cyrano, Heidellune, Cheonma, Deimos).
  • No explicit S/A/B tier letter rating is stated.

Awakening2025-09-02

Expanded Inner Break awakening

  • New Inner Break awakening added for Hanzo.
  • 1/3/5-star awakening strengthens ultimate 'Phantom Slaughter Formation': at max, plants 12 phantom traps on random empty tiles within 3 tiles (invisible to enemies, last 2 turns), then grants 'Shadowless Slaughter' for 1 turn. Enemies that step on a trap take 110% ATK physical fixed damage and are stunned for 1 turn. Shadowless Slaughter (1 turn): Move +1, cannot be targeted, guaranteed crit on attack, and gain an extra action after attacking (then removed).

Each time Veil of Darkness decreases, gain 'Black Moon Mist'. At ally turn start, if HP is 50% or below, apply 'Black Shadow Formation' to enemies within a 2-tile radius for 2 turns and recover 50% of total HP.
