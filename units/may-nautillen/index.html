Unique Passive

Model of the Snow Country Passive

  • First obtained: Counterattack range +1, and on counterattack attack power +15%. If no adjacent ally, gains pre-emptive attack and enemies within 2 tiles around have their extra-hit damage and combo (assist) damage reduced by 20%.
  • Max Awakening: Counterattack range +1, on counterattack attack power +30%. If no adjacent ally, gains pre-emptive attack and enemies within 2 tiles have extra-hit and combo damage reduced by 50%.
  • Max Manifestation: Counterattack range +1, on counterattack attack power +50%. If no adjacent ally, gains pre-emptive attack and enemies within 2 tiles have extra-hit and combo damage reduced by 70%. When attacked by an enemy, gains Model of the Snow Country 3 effect for 2 turns.
  • Model of the Snow Country 3 cannot be removed: When attacking an enemy, attack power and physical penetration increase by 30%; after attacking, recover 1 TP and this effect is removed.

Signature Gear

Guide

Performance:

  • Among the classes, Decurion Valkyrie is the most usable. Since it raises combo damage and has healing, it is worth using in Training or Raid.
  • The skill Gale Fang Fang costs only 1 TP and, with a Whetstone, deals up to 190% damage, an excellent single-target skill, but its 3-turn cooldown is on the long side. In the end the most-used skill is Flying Void Flash Blade.
  • The ultimate Snow Moon Flower Ultimate Soul is a mobile-original ultimate, easy to use and applies Taunt too, an excellent area skill.

Exclusive Weapon and Recommended Gear:

  • Exclusive weapon is Celestial; additional stat is Attack. It increases counterattack damage, and heals HP based on attack power once per turn after a counterattack. When May equips it, counterattack damage and healing increase, and she gains the 'Counterstrike' buff (attack power up) after counterattacking. Since hero-grade and lower rapiers perform poorly in this game, obtaining the exclusive weapon is recommended.
  • For armor, the common Valkyrie recommendation Senior Scout can be used, but since May is closer to a dealer, giving her dealer armor like the Assassin set or Chivalrous is more recommended. At legendary grade, wearing the Red Wind set (motif of Alcyon's outfit) to boost Decurion Valkyrie's combo specialization is an option, and mixing in a set that gives attack up on Musin 2 for a max-damage set.
  • For accessories: to secure survival, use the Healing Censer along with Sara and Joan; to go as a dealer, the Whetstone is recommended to max out Gale Fang Fang.

Evaluation:

  • A Valkyrie with a strong offensive tendency. The ultimate is powerful, she can specialize in combo, and with a Whetstone equipped, Gale Fang Fang's damage is very usable.
  • No explicit S/A/B tier rating is present on this page.

Awakening/Manifestation progression: - Stage 1: Ultimate Enhance 1; Stat Enhance 1 - Stage 2: Unique Passive Enhance 1; Stat Enhance 2 - Stage 3: Ultimate Enhance 2; Artifact obtained - Stage 4: Unique Passive Enhance 2; Artifact Enhance 1 - Stage 5: Ultimate Enhance 3; Artifact Enhance 2 - Stage 6: Unique Passive Enhance 3; Artifact Enhance 3
