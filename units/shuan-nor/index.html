Unique Passive: Shadow of Death [Passive]

사이럽스의 용병. 명랑하고 시원시원한 성격이며 돈이 되는 일이라면 뭐든 받아들여 처리하는 편이다. 그녀가 돈에 집착하게 된 건 사실 동생들 때문이다. 전쟁 중에 잃어버렸던 동생을 구하기 위해 크로우가 의뢰하는 위험한 임무에도 기꺼이 뛰어든다.

Classes

Rogue (Tier 1)

Medium defense · Dealer · Weapons: Dual Daggers

A starter melee DPS who closes on enemies with Leap movement and +5~10% Crit to land dagger damage. Use Stealth (untargetable, +20% Crit, and re-Stealth plus extra Movement on defeating an enemy) to dive in safely, teleport to the back line with Shadow Flash, and finish enemies at 50% HP or below with the Chain Slash follow-up. Chaining defeats into the next Stealth to clear several enemies in one turn is the key to playing this unit.

(Chain Slash: (TP 2, Range 1-1, Area Single, CD 2 turns): Attack an enemy for 100% of ATK as damage; if the enemy's HP is 50% or below, a follow-up strike triggers for 80% of ATK as damage.)
