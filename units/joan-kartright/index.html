Guide

Note: The following is subjective NamuWiki community assessment (KR server).

  • Overall: "Sara 2.0." Tanking is slightly weakened, but she is considered a superior version of Sara in nearly every other respect. Since Sara was already a top-tier DPS-tank, Joan (her upgrade) is rated as having the best performance of all female characters, especially in 1:1. Burying/applying debuffs pulls its weight and eases the rest of the team's composition. Her Light attribute means no weaknesses, unlike Fire-attribute Sara who was weak to Blue attribute, making her more superior as a DPS-tank.
  • However, the power gained per enhancement stage is large, so she can feel weak at 3-star. At full enhancement she grows into a PvP-essential melee DPS; a "late bloomer" growth table, so invest heavily.
  • Passive: ATK increases unconditionally; gain 1 Special Judicial Authority stack at game start, max 3. Guard: dmg dealt +510%, preemptive strike, guard 1 ally within 2 tiles for 2 turns. Restraint: DEF/RES +520% for 2 turns, banish 1 enemy within 2 tiles. Investigation: crit chance +5~20% until turn end, gain extra action ("the flower of Joan's passive"). Feels like an updated version of Sara's passive; less tanky but superior due to extra action.
  • Class: Divine Valkyrie (defense-oriented, shared class, not recommended). Royal Valkyrie - recommended as the #1 priority raise and endgame class. Security Valkyrie - "isolated enemy" condition is unclear and rarely triggers, so class not recommended, but Crack Down is nearly broken-tier, making it the #2 raise priority.
  • Signature skills (all 3 considered core): Pasoehon (removes all enemy TP, 100%+(removed TP)x20% dmg ignoring cover; TP max 5 = up to 200% dmg; range 1-2; learned at Tier 2) - core damage skill, "broken." Bigong-seom-in (grants enemy preemptive strike, dodges their counter at reduced dmg, then hits for 100%+(lost HP ratio), gains 50% ATK shield; crit during cooldown reduces cooldown by 2). Crack Down - considered the most broken of her signature skills.
  • Ultimate Storm Fury Fang: single-target burst. ATK multiplier totals 225% at 3-star, 270% at 6-star. Cannot be countered; gains extra move after. Identical performance to Sara's Storm Fury Fang.
  • Signature weapon: Imperial Sylphid, empire-improved Sylphid. Added stats changed to Physical Penetration 10% + crit 10%. Weapon skill: dmg dealt to enemies at 5075% HP or below +1520%. Joan's exclusive effect: at 50~75% HP or below, gain shield equal to lost HP for 2 turns. Weapon acquisition strongly recommended since heroic-grade-or-lower rapiers perform poorly.
  • Recommended gear: Senior Scout set for HP/recovery (Sara-like DPS-tank), or Legendary Poongun set (G.S costume motif) which is similar but stronger and adds ATK buff. Accessory: Healing Censer standard for survivability; Blade of Balance is iffy since Joan out-damages Sara; heroic-grade Wound Aggravation Amulet also good for tanking.

Awakening/Manifestation progression: Progression via Inner Cube stages: - Stage 1: Ultimate enhancement 1, Stat enhancement 1 - Stage 2: Unique Passive enhancement 1, Stat enhancement 2 - Stage 3: Ultimate enhancement 2, Artifact acquired - Stage 4: Unique Passive enhancement 2, Artifact enhancement 1 - Stage 5: Ultimate enhancement 3, Artifact enhancement 2 - Stage 6: Unique Passive enhancement 3, Artifact enhancement 3 Passive scaling (Special Judicial Authority stacks, max 3): - First acquired: ATK +5%. Guard/Investigation/Restraint effects at 5%. - Max Awakening: ATK +20%. Start game with 1 Special Judicial Authority. Effects at 20%. - Max Manifestation: ATK +50%. Start with 1 stack; gain 1 at ally turn start; gain 1 after using Storm Fury Fang. At max stack: clear all own debuffs and gain 1 TP. Guard 7 gives +20% dmg dealt + preemptive strike; Restraint 7 gives DEF/RES +40%; Investigation 7 gives crit chance +40%. Ultimate scaling: - First: 60% dmg, ATK & crit chance +15% each per extra hit, 2 extra hits. - Max Awakening: 60% dmg, +30% each, 2 extra hits. - Max Manifestation: 80% dmg, +40% each, 2 extra hits.

Reworks

KR-server balance changes for this unit, newest first (see the full Rework History).

Awakening2025-05-27

Expanded Inner Break awakening

  • Added Inner Break awakening. Stages 1/3/5 enhance ultimate 'Wind Fang Rending Slash': at max, deals 80% ATK damage then 2 extra escalating hits (ATK and Crit Rate +40% each); cannot be guarded or countered.
  • Stages 2/4/6 enhance unique passive 'Strict Executioner': at max, ATK +50%; gain a Special Jurisdiction at game start, at ally turn start, and after using the ultimate.
  • Special Jurisdiction (3 stacks, permanent, cannot be dispelled): at max stacks on turn end, clear all own debuffs, gain 1 TP, and consume stacks to use Guard 7, Investigation 7, or Restraint 7.
  • Guard 7 (1 stack): guard a nearby ally within 2 tiles for 2 turns, +20% damage dealt, gain pre-emptive attack.
  • Restraint 7 (2 stacks): banish a nearby enemy within 2 tiles for 1 turn; grants +40% DEF and RES for 2 turns; cannot be guarded.
  • Investigation 7 (3 stacks): gain an extra action and +40% Crit Rate until turn end.
