Signature Gear

Guide

Note: The following is NamuWiki community assessment (KR server), subjective community opinion.

A character who, after stacking self-buffs 3 times to gain the "Cheongryong's Shuri" buff, gets greatly boosted stats and turns all skills into stun tools. Exclusive weapon is Dragon Buster.

Being an early-release character, he has no unique skill and his classes are not very special, but he has Sinsalchang, a powerful defense-ignoring damage skill, so there is plenty of room to run him as a "jukchang" (glass-cannon spear). Fortunately Sinsalchang is also an AoE hitting 2 targets, so it's a plus that it triggers the passive. His ultimate Gwangryongtan applies not Shock, nor Shock-Stun, but a "Stiffen" counter-blocking debuff as an AoE; it has the same effect as the Shock debuff but a different name only because the exclusive weapon Dragon Buster has a Shock-applying effect. It would be nice if Shock and Stiffen overlapping turned into Shock-Stun, but that is not the case (footnote: Shock-Stun stuns when stacked 2 times).

In this game, ordinary AoE skills have poor damage multipliers, so he is mediocre at low awakening; but from 5-awakening the passive is greatly enhanced so he gains self-buffs on every attack and extra when hitting 2+ enemies, and from 6-awakening the stun granted by the Cheongryong's Shuri buff becomes guaranteed. So depending on enemy placement he can gain Cheongryong's Shuri on turn 1 and then run wild, which is why 6-awakening-and-above Shuri is rated as very strong. If high awakening, give him a try.

Dragon Buster is a spear that applies the Shock debuff by chance. Its bonus stat is attack. When Shuri wields it, Shock chance rises and he gains an attack-based shield for 2 turns by chance. The Shock debuff's effect is only counter-block, and even at 6-awakening it's just 40%, so it's not worth awakening (the weapon). The shield acquisition, at least, becomes 100%.

No explicit S/A/B tier rating is present.
