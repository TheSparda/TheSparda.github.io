Unique Passive: Sword Princess of the Gale [Passive]

  • Rivalry 1: Cannot counterattack. Normal AoE skills cost +1 TP. When the caster attacks, Crit Rate and Crit Damage -20%. When attacked by the caster, Crit Rate received and Crit Damage received +20%.
  • Sword Princess of the Gale 1: Movement +1. Preemptive Attack. Counter damage +50%. When attacking an enemy with the Rivalry effect, gain an additional action once per turn. When you deal lethal damage to an enemy with the Rivalry effect, gain an additional turn and recover HP equal to 20% of Max HP.
