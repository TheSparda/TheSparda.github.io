Unique Passive

Wandering Princess Passive

  • First Acquisition: For each 1 tile moved this turn, attack power increases by 2%, and if there are no other allies within 2 tiles around, critical hit chance increases by 10%.
  • Max Awakening: For each 1 tile moved this turn, attack power increases by 5%, and if there are no other allies within 2 tiles around, critical hit chance increases by 20%.

Classes

Tier list:

  • Tier 1: Pike, Archer
  • Tier 2: Lancer, Marksman
  • Tier 3: Royal Lancer, Sniper

Pike Tier 1: A basic class that performs melee combat wielding a spear, which has a range advantage over other melee weapons.

Vigilance

LvEffect
1If you attack enemies and deal damage to 2 or more enemies at the same time, you gain the Vigilance 1 effect until the start of the next turn. Vigilance 1: Attack power +5%. At the end of the character's turn, recover HP equal to 5% of total HP.
2If you attack enemies and deal damage to 2 or more enemies at the same time, you gain the Vigilance 2 effect until the start of the next turn. Vigilance 2: Attack power +10%. At the end of the character's turn, recover HP equal to 10% of total HP.

Archer Tier 1: A basic class that uses a bow or crossbow as a weapon to deal physical damage to enemies at range.

Archery

LvEffect
1When making a combat attack without moving, critical hit chance increases by 5%.
2When making a combat attack without moving, critical hit chance increases by 10%.

Lancer Tier 2: A vanguard of the battlefield that wields a spear to charge enemies and deal destructive, powerful physical damage.

Vanguard

LvEffect
1If HP is 100%, movement +1. When making a combat attack, if you have moved this turn, attack power and critical hit chance increase by 15%.
1If HP is 100%, movement +1. When making a combat attack, if you have moved this turn, attack power and critical hit chance increase by 20%.

Marksman Tier 2: A sharpshooter skilled with bows or crossbows. Rapidly fires many arrows to deal critical wounds to enemies.

Accuracy

LvEffect
1When attacking an enemy, defense penetration rate +20% to deal damage, and if you defeat the enemy after attacking, critical hit chance increases by +20% for 2 turns.
2When attacking an enemy, defense penetration rate +25% to deal damage, and if you defeat the enemy after attacking, critical hit chance increases by +25% for 2 turns.

Royal Lancer Tier 3: An advanced knight class that wields a spear to charge enemies and deal destructive, powerful physical damage.

Crushing Advance

LvEffect
1If HP is 100%, movement +2. When making a combat attack, if you have moved this turn, attack power and critical hit chance increase by 25% and remove 1 buff from the target.
2If HP is 100%, movement +2. When making a combat attack, if you have moved this turn, attack power and critical hit chance increase by 25% and remove 2 buffs from the target.

Sniper Tier 3: An excellent sharpshooter who uses a bow or crossbow to precisely eliminate enemies from range.

Isolated Target

LvEffect
1When attacking an enemy, defense penetration rate increases by 30% to deal damage. If the enemy is isolated, critical hit chance increases by 30% and critical hit damage increases by 10%. Isolated: A state with no other allies on adjacent tiles.
2When attacking an enemy, defense penetration rate increases by 40% to deal damage. If the enemy is isolated, critical hit chance increases by 30% and critical hit damage increases by 15%.

Guide

NamuWiki community assessment (KR server), subjective community opinion:

  • Overall she got a good class and skills, and an easy-to-use character passive, but the attack-power increase from movement is not high, so she is disappointing even for use as a glass cannon.
  • Compared to some Hero-grade units that can somehow find a use even without Awakening or Inner Break, she is at a level where there is basically no way to use her.
  • Like Iyolin, she is in the position of a princess of a fallen nation, but since even in the original she had almost no story weight, there is a high chance she will not get a chance for revival either.
