Guide

Class (8.1):

  • Shared classes are Arc Knight and Dark Knight; unique class is Dragon Slayer. Mainly used are Dragon Slayer (raises crit rate, enables a one-shot ultimate) and Arc Knight (can block enemy class passives).
  • In PVE, Dragon Slayer is used to leverage the high crit-damage ceiling; in the 2025 PVP environment where physical penetration became meta and crits fell out of favor, Arc Knight is mainly used.
  • Aside: narrative director Choi Yeon-gyu reportedly requested buffs to the Dragon Slayer class from the dev team quite a bit, but it did not pass.

Core Skills and Ultimate (8.2):

  • Main skill is the single-target Yeolsalseom from Dragon Slayer. Base 110% of Attack, consuming all TP but dealing +25% extra damage per 1 TP lost; crit rate rises in proportion to lost HP; on crit, a 2-turn +20% Attack/Magic buff applies and it teleports within 4 tiles nearby 1. High single-target power plus mobility, a broken-tier skill. Synergizes well with Karls, who can restore TP via revival and tends to stack crit rate. Because of this skill, crit rate is Karls' core stat, which is why the Dragon Slayer (which grants a flat 20% crit rate) is recommended. Since consuming all TP is the condition, aside from cooldown it can even be cast at 0 TP. So giving him the TP1 skill Yeonseom means he never needs a basic attack. Adding "Dash" to supplement mobility and crit rate completes the basic setup.
  • Notably, Karls is a Fighter but has no Swordsman advancement, so he cannot learn Danwolseom. His only ranged (2+ tile) attack is Dark Embrace. Leave ranged combat to others and close in with Dash and Yeolsalseom warp. He has Knight instead of Swordsman so he can learn Vigor Shout, a very good buff, but it costs 4 TP and Karls is a dealer, so it is not much recommended.
  • The ultimate Heaven Earth Crusher hits AoE in a cross shape (surrounding 8 tiles plus 6 tiles front/back/left/right) centered on himself: first 70% of Attack damage, then an additional 70% of Attack as defense-ignoring damage, a defense-penetrating ultimate. No extra effects, but high multiplier, wide range, and defense penetration make it quite powerful.
  • At Max Manifestation it totals 200% damage; with Dragon Slayer, ultimate damage is +50% when HP is 50% or below, giving it comeback power on par with the Black Prince's Asura Pacheonmu.

Recommended Gear (8.3):

  • Signature weapon is Meolsaljiokgeom, a weapon that shares history with the Genesis War series. Bonus stat is Attack%. It grants crit rate whenever HP is not 100%, and on crit deals Attack-scaled damage to enemies within 2 tiles. Also applies a "Rupture" debuff dealing 5% of Attack physical damage per tile moved for 2 turns. Good synergy with the crit-focused Karls.
  • For armor, the Crow set is most recommended; if unavailable, the Assassin set is standard. A high-manifestation Karls who can equip artifacts can use "Hellkite's Gaze" (which raises HP/crit rate/crit damage), so the Crow set is not mandatory. But since Attack drops 10%, a Ilgidangcheon set or a Hyeopgaek 2 + Salsu 2 set is needed to shore up Attack.
  • Users building physical penetration sometimes drop Meolsaljiokgeom and use Imperial Armada plus the Ilgi set. Karls has high base Attack, so hitting 100% physical penetration can delete most opponents in one hit.
  • Note the Karls-themed "Bloodstained Reaper" set is somehow Heavy type rather than Medium, so Karls himself cannot wear it.
  • For accessories, since crit is so important, the Golden Dice is essentially required. A high-manifestation Karls who can supplement crit rate via artifact can also run the Sawtooth Arrowhead as main accessory. The Goyeongban is also viable.
  • Artifact: in practice, Hellkite's Gaze is used as a fixed choice.

Overall Assessment (8.4):

  • An excellent character with a strong nuke, AoE, high-performance revival, and good mobility, with simple setup so even beginners can use him easily. Popping a crit with Yeolsalseom lets him warp for repositioning, so mobility is barely a weakness. With a revive and a 0-TP-cost skill, he is brainless to pilot; revival comes with a 3-part combo of skill cooldown reset + AoE damage + TP recovery, enabling tactics like warping into the middle of the enemy with Yeolsalseom then, after reviving, scorching the map with Heaven Earth Crusher. A high-manifestation Karls is a guaranteed-crit character, so even basic attacks deal skill-level damage, bomb-drop-level effect.
  • For high-manifestation Karls, from the 4th manifestation the passive gains HP boost, making him fairly tanky; via artifacts he can raise melee Attack (Warrior's Essence) or equip the effectively-signature Hellkite's Gaze, paying 10% Attack to secure essential crit rate, crit damage, and HP, greatly easing the difficulty of crit specialization. At Max Manifestation there is a 25% HP increase, so he can run as a tanky dealer with HP gear or switch to full burst, and runes no longer must be crit runes, giving flexible builds. In short, beginner-friendly yet high-ceiling with broad investment options.
  • Cautions: to gain the "Star of Transcendence" buff you must field 1 ally with higher Attack than Karls, but fielding the Black Prince, or a well-raised Scharnhorst / young Lacid, etc., makes this easy; even a well-raised Sakiseuki works. Also, though the revive is strong, it does not clear debuffs, so if stunned you can revive and die again doing nothing. Also crit (especially crit rate) focused build is essential, but the only relevant trait class is Dragon Slayer, so stat-tuning is tricky. High-manifestation Karls becomes tanky, revive gains debuff removal, and crit setup can be solved via artifact, so if you main Karls, gather shards to secure high awakening + high manifestation. If all that is too much bother, money solves it.
  • In PVP he was usable due to the revive, but his lord the Black Prince arrived with Ammyeongyeonsalgeom, a skill that ignores revival and removes the target, so pulling Karls out against the Black Prince is a bit lacking. (Original-lore-accurate.) Still, combining high manifestation with HP-raising armor like Pungun leaves room to endure Death Lord Black Prince's Ammyeongyeonsalgeom.
  • Footnote 2: with Myeonggye 2-set + Pungun 2-set, HP rune work and gear work, over 9,000 HP is possible at 6th manifestation. Except against Sword Emperor Black Prince, he won't die in one hit, and revives at 80% HP, so he must be killed twice.
