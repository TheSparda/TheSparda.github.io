Guide

Class Doom Breaker is the payoff class — Calamity Devourer punishes debuffed enemies with big damage amps and buff-strip lifesteal, while Haste chains extra actions behind a Floating Cloud shield. Dark Knight is the AoE-Weaken alternative.

Skills Doom Break ramps every turn into Final Notice 7 — a 60% damage / AoE / Ultimate amp with heavy TP and cooldown refunds. Requiem's aura shreds enemy penetration and hands him a penetration spike under 50% HP.

Equipment Requiem (exclusive) for the penetration and comeback TP; Fade's Deadly Poison + Meticulous Preparation feed the debuff-punish loop, Ring of Purification covers control immunity.

Overall Assessment A scaling bruiser that snowballs on kills: escalating damage amps, buff-strip sustain, deflection shielding, and strong debuff-punish synergy.

Character review of Battle God Zephyr by 하막군의게임튜브 · 2026-08-04

Rebalanced for Global. Battle God Zephyr’s kit was changed between the Korean build in this video and the Global release, so some numbers and matchup calls below may not match what you see in game. The skill, class and gear tables higher up this page are the ones to trust.

Battle God Zephyr is the giveaway collab — 3 manifestations handed out free — and the reviewer's advice is simply to play him, because he is more fun than the free-unit label suggests. The pattern is: stack the passive, then fire the ultimate every turn, picking off one target at a time. (18:06)

Two things he is firm about. The exclusive weapon is bad and you should not pull for it. And several of his current strengths look like bugs that will be patched, so expect him to get quietly weaker. (16:34)

War God Reincarnation is the whole kit

At end of action he gains a stack of War God Reincarnation, adding damage dealt — which is a better multiplier than ATK. At 5 stacks it converts to Final Ultimatum: more damage dealt, damage reduction, and at the start of an ally turn full TP plus a 5-turn cooldown reduction on the longest skill.

Since the longest cooldown is the ultimate, that means the ultimate comes back every single turn. (1:31)

Because stacks come from ending an action rather than attacking, the way to build them fast is to chain skills that grant extra actions: cast Strong Luck from Divine Privilege+1 more → for its 90% cooldown reduction, then Haste for the extra action, which brings Divine Privilege back for Wall of Iron — four stacks banked before you have really done anything. (9:45)

The duration is what the free manifestations buy you. At 1 manifestation War God Reincarnation lasts a single turn, which he says is close to unplayable. At 3 it lasts two turns; at full manifestation, three. Everything else is numbers. (3:32)

Class: Vindicator

Vindicator+1 more → is the same class as on Dragon Slayer Zephyr, with the same finding: the -20% from Forced Salvation and the once-per-turn -40% stack on the same hit for 60% reduction, on top of cutting the attacker's ATK/M-ATK by 40%. Take one hit and the next turn heals him for 80% of ATK. Since his plan is to survive, stack, and then burst, this is the default. (4:34)

Doom Breaker he rates poorly — +50% damage taken on debuffed enemies is fine, but the ATK bonus and damage reduction only arrive after you kill a debuffed target, which is too late to matter. (5:38)

Gear, and why not his own weapon

Envoy of the Storm with Scorpion of the Underworld and Goddess's Blessing — the accessory is non-negotiable, since it returns TP once per turn.

For the weapon, skip his own and take Victoria Armada (Roberto de Medici's): its buff raises the wearer's single-target skill damage, and nearly everything Battle God Zephyr casts is single-target. Young Sect Leader Cheon's weapon is the alternative if you want its TP recovery instead of the accessory. (11:18)

Armour-wise the best fit is the Free Warrior or Spirit set; One Against a Thousand is pointless given how little penetration matters. (12:20)

His own weapon is the problem

Its payload, Time After Ruin, only unlocks below 50% HP on a 4-turn cooldown. And the reviewer's own build means that almost never happens — between Vindicator and Wall of Iron he describes the character as absurdly durable, so the condition simply doesn't fire.

Worse, the TP recovery it grants is redundant: Final Ultimatum already refills him every turn. His summary is that the weapon contradicts the character. Take the +15% Max HP if you must, but Victoria Armada is better. (13:53)

Runes

Not the usual sword layout. Strong Luck already reduces incoming Crit chance by 90%, so Crit-damage-taken runes do nothing. Go Chaos/Ultimate/Conspiracy and build health — his whole game is surviving to reach 5 stacks. (14:55)

Artifacts: Ring of Purification over Fade's Deadly Poison

The same three as Dragon Slayer Zephyr, but the ranking flips.

Fade's Deadly Poison+1 more → is excellent on Dragon Slayer Zephyr because Dragon Breath applies it to a whole group at once. Battle God Zephyr has no such tool — every skill he casts is single-target bar the ultimate — so it lands on one enemy at a time.

Ring of Purification+1 more → is the pick instead. He notes, with the same caveat as above, that it is currently better than written: it should be removed when hit by a damaging Action Control or Skill Control skill, and in practice most such skills don't remove it. Another one to expect a patch for. (15:59, 16:31)

One oddity worth knowing

His stat line carries an area-skill damage bonus that applies to essentially nothing — Dark Embrace is the only area skill he has, and nobody runs it. What matters is that damage dealt and ultimate damage stack multiplicatively, which is why the ultimate hits so hard. (18:36)
