Unique Passive: King of the Desert [Passive]

제국 칠용사의 일원이자 트리시스를 다스리는 대족장. 산전수전 다 겪은 노장인 만큼 깊은 통찰력에 이기기 위해서라면 자존심을 버리고 교활한 수단도 쓸 수 있을 만큼 약삭빠른 면이 있다.

Classes

Rogue (Tier 1)

Medium defense · Dealer · Weapons: Dual Daggers

A beginner melee dealer who leaps to enemies and lands dagger damage with +5~10% Crit Rate. He dives in safely via Stealth (cannot be targeted, +20% Crit Rate, and re-enters Stealth with extra Movement when he defeats an enemy), teleporting to the backline with Shadow Flash and finishing off enemies at 50% HP or below with Combo Slash's follow-up strike. The core of playing him is whether defeats chain into the next Stealth, letting him clear multiple enemies in a single turn.
