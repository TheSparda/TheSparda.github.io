Guide

Note: The following is NamuWiki community assessment (KR server), subjective community opinion, translated faithfully.

One of the best guard/escort tanks in the game. From passive to active skills, he has everything an escort tank should have, and being Light-attribute he is not affected by attribute matchups. Even as of '25 he is a top-tier escort tank, but as damage inflation accelerates, the tank class's standing is becoming increasingly precarious except for Isabel Ripine and his younger sister (Dunamis).

In November '25, Inner Break finally arrived, giving him a lifeline. With 2-tile escort possible at max Awakening, he can at least save face, but his lack of debuff resistance leaves something to be desired as a pure tank. His counterattack damage is strong but this too is only "saving face," since by the game's nature 1-tile counters rarely shine in either PVP or PVE. Unlike Isabel or Dunamis, he has no skill to reduce allies' AoE damage taken, so it's roughly a case of "escort and counter are at least possible."

So what sets Durant Rembrandt apart from other tanks? Surprisingly, it's damage. Compared to all tanks existing as of January '26, namely Isabel, Dunamis, and Siren, Durant is the only character who, when granted Vitality, can deal defense-based fixed AoE damage twice in one turn with 3TP. Because the Flash Combo Secret Manual grants the Focus effect when using a melee AoE, even without landing 2 AoEs, one AoE + Charge Strike can deal considerable damage. Since using Durant naturally leads to using Holy King Lacid, it can be called the ultimate combo.

The main classes used are Fortress and Centurion: Centurion for counterattack damage, Fortress for defense-based fixed AoE damage. Unless Crusader gets buffed in the future, consider Crusader as never worth using.

Main skills: as an AoE dealer, Twin-Impact Wave and Void Rend Wave. As a single-target skill, Charge Strike is usable, and Sun Light, reduced to 4TP, is worth considering as a support skill. When granted Holy King Lacid's Vitality, first use Twin-Impact Wave on turn 1 to set position, then use Void Rend Wave or Charge Strike; with the Centurion class, a tactic of throwing him in the center and pulling aggro for 1 turn with Taunting Shout is also worth trying. All these tactics presuppose the Flash Combo Secret Manual, but in that case his performance as a tank is compromised, so operate with caution.

Meanwhile, Wind of Recovery requires being hit by an action-control effect twice (2 procs) before the debuff is removed, so against skills like Banishment he cannot properly perform his tank role. Unless deploying alongside Dunamis or Siren, one must account for some deficiency in his tanking ability. Peak Condition endures powerful single-target attacks and, combined with Durant's character passive, shows high self-heal ability, so it is worth including as an option depending on the situation.

Awakening2025-11-25

Expanded Inner Break awakening

  • Added Inner Break awakening (can awaken without Raznite since it has no ultimate); existing class and skill balance also adjusted.
  • Fortress class passive 'Iron Wall 1': +40% DEF/RES, +10% Physical/Magic Penetration resistance, immune to movement-hindering effects, adjacent tiles become control zones granting cover to allies.
