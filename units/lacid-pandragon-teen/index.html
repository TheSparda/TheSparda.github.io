Guide

Note: the following is NamuWiki community assessment (KR server), subjective community opinion.

Performance: Base class is Fighter. Tier 3 classes are Sword Saint / Dragon Knight / Command Knight, all new classes at his release, showing the devs clearly focused on this character. Ult is the traditional Snow Flower Chaotic Shadow Slash. Unique passive "Kingdom's Future": attack power just rises unconditionally (remarkable on its own); when buffed by an ally's active skill he gains the "Resolve" buff (skill TP cost -1) and shields the buffing ally. The flat attack scales with awakening level, and from 5th awakening the longest remaining skill cooldown is also reduced by 1. Caveat: the trigger is "buff", but shields are NOT treated as buffs, so shields from Esmeralda's exclusive weapon or Nina's Warrant Barrier will not trigger it.

  • Sword Saint: learns Sword Wind Flash; passive raises attack on "combat attack", takes less counter damage, and grants "Momentum" (+move, +attack on combat attack). High attack boost, usually the #1 pick. But "combat attack" excludes AoE attacks, so it does NOT apply to Xavier's Breath or the ult Snow Flower Chaotic Shadow Slash; a drawback for AoE-focused play.
  • Dragon Knight: learns Xavier's Breath; grants "Ice Dragon's Protection" AoE-damage buff to allies within 3 tiles, plus on "combat attack" deals +20% attack as extra cold magic damage with a chance of Cold Slow. Built for AoE-skill play; big plus that the buff also applies to allies.
  • Command Knight: shout-skill passive removes 1 extra debuff from target allies, grants self attack-up and damage-taken-down, and gives extra attack/skill chances; learns Battle Shout: attack +25%, defense/resistance +20%. Lacid also learns Vitality Shout from Knight, so he has the makings of a dual-buffer. But his dealer credentials are so strong that buffer use lags; since the Black Prince's release, even for buffer use players pick Dragon Knight (for AoE buff) rather than Command Knight.

Recommended Gear: Exclusive weapon is Awake Barisada. Unlike Crow's debuff-focused Barisada, its weapon skill increases damage and crit rate when Lacid himself has a buff, reduces damage taken "on combat attack", and applies Cold Slow to the enemy at 30% chance after combat attack. The buff-conditional damage+crit is good, but Cold Slow is single-target only, so it does not apply to Xavier's Breath or Snow Flower Chaotic Shadow Slash.

  • Armor: since he invests in attack itself rather than crit, for Dragon Knight AoE dealing the Legend2 + Hero2 combo (Hanzo outfit motif, attack up) is recommended; otherwise the One-Man-Army set (Nangcheon outfit motif, combat attack-up + physical penetration).
  • Accessories: for single-target (Sword Saint), the Hero-grade fully-awakened Wolf Fang or Balanced Blade. The Legend Wolf Fang accessory Marksman's Magic Bullet is hard to fully awaken and gives resistance not HP, so it is mediocre. Hero-grade Wound Aggravation Charm is sometimes used but its heal-reduction does not apply to AoE/ranged skills.
  • All-purpose (single + AoE): Ancient Hero's Ring is best; its crit-damage reduction and heal-on-crit-hit greatly help the squishy Lacid. Legend-grade Sawtooth Arrowhead is usable but wasteful on him since his crit rate isn't high and crit dealers want it more.

Evaluation: Armed with a broken unique passive (unconditional attack up) and two strong exclusive skills, he boasts standout strength among Green attribute characters. In PVP his 1-turn Xavier's Breath (buff up, charge in, fire) dominated for a while. However, outside of Command Knight he has no survival/defense buffs and is somewhat squishy, and needs to receive a buff to enable the passive, so he isn't purely brainless to pilot. Also, the passive-enabling buff does not include shields (shields are not treated as buffs; Esmeralda's exclusive weapon or Nina's Warrant Barrier won't trigger it). In 2025 he became the first beneficiary of the Inner Break update and got stronger. At the same time the worldbuilding's strongest, the Black Prince, arrived, so unlike the past when he was mainly a dealer, a buffer build (Dragon Knight + Battle Shout + Vitality Shout to support a 1-turn Ashura Pacheonmu) is now worth considering. Either way, wide usefulness as dealer or buffer makes him high value to invest in; if you need a Green Legend, worth considering first. No explicit S/A/B tier letter is given.

Boy version: Base class Fighter; Tier 3 Royal Knight / Arc Knight / Crusader. No ult and no exclusive weapon (pre-Crow training). Unique passive "Radiant Star": if allies are within 2 tiles, attack and spell power rise, and attacking enemies restores own HP; if he pincers with an ally, that ally is also healed. Has Arc Knight (often used even by Legends) so raising him works at a basic level, but that's all; investing in him is not much recommended.

Team Synergy: From the ult note, Cold Slow upgrades to Freeze (1-turn action-lock) when stacked, so synergy is good with Cold-type siblings (Crow, Iyolin). Ironically Beradin, the main enemy of the Pandragon liberation struggle, is a Cold-magic specialist, so synergy with that side is also good.

Awakening/Manifestation progression: Two tracks per stage: Awakening and Inner Cube. - Stage 1: Ult+ 1 | Stat+ 1 - Stage 2: Unique Passive+ 1 | Stat+ 2 - Stage 3: Ult+ 2 | Artifact acquired: Peak Condition, Pincer Link, Xavier's Blessing - Stage 4: Unique Passive+ 2 | Artifact+ 1 - Stage 5: Ult+ 3 | Artifact+ 2 - Stage 6: Unique Passive+ 3 | Artifact+ 3
