Guide

NamuWiki community assessment (KR server). This is subjective community opinion.

Performance: Base class is Mage. Tier 2 has the unique class "Fatima"; Tier 3 classes are Archmage / Frostmage / Fatima Ultimate. Ultimate is "Parhelion Ray." Unique passive is "Ultimate One," starting the game with 2 stacks of the unremovable buff "Chaos Cube." Chaos Cube gains 1 stack each time you deal damage and loses 1 stack each time you take damage: max 3 stacks, +510% spell power per stack, 25% damage taken reduction per stack, and at 5-awakening removes all debuffs on self at character turn end. A passive that wants to hit without being hit; hard to maintain in this game's frequent melees, but with the exclusive weapon you can start a stage at 3 stacks, guaranteeing the first hit. At base card 3 stacks is only 15%, but at 6-awakening it rises to 30%, so awakening feels impactful. The 5-awakening debuff-clear is highly valued in this game where debuff-clearing skills have count limits, and ignoring even Silence is a very important point.

Classes: Tier 3 options are Archmage (TP steal), Frostmage (learns Moonlight, has a revive), and attack-specialized Fatima Ultimate.

  • Archmage learns Thunderbolt and has a passive that, after a "combat attack" leaving the enemy alive, steals 1 TP and grants +50% spell power for 1 turn. Thunderbolt is very useful and TP steal applies to basic attacks, so TP is relatively plentiful. But it does not apply to area skills, so it pairs poorly with area-focused Ophelia; sometimes used in raids where single-target setups are chosen instead of area skills.
  • Frostmage learns Moonlight; on death, once per stage, clears all buffs/debuffs on self then enters a 1-turn action-impossible invincible "Crystallization" state, and after 1 turn Crystallization ends and revives at up to 100% HP. An amazing revive passive, but it lacks a spell-power boost option so firepower is halved. Worth considering in PVP etc. where survivability must be prioritized over firepower.
  • Fatima Ultimate learns the exclusive "Ultimate Meteor" and follows the Fatima -> Fatima Ultimate exclusive tree. Its passive grants "Max Output" when ending the character turn after dealing damage or when gaining an active-skill buff from another ally; gaining a buff via active skill also extends that buff's duration by 1 turn. Max Output is a 2-turn buff granting up to +15% spell power on skill use, stacking to 3, so up to +45% spell power. It learns Prominence in Fatima and Meteor in Fatima Ultimate, and with the passive the spell-power ceiling is high, so it is recommended to prioritize and is a fitting endgame class.

Exclusive skills and Ultimate: Two exclusive skills: "Prominence" (from Fatima) and "Meteor Ultimate" (from Fatima Ultimate).

  • Prominence: costs TP 3. Deals 70% spell power damage to enemies within 3 tiles around self and applies Burn. Wide range and Burn for only TP 3, so quite usable. Cooldown 3 turns.
  • Meteor Ultimate: costs TP 4. Choose one of 3 meteors to drop on a target. Cooldown 3 turns. Flare Meteor: 110% fire damage to target and enemies within 3 tiles, 2-turn Burn. Glacial Meteor: 90% ice damage in same area, 2-turn Cold Slow. Jupiter Meteor: 110% lightning damage in same area, 2-turn Shock. Ophelia's core skill; matching the party's main attribute is the strength, and since Cold Slow becomes Freeze at 2 stacks, a good hit can turn a wide group into sandbags for 1 turn. Many ice-area users exist in this game but Ophelia is Dark attribute so she avoids attribute overlap, making her easy to slot into teams.
  • Ultimate Parhelion Ray: designates a target within 3 tiles for 110140% spell power damage, plus 4560% spell power to enemies within 3 tiles around the target (area). Damaged enemies get "Magic Vulnerable" (-50% resistance). Looking closely, it is an area skill but the off-target damage multiplier is even lower than Prominence and the extra effect is not a DoT type. In other words this ultimate is actually a single-target skill with area damage tacked on; the resistance-halving debuff makes it more support-oriented than damage on the area side.

Exclusive weapon and recommended gear: Exclusive weapon is "Sea of Stars," a Legendary staff whose extra stat is spell power. It has a weapon skill that increases skill damage and, when hitting 2+ enemies at once, has a chance to reduce that skill's cooldown by 1 turn. Weapon skill cooldown is 3 turns, dropping to 2 turns from 5-awakening. When Ophelia uses it, the damage bonus and cooldown-reduction chance are higher and she gains 1 extra Chaos Cube buff at stage start. Very useful since she can start a stage at 3 Chaos Cube stacks and her main skills are area with long cooldowns; but note the cooldown reduction is only guaranteed at 6-awakening.

  • Armor: use the Prime Minister set (Beradin outfit motif) to go all-in on spell power, the Great Sage set (Vishnu Man motif) to raise area damage, the Cardinal set (Van Dyke Cardinal motif) to secure TP, or the Pope set (Rubens Heisenberg motif) to secure cooldown. At Hero grade, give the Dark Mage set for spell-power stacking.
  • Accessories: at Hero grade, Conductive Crystal (good with area skills) or Necronomicon (raw spell power); at Legendary grade, the Stopwatch is a safe pick for cooldown reduction. In raids, Fire Crest is sometimes used to apply a damage-taken-up debuff on critical hits.

Evaluation: Area-magic specialist. Learns the easy-to-use fire area skill Prominence, the 3-attribute-choice Meteor Ultimate, and the strongest ice area skill Moonlight; with good control she can secure up to +75% spell power from buffs alone. Utility is limited to stunning via Cold Slow / Shock stacks, so she is focused on Meteor raw damage rather than fiddly utility, though thanks to the many ice-area users she can sometimes exploit Freeze depending on party composition. However, like these area-dealer mages typically are, her survivability is terrible, a textbook glass cannon with awful HP/defense growth. The Chaos Cube damage reduction helps somewhat, but taking damage also cuts her damage output, so losses compound. Proper use is manual control or attaching a tank. (No explicit S/A/B tier rating stated.)

Awakening/Manifestation progression: Two parallel tracks: Awakening and Inner Cube. - Stage 1: Ultimate Enhance 1 / Stat Enhance 1 - Stage 2: Unique Passive Enhance 1 / Stat Enhance 2 - Stage 3: Ultimate Enhance 2 / Artifact Acquired - Stage 4: Unique Passive Enhance 2 / Artifact Enhance 1 - Stage 5: Ultimate Enhance 3 / Artifact Enhance 2 - Stage 6: Unique Passive Enhance 3 / Artifact Enhance 3
