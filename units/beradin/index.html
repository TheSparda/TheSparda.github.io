Guide

This is subjective NamuWiki community opinion (KR server); no explicit S/A/B tier rating is present.

  • A specialist in ice/frost magic. He can use nearly every frost spell in the game up to the highest-tier spell, Moon Light. Ironically this gives him good synergy with the frost-swordplay-focused Pandragon royals: Iyolin, Lacid (youth), Crow, and Ice.
  • The exclusive weapon is not well regarded even after its rework: the buff randomness is too strong, and even with a high ceiling the "shield" condition is quietly annoying. On a Moon Light basis, the 3-awakening Archmage's Staff and 5-awakening Obsidian Staff (both common Legends) do similar damage to the base exclusive weapon, so the consensus is you don't need to roll the weapon-summon gacha. Worse, when a character other than Beradin holds it, the chance to gain a buff is only 15~30%, so its general utility is also poor.
  • To make use of the unique passive you need to be somewhat close to enemies, and since the exclusive weapon grants a spell-power boost while holding a shield, the recommended endgame class is Royal Wizard: it easily gets a shield just by applying an action-control effect and easily meets the spell-power-boost condition. Frost Mage can learn Moon Light and its passive is even a revive, but it has no spell-power-boost option so firepower is halved, so it's not much recommended. Usually you take Moon Light from Frost Mage and settle on Royal Wizard. Before the rework, since there was no ultimate, you had to grudgingly invest in Dark Wizard (which doesn't synergize with the unique passive) just to get Triple Elemental Blast, but after the rework it became unnecessary, so Dark Wizard is now the lowest priority.
  • A notable downside: when he gains an extra action, the defense buff evaporates. Even after working hard to land an AoE and earn an extra action, if the defense buff is gone and TP is also depleted, he must take enemy attacks bare-bodied. If there's an adjacent enemy he can push them with the passive to gain a defense buff, but the passive value maxes at 3 stacks so it's only up to 18%, and even in Royal Wizard state no shield is created. Pushing enemies can be seen as a kind of action-control effect, yet no shield is generated, which is a shame. Since there's no need to place a light-armor mage on the front line, treat defense buffs/shields as insurance only and aim for ordinary distance-keeping mage play. No need to place him up front just to stack Ring of Ruin via the passive. Even without doing so, since this game has short magic range and frequent melees, being a relatively defense-strong mage is a merit either way, insurance or not.
  • After the rework he's mainly used in Knight Order battles. A tactic of receiving Warrant Barrier, hiding for 1 turn, then launching Hex Elemental Blast on turn 2 and stepping up as a body-tank is considered quite effective. You can even give up spell-power boost entirely and deploy him as Frost Mage to use the turn-2 Hex Elemental Blast tank tactic: 1 turn from Immortal Rest, 1 turn before revive, 1 turn after revive, roughly 3 turns of tank duty.
  • From mid-2025, since Iskenderun of the Sea joined as the same blue attribute (providing shield and HP recovery, boosting magic damage, and giving -80% lightning resistance), an approach adopting lightning-type magic can now be considered. "If it's a recreation of Genesis 3 Part 2, is it a recreation?" - as a same-attribute, lightning-magic-holding melee spell-power dealer, he's the only character who can 100% utilize Iskenderun's supporting potential.

Awakening/Manifestation progression: - Stage 1: Ultimate enhancement 1; Stat enhancement 1. - Stage 2: Unique passive enhancement 1; Stat enhancement 2. - Stage 3: Ultimate enhancement 2; Artifacts acquired: Simple Protection Amulet, Meticulous Preparation, Flickering Orb. - Stage 4: Unique passive enhancement 2; Artifact enhancement 1. - Stage 5: Ultimate enhancement 3; Artifact enhancement 2. - Stage 6: Unique passive enhancement 3; Artifact enhancement 3. Note: Passive at Max Awakening becomes Ring of Ruin 7 / +45% spell-power AoE damage, plus a shield equal to 25% of spell power per damaged enemy for 1 turn; stack 6 grants immediate extra action, on trigger recovers 2 TP and clears. Ultimate at Max Awakening hits 150% spell power (Max Break: 165%).

Reworks

KR-server balance changes for this unit, newest first (see the full Rework History).

Awakening2025-02-18

Expanded Inner Break awakening

  • Inner Break 1/3/5 empowers ultimate Hex Elemental Blast: at max, deals 165% M-ATK to enemies within 3 tiles and applies chill slow, shock, burn plus a random debuff (2 turns); heals allies in range 100% M-ATK with a random buff; grants Immortal Rest for 1 turn.
  • Immortal Rest: HP cannot drop below 1, cannot cast skills or counter, cannot be dispelled; on expiry all normal skill cooldowns -1 and gain 1 TP.
  • Inner Break 2/4/6 empowers unique passive Ring of Ruin: gain Ring of Ruin 7 per enemy damaged; once per turn at end of turn push adjacent enemy 1 tile and deal 45% M-ATK to enemies within 2 tiles, gaining a shield worth 25% M-ATK per enemy hit (1 turn).
  • Ring of Ruin 7: DEF/RES +12%; at 6 stacks grants an instant extra action (cannot be dispelled), recovering 2 TP and removing the effect on trigger.
  • Inner Break 3 instantly equips artifacts (4/5/6 enhance): Basic Protection Charm grants a shield worth 40% to 100% M-ATK at game start.
