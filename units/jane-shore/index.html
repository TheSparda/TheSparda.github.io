Signature Gear

Guide

  • Overall: A dark-attribute raid-only Gunner mainly for whales or borderline-recruits. The passive's male-only condition is partly lifted at 5 awakenings, and the weapon is openly recommended at high awakening, so she is treated as a whale character. Marketed as usable as a sniper, but the bizarre spec of only being able to attack in a straight line (a "fixed turret") makes her hard to use. Her male-only passive drew attention for PVP use, but she is not exceptionally strong, so she struggles to meaningfully dent the PvP tier-1 comps and was quickly buried. Design intent points to inserting her into raids needing dark-attribute ranged damage, so the debuff-important Cerberus dark-attribute raid is her only real job, though she isn't especially strong even there. At the time of Jane's update, the tier-1 comp had no de-facto fixed female character except Esmeralda, and even Cerberus is treated as genderless.
  • A critical bug where runes set on characters vanished right after Jane's update prevented normal team setups and blocked further research. Only the discovery of "mobile Ascending" via combo with Silver (released Feb 2025) came of it.
  • Skill build differs by role: as a tactical-round debuffer, take Specialist with Tactical Round Load and Double Down, plus Bullseye if possible; as a sniper, take Widowmaker with Ascending Kiss, Tactical Maneuver, Armor-Piercing Load.
  • Class notes: Specialist is nearly a straight upgrade over Ace (loses 5% crit rate but gains ATK buff and a cover-state buff, a strong return for 5% crit). Widowmaker is the sniper class enabling Dark Round with attached debuff.
  • Ultimate Reaper's Magic Bullet: single-target dealing 130~160% ATK plus 3% max-HP extra damage per enemy debuff (HP-based capped at 100% ATK), no counter, no bullet cost; passive can cut its TP cost to as low as 1, giving many use cases.
  • No explicit S/A/B tier letter rating is present in the text.

Awakening/Manifestation progression: Two parallel tracks progress per stage: "Awakening" and "Inner Cube". - 1Stage 1: Awakening = Ultimate Enhancement 1. Reworks

KR-server balance changes for this unit, newest first (see the full Rework History).

Awakening2025-09-02

Expanded Inner Break awakening

  • Inner Break awakening adds two enhancement tracks.
  • Stages 1/3/5 enhance ultimate [Death God's Magic Bullet]: at max, deals 180% ATK plus 8% Max HP per debuff on the enemy (HP-based portion capped at 140% of caster ATK); cannot be countered and consumes no ammo.
  • Stages 2/4/6 enhance unique passive [Fatal Whisper]: at max, on combat attack against a debuffed enemy gain Femme Fatale; applies Fatal Temptation 7 and Crit Rate +8% per enemy debuff; Crit Damage +25% vs male targets; grants Quick Draw 3.
  • Femme Fatale (4 stacks, permanent, cannot be dispelled): -1 TP cost to cast Death God's Magic Bullet per stack.
  • Fatal Temptation 7 (2 turns, cannot be dispelled): target takes +30% damage, deals -40% damage, and grants the caster preemptive strike on its combat attacks.
