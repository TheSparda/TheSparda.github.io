Signature Gear

Guide

Note: This is NamuWiki community assessment (KR server), subjective community opinion.

Overview: Base class Defender; Tier 3 classes Centurion / Sanctum Sentinel / Prime Shield; ultimate Absolute Chain Wave. Unique passive Oath of Solidarity: escorts adjacent allies, and when taking non-DoT/non-counter damage stacks Will to Revive; at 4 stacks triggers ATK-based self-heal. She is already tanky, self-heal is baseline, and the Radiant Gatekeeper adds pseudo self-heal, so survivability is excellent. Awakening skills add an ally support aura, letting her also act as an ATK/spell-power buffer, but the aura is a Power attribute so it does not stack with other Power effects.

Classes:

  • Centurion:+1 more → Counter-focused. DEF/RES +20%~30%, ATK gains 20%~30% of DEF, counter dmg +20%~30%. The higher DEF goes, the stronger ATK and counters, a "porcupine" that punishes attackers. Since Siren stacks DEF heavily, well-built Centurion Siren often becomes the endgame class; note no RES gain, so watch magic attacks. Learns useful but low priority.
  • Sanctum Sentinel: ATK/DEF/RES +20%~30% unconditionally; at turn start gains Silence Bulwark: dmg taken -10%~15%, banish immune, TP-removal immune, skill-control immune, removes 1 debuff when the buff ends, 2 turns, cannot be dispelled, but removed when attacked. Balanced/plain; teaches the essential Radiant Gatekeeper Summon. More PvP-oriented (better with fewer enemies). Less flashy than the other two but a solid pick when immunity is needed.
  • Prime Shield: Most defensive; highest DEF/RES gain (30%~40%) plus dmg taken -10%. On taking non-DoT/non-counter damage, stacks Absolute Grudge: ATK +20%, Physical Pen +10%, consumed after attacking, max 3 stacks, permanent duration (max stack immediately if the attacker is large-size). Counters can reach ATK +60% + Phys Pen 30%. Good for throwing into the enemy line to soak hits then unload, or for big single hits via God Hammer / ultimate. Teaches God Hammer (a strong main skill), so it must be taken. Also a favored endgame class for players wanting survivability plus burst.

Key skills & ultimate:

  • Shield of Retribution: TP 1. Choose Punishment Aura or Retribution Barrier; 1 adjacent ally also gets the barrier; gain re-action after use; cooldown 3. Core skill; extends escort range and Punishment Strike fires separately from counters with no range limit. Cooldown 3 = effectively permanent uptime.
  • Radiant Gatekeeper Summon: TP 2, cooldown 3. Summons a gatekeeper for 3 turns with 70% of summoner stats (excl. spell power): provides cover, escorts allies, holds a control zone; has Sanctum Healer (heals 2 random allies within 2 tiles for 100% ATK on summon, 50% ATK when an ally ends turn within 2 tiles) and Sanctum Avenger (100% ATK defense-ignore dmg to enemies ending turn within 2 tiles). No separate turn; dies if summoner dies; buff/debuff/move-control immune. An all-in-one escort tank + healer + turret + assist damage. With Siren adjacent, a single enemy attack can trigger Siren counter + Punishment Strike + Sanctum Avenger (triple hit).
  • God Hammer: TP 3, cooldown 3. Targets a 3x3 area within 3 tiles: Slow debuff + 140% ATK dmg, removes 2 buffs; enemies not afflicted by Slow take 200% dmg; 60% chance to create a Sanctification Zone. Considered high-value: 200% at TP 3 is unrivaled (even Black Prince's ultimate needs max enhancement to reach 200%). Zone combines heal-damage plus buff-strip/debuff-clear.
  • Charge Flash:+1 more → TP 2, cooldown 2. 80% DEF physical fixed dmg, knock back 2 tiles; if the target hits a wall/enemy, Stun + quick approach for 30% ATK extra dmg. Useful attack/stun/mobility skill.
  • Dual Impact Wave: TP 3, cooldown 2. Leap-attack, 60% DEF physical fixed dmg to enemies within surrounding 8 tiles, 2-turn Slow (move -1). Can leap over terrain/obstacles.
  • Taunt Shout: TP 3. Taunt enemies within 2 tiles 1 turn; dmg taken from targets -20%. Taunt forces basic attacks on caster and reduces their dmg dealt by 20% (combined 40% reduction). High value for tank play but not essential since she is already an escort tank.
  • Wave-Break Advance Flash: TP 2, cooldown 1. 75% ATK dmg + 75% ATK splash to nearby enemies; applies Weakness Exposed 2 turns (dmg taken +10%, crit-received chance +10%, max 3 stacks, does not stack with other Exposed). Good for raids.
  • Ultimate Absolute Chain Wave: Move to a tile within 3, ATK 80% + DEF 80% dmg to enemies within 2 tiles of target, 100% Stun, applies Armor Crack; 2 dmg to installations. (Note: guide text lists Armor Crack as DEF/RES -25%, max 2 stacks, 2 turns; the skill table lists -35% (?).) Super-enhanced Dual Impact Wave; scales off both ATK and DEF, so raise both.

Recommended gear notes: Dedicated weapon Thunderdoom Guardian is a must (DEF-to-ATK conversion, escort dmg reduction, TP payback, penetration resist, +1 movement); a copy alone is strong, 3-star adds +1 move. Armor: Centurion -> Ferocity 4-set to maximize ATK for counters; Prime Shield -> Endurance 4-set to maximize HP + dmg reduction. Accessories vary: Savior's Tablet (well-matched), Dragon Scale / Steel Greaves, Golden Apple Hairpin (crit-dmg reduction), Healing Censer, Cursed Eye, Ancient Hero's Ring.

Evaluation: An all-in-one tank doing escort-tank + heal + damage + debuff-clear + ATK buffer alone. Called a top-tier tank succeeding Dunamis and Isabel. Both bruiser (Prime Shield + Endurance) and offensive (Centurion + Ferocity, recommended only at full awakening) builds are excellent. Said to shine in PvP (Isabel is stronger in PvE), but she is not weak in PvE either. Though she came after Isabel she is not a strict upgrade; the fairer view is mutual-compatibility / trade-offs. Isabel gets strong dmg-reduction on escort and CC immunity but cannot heal allies; Siren has no CC self-resistance (her biggest weakness) but heals via Will to Revive, buffs multiple allies evenly, strikes lightning after combat, and her ultimate benefits from counter-dmg bonuses. Siren relies on the summoned gatekeeper (which has full CC immunity) to handle CC. Neither dominates the meta. Best synergy partners: Beramode, Dycus Rockwood, and Saint-King Lacid Pandragon (same faction/attribute, strong Pandragon buffs, easy for newbies). Note: at Max Manifestation, the Oath Guardian buff is a Power effect so it does not stack with the Saint-King's buff (Saint-King takes priority).

Awakening/Manifestation progression: Each stage grants two enhancements: - Stage 1: Ultimate Enhancement 1 + Stat Enhancement 1 - Stage 2: Unique Passive Enhancement 1 + Stat Enhancement 2 - Stage 3: Ultimate Enhancement 2 + Artifact Acquired - Stage 4: Unique Passive Enhancement 2 + Artifact Enhancement 1 - Stage 5: Ultimate Enhancement 3 + Artifact Enhancement 2 - Stage 6: Unique Passive Enhancement 3 + Artifact Enhancement 3 Ultimate scaling by rank: First acquire = ATK 60% + DEF 60% dmg, 50% Stun chance, 2 dmg to installations. Max Awakening = ATK 75% + DEF 75%. Max Manifestation = ATK 100% + DEF 100%, 50% Stun, plus Armor Crack.

KR Creator Guide

Character review of Commander Siren Woodville by 하막군의게임튜브 · 2026-08-04

The reviewer applies his own test for whether a unit is genuinely strong: can it go on a defence team? Commander Siren Woodville can, and that's his recommendation — pull her if you're able.

She is extremely durable with high health, but unusually for a tank she also hits hard, so you can't simply ignore her and kill everything else first. Tanks also stay relevant for a long time, so she's a durable investment. (24:25)

Everything routes through defence

Her passive gives +30% DEF/RES, the classes add another 30-40%, and then two separate effects convert defence into attack: the Vigor armour set (30%) and her own weapon (20%). That's 50% of her defence added to her attack — which is why every build decision points the same way. (15:36)

Regenerative Will stacks whenever she takes damage that isn't damage-over-time or counterattack damage — so effectively any real attack. At full manifestation it only needs 3 stacks to heal her, and since she guards allies within 2 tiles she gets hit constantly. He reports it firing much more often than expected. (19:27)

Class: Sanctum Sentinel to hold, Prime Shield to hit

Sanctum Sentinel gives +30% ATK/DEF/RES and Silent Vow at every turn start: immunity to Banish, TP removal and Skill Control, which clears a debuff when she's attacked.

The consequence he draws out: since it clears on being hit, Action Control attached to an attack — a stun rider on a charge, say — gets removed immediately. Effectively stun immunity too, with the caveat that with two debuffs on her you can't choose which one clears. (3:37)

Prime Shield trades that for +40% DEF and a flat, non-clearing -10% damage taken, plus Absolute Grudge on being hit: ATK and Physical Penetration that build as she is attacked and reset once she attacks. Take three hits, then swing.

Against a large monster it goes straight to max stacks — which he reads as being aimed squarely at Dragon's Nest Assault rather than raids. (4:42)

His split: Sanctum Sentinel by default, Prime Shield when you want her hitting back. (5:43)

Summon Radiant Gatekeeper is the annoying one

The summon holds a Control Zone, guards allies and grants cover — standard tank fare. The line that matters is buff/debuff immunity.

He describes meeting one in guild battle: the Gatekeeper guarded everything around it, so his attacks kept being redirected into it, and because it's immune to debuffs he couldn't put it to sleep and work around it. He ended up simply eating the damage. Placed well, it's genuinely obstructive. (8:19)

Like Arondight, the summon snapshots 70% of her stats at the moment of summoning. (9:55)

The rest of the kit

Shield of Retribution grants a shield and the Punishing Aura — ATK, guarding allies within 2 tiles, and Punishing Strike (60% DEF-ignoring damage) triggered after being attacked, after a single-target attack, or after a flanking attack. It also gives an adjacent ally the shield and grants her an extra action.

His practical read: don't overthink the three trigger conditions, just keep the aura up. The extra action lets her run forward, cast it beside an ally, and then move on to block a lane. (6:14)

God Hammer is a 3x3 at range 3 for 140% that strips two buffs and applies Slow — and against a target already immune to Slow it deals 200% instead. It also has a 60% chance to lay a consecrated zone, which he rates as too random to plan around. (9:55, 10:57)

The ultimate charges up to 3 tiles and deals 80% of ATK plus 80% of DEF in a 2-tile radius with a guaranteed stun and Armour Fracture. Opening a fight by stunning four units is the play. (11:39)

The combo that doesn't work

He walks through an idea and then disproves it: cast Shield of Retribution, take the extra action to summon the Gatekeeper as a roadblock, spending all 3 TP, then fire the ultimate next turn.

It fails from ★4 on the weapon, because the TP refund drops from "3 TP or more" to "2 TP or more" — so summoning refunds a TP and you never reach the empty state the plan needs. If you want that opening, stop the weapon at ★3. (12:30, 14:35)

Gear

The Vigor set, and he says nothing else is really acceptable — the 30% of defence converted to attack is too large to give up.

Accessory: Savior's Tablet, for the penetration resistance. Her defence is only worth having if attackers can't penetrate it, and it adds 10% DEF on top. (13:01)

The weapon

Max HP, up to -10% damage taken, ATK from 12% of DEF, penetration resistance, +20% of DEF as ATK, +30% Physical Penetration and counterattack damage, -15% damage taken while guarding, and a TP refund. A lot of what other units get from an artifact is built into it.

★3 adds Movement +1 and is the value line — and, per the section above, also the last rank that preserves the ultimate opener. ★5 and ★6 add penetration resistance, which he rates highly: stacked with Savior's Tablet and Devotion runes it's poison for the 100%-penetration builds now common. (14:05)

Runes

Sun in 3 and 5, Ultimate in 2 and 4 — near-fixed. For 1 and 6, Devotion runes are the best, and he says even a rare-grade Devotion is worth using; Boar otherwise. (16:38)

Manifestation

2 grants Bond Oath to an adjacent ally (sharing 10% of damage taken from allies within 3 tiles) and Oath Guardian to herself, and upgrades the aura from DEF/RES to ATK/DEF/RES. Between everything she's at roughly 30-35% damage reduction before any gear. 4 improves the command aura. 6 takes Regenerative Will to 3 stacks and extends Bond Oath to 4 tiles. (18:10)

One conflict to know: Oath Guardian is an Authority, so it doesn't stack with Holy King Lacid's Pandragon's Legacy. Players disagree about which to prefer — Legacy's Movement +1 and +30% base stats, or Oath Guardian's 10% damage reduction. (20:29)

Artifacts

Wind of Recovery+2 more → is his pick: ATK, DEF and RES, plus -20% damage taken whenever she's hit by Action Control — which happens constantly to a tank.

Peak Condition+12 more → is the alternative he runs on Dunamis Rembrandt and Durant. Flash Combo Secret Manual+4 more → has no real use here. (21:49)
