Signature Gear

Guide

(NamuWiki community assessment, KR server. Subjective community opinion.)

Performance/Overview: Base class Ranger; Tier 2 has the exclusive class Corsair; Tier 3 classes are Highmaster/Captain/Falconier. Ultimate is JJ! (throwing pet hawk J.J.). Unique passive Silver Faith: at ally turn start with no move-control debuff, 50%~80% chance to gain a 1-turn unremovable buff granting ATK +5% and crit chance +20% per 1 move range. If held at end of action, grants one extra action per turn (removed after a combat attack). Silver's base move is 4, so conditionally once per turn she can gain ATK +20% and crit +80%; since removal is only on combat attacks, using an area skill keeps the buff and yields crit + extra action, making it a very strong passive. Free Wind nullifying move-reduction debuffs for the whole team also has future potential. However Silver Faith is very RNG-heavy: unlike the old norm where 5-star gave a guarantee, even 6-star tops out at 80%, and only Awakening stage 4's unique skill makes it guaranteed.

Classes: Three Tier 3 classes: Highmaster (weakens 2 random nearby enemies each turn), Captain (focused on solo action), Falconier (focused on ATK and TP steal). All classes have control-zone-ignoring movement. Highmaster learns Single-Point Breaker and can only use "sword"-type weapons, so it cannot use the exclusive weapon (like Ranger Mary who also has a rapier exclusive weapon); it runs a separate Scout-Highmaster tree and its Single-Point Breaker is low priority, so it gets pushed back. Captain learns Whirlwind Blade, has the Naval Blockade passive (ATK up to +25% when no ally within 8 tiles, generates control zone, damage taken reduced by enemy count x20%, max 60%); a tanking-oriented class, differentiated from Valkyries by generating a control zone while also having control-zone-ignore movement; Captain is recommended for priority leveling due to its strong single-target skill. Falconier learns Sky Path, gains up to +25% ATK unconditionally, and its Sky Watcher triggers when an enemy ends action within 2 tiles (steal 1 TP, deal up to 30% ATK damage); good turret-like use with auto-attack + TP steal + ATK up, but has no DEF buff so use carefully, pairing with a guard-capable tank. Green attribute lacked a guard tank, solved by adding Isabelle Lippine; light-attribute tanks like Durant/Joan can also guard.

Exclusive skills and Ultimate: TP 1: charge skill, low damage but low TP cost; being an area skill it does not remove Silver Faith, so guaranteed extra action after use; 1-turn cooldown plus control-zone-ignore lets her roam enemy lines; low damage is a balance choice for the passive; blade buff being RNG is a downside. TP 2: 140% single-target melee, TP payback on crit; low multiplier but TP payback; crit is easy since Silver Faith gives +80% crit; 1-turn cooldown; note it is a "combat attack" so it consumes the extra action. TP 1: strong mobility + penetration-support skill; because she rides J.J., ignores obstacles/terrain, one of the best movement skills in the game (can trivialize position-hold clear conditions like Brave's Grave floor 53 or Ice Dragon Castle); applies a debuff (+30% penetration damage taken), can gain extra action with Silver Faith; absurdly good for a TP 1 skill, but 3-turn cooldown is fairly long. Ultimate JJ! steals all of one enemy's TP within 5 tiles and deals 160%~190% (up to 220% with awakening) uncounterable/unguardable single-target damage; long range and Silver's many TP sources make it usable; refills her own TP, good utility and post-use tempo.

Exclusive weapon and recommended gear: Exclusive weapon Pirate King's Treasure Sword; extra stat ATK up; weapon skill is unconditional crit chance up; exclusive effect grants unremovable buff Unfair Trade at ally turn start, scaling greatly with weapon awakening level:

  • Base: give 1 removable debuff on self to enemy in combat, remove Unfair Trade. Cooldown 3 turns.
  • 2 Awaken: give 2 removable debuffs. Cooldown 3 turns.
  • 3 Awaken: give all removable debuffs on self to enemy. Cooldown 3 turns.
  • 4 Awaken: give all removable debuffs, steal 1 removable buff from target. Cooldown 2 turns.
  • 5 Awaken: give all removable debuffs, steal 2 removable buffs. Cooldown 2 turns.
  • 6 Awaken: give all removable debuffs, steal all removable buffs. Cooldown 2 turns.

At 6 Awaken it becomes insane, dumping all debuffs on the enemy and stealing all their buffs (including shields/defensive buffs) before attacking, so a 4 Awaken+ Silver's attack effectively hits the enemy bare. Unconditional crit up is also good, buff-ignoring attacks possible, buff-lottery possible; but low awakening is somewhat mediocre. Non-exclusive recommended weapon: Moonlight Sword, since +0.1% ATK per 1% crit means Silver's +80% crit yields +8% ATK, so the damage ceiling is Moonlight Sword (rapiers have low ATK). Armor depends on build: base crit is so high that crit can exceed 100% needlessly, so rather than crit/crit-dmg sets, prefer ATK/crit-dmg Assassin set (Hanzo motif), Nangcheon motif, phys penetration + flat ATK, or Windstorm set (G.S motif) for tanking. Accessory also varies: Arrowhead for damage ceiling, or balanced Solitary Shadow Plate for both tanking and damage.

Evaluation: A nimble, sturdy pirate lady who steals things even better than a thief. Extra actions from her passive plus her skills give excellent mobility, good at hit-and-run yet usable as a tank; TP steal/payback on skills and ultimate enable tricky play. With a vigor buff, combos like TP1 enter -> extra action -> TP2 refund -> vigor -> TP1 retreat or the reverse are possible. Multipliers look low but with the exclusive weapon, the basic Silver Thunder Flash -> extra-action Whirlwind Blade combo lands Whirlwind Blade as a guaranteed crit on a de-buffed target; using Sky Path -> Silver Thunder Flash -> Whirlwind Blade, if RNG hits you can also apply +50% penetration damage taken. Even better with an Arc Knight-type ally nullifying the class passive. However, since the enter-the-enemy Captain class learns the damage skill while the offense-heavy Falconier learns the movement skill, you must level both classes to use her properly; guaranteed passive activation is at Awakening stage 4 and the exclusive weapon favors high awakening, so she needs significant investment. Also the AI rarely uses the Silver Thunder Flash-Whirlwind Blade combo, so manual control is needed for full potential. Surprisingly capable of sniping Black Prince: the 2 TP skill Latent Heat Flash applies a 2-turn heal-block on crit, and Silver with the Crow 4-set can crit almost guaranteed after Silver Faith activates, suppressing Black Prince's extreme recovery.

KR Creator Guide

Character review of Silver by 하막군의게임튜브 · 2026-08-04

Silver was a long-awaited release and the reviewer's take is measured: better than he expected, but not a PvP powerhouse. Her real home is the Hero's Grave, where her mobility and combo lines shine and where hitting two or three targets in a chain is easy. In PvP he doesn't see her damage keeping up, despite an excellent base stat line. (17:43)

Silver Conviction is huge, and it's a dice roll

At the start of an ally turn, if she has no movement-control debuff on her, she has a 70% chance to gain Silver Conviction — and it grants +5% ATK and +20% Crit Rate per point of Movement. At 4 Movement that is +20% ATK and +80% Crit Rate, on top of the weapon's contribution. He shows her sitting at 85-89% Crit from this alone. (1:32)

Two problems follow from that. First, the 70% is a real coin flip in a single-attempt format like guild battle — he had to re-record several times because it wouldn't proc. Second, and more important, Silver Conviction is removed after a combat attack, which also cancels the extra action it grants. (3:04)

So the way to actually use it is to act with things that are not combat attacks: the movement skills, the summon, the heal, and the Heal Block skill all qualify, and she keeps the extra action afterwards. He suggests applying Heal Block and then following with Gale Blade to kill something like Crow. (3:36)

Class: Falconer over Captain

Captain ignores Control Zones when moving, creates a 1-ring Control Zone (smaller than a tank's), reduces damage taken per enemy inside it, and adds +25% ATK with no adjacent allies. The concept is an Emilio-style zone-and-withdraw role — but the reviewer doubts she is durable enough to stand where that requires. (4:40)

Falconer is his pick: +25% ATK flat, Control Zone ignore, and Sky Watcher, which fires on any enemy ending its action within 2 tiles — stealing 1 TP and dealing 30% of ATK. The TP theft is the point: it feeds her, and once she is at full TP the skill chain below does the work. (5:42)

The combo: Sky Path into Gale Blade

Silver Thunder Flash on its own is weak — 50% damage and only a 70% chance at the penetration buff. It becomes worthwhile as part of a chain. (7:16)

Sky Path is the enabler. It moves her up to 7 tiles and applies Keen Gaze to enemies within 2 tiles of the landing spot, raising penetration damage they take. Because it isn't a combat attack, she keeps her extra action.

Then Gale Blade — 140%, refunds TP on a critical hit, and only a 1-turn cooldown, so with her Crit sitting near 95% she can cast it nearly every turn. Fly in with Sky Path, tag a group with Keen Gaze, and cut them down; next turn Silver Thunder Flash punches through and stacks its own penetration buff on top for a much bigger Gale Blade. (7:47)

Gear

Black Crow is the one set he says not to run — her Crit is already high enough that it's wasted. Two pieces of it is fine if you want some. One Against a Thousand or Envoy of the Storm are better, or a 2+2 split.

Accessory: Serrated Arrowhead for Crit damage, since she crits constantly. Ancient Hero's Ring is, as ever, also good. (12:30)

The weapon is optional

Unfair Deal hands the enemy one of your debuffs, scaling with awakening: 2 at ★2, all of them at ★3, all plus stealing one buff at ★4, two at ★5, everything at ★6, with +5-10% Crit Rate alongside.

His verdict is that it is not mandatory. If you want the Crit it's fine, but other swords work, and the character was given away at 5★ — play her first and decide later. (12:00)

Runes

Crit runes are pointless on her. Chaos, Chaos, Ultimate, Ultimate, Conspiracy, Conspiracy is his default for maximising health and ATK, with slots 5 and 6 open to taste. (13:33)

Manifestation: 4 is the line

2 raises Silver Conviction's chance to 90%, 4 makes it 100%, and 6 adds a clause preventing it being cancelled. If you intend to play her seriously, 4 is the number — the whole kit hinges on that buff actually landing. (14:35)

Artifacts

Wind Step+10 more → adds Movement, and since she has no dash skill and the Movement directly scales Silver Conviction, it is strong — 2 stacks puts her at 6 Movement, and it only drops when she is hit.

Warrior's Essence+10 more → raises ATK on melee skills, and her main attack is melee, so it fits.

Pulse of Life+3 more → is the survivability pick and the one he explains most warmly: Falconer's TP theft triggers it, healing 5% of Max HP per TP she holds — 15% at 3 TP — every time an enemy stops within 2 tiles, plus a shield at end of turn when no allies are adjacent. (15:39)
