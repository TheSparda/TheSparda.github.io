Signature Gear

Guide

Note: NamuWiki community assessment (KR server), subjective community opinion. No explicit S/A/B tier rating is given in the source.

Base class is Mage. Tier 3 classes are Archmage / Frost Mage / Dark Wizard. She has no ultimate; her dedicated weapon is "Trinity Force," apparently conceived around Triple Elemental Blast.

Her unique passive is "Three-Color Witch": after using fire/ice/lightning skills, she gains a different enhancement per element for 3 turns:

  • Fire: Spell Power + Crit Rate up 15~20%. On activation, removes fire debuffs.
  • Ice: gain a Spell-Power-scaled shield; while the shield lasts, Spell Power +10%. On activation, removes ice debuffs.
  • Lightning: Spell Power +5~10%, skill range +1. On activation, removes lightning debuffs.

She has no ultimate, but has the traditional strongest spell Triple Elemental Blast, plus staple spells like Moon Light, Electric Wave, Thunderbolt, and Magic Spear.

However, the enhancement granted by her dedicated weapon is random, making it hard to use; her only fire skills are Flame Ball and Triple Elemental Blast, so fire-related buffs are hard to leverage. Her damage ceiling is high, but reaching that ceiling demands time and luck, so she is not much recommended. Notably, her dedicated weapon is the only one where awakening raises only the bonus stats and not the weapon skill's multiplier, so it is judged a weapon with no value in high awakening.

The post-awakening mechanic is similar but also different. Performance depends on how much enhancement you accumulate, but one Triple Elemental Blast can accumulate all enhancements. At high awakening, if all three elemental enhancements apply, a re-attack or extra action becomes possible, so how quickly you can use Triple Elemental Blast is key.

You will generally use Archwizard (Archmage) / Frost Mage. Archwizard can steal 1 TP after a combat attack; Frost Mage has high survivability and starts with boosted ice-magic attack power. Cautions: the weapon has only a 1-in-3 chance to land the element you want, and for Archwizard, stealing 1 TP requires a "combat" attack. Kun's only combat-attack skills are Ice Missile, Magic Spear, or a basic attack, so to steal 1 TP while also getting elemental enhancement, Ice Missile use is forced.

If you must use Triple Elemental Blast on turn 2, use Archwizard: Ice Missile steals 1 TP + the ice attribute's "required TP -1" effect lets you use Triple Elemental Blast on turn 2. If you only need an AoE on turn 2, Frost Mage also works: if ice mastery appears on the weapon from the start you can use Triple Elemental Blast on turn 2, and even if not, Moon Light can be cast for 3 TP. Frost Mage's high survivability makes it worth considering. Of course, stealing 1 TP mid-combat can matter, so running Archwizard is one option, and ignoring it and picking one Triple Elemental Blast plus two elemental spells is another.
