Classes

Mage (Tier 1)

Light defense · Dealer, CC · Weapons: Staff, Rod

A beginner Light-element magic class with Range 3. She combines damage and control, applying AoE Burn with Flame Ball and Frost Slow with Ice Missile (2 turns; stacking it into Freeze locks down actions). Her M-ATK rises when allies are adjacent (Composure), and Mana Blade grants a Shield per enemy hit, making her stable when dealing AoE damage beside allies.
