Guide

Note: the following is NamuWiki community assessment (KR server), subjective. No explicit S/A/B tier rating is stated.

Performance: Base class Mage. Tier 2 has exclusive class "Charming Mage"; Tier 3 classes are Dark Wizard / Florist / Dream Weaver. Ult is "Hedonic Grab". Unique passive "Beguiling Mirage": when no enemies are nearby, if attacked by a male character and an adjacent empty tile exists, she leaves a clone and moves, entering "Phase Leap" and taking no damage, gaining the Beguiling Mirage buff. During Phase Leap she takes no damage/skills/effects but cannot act. It is removed at the start of the ally turn (so no impact on acting), but she cannot gain TP during it, so be careful there.

Class: Tier 3 options: Dark Wizard (learns Triple Elemental Blast, "TEB"), Florist (disrupts male enemies' skill use), Dream Weaver (supports allied males).

  • Dark Wizard: common class that learns the Pandragon mage corps' signature Triple Elemental Blast; passive confuses nearby enemies (making them flee) and cuts resistance at char turn end. Whether inherited from her grandmother, Este (strong survivability vs male enemies) can use it usefully, but confusion/resistance-reduction applying to only 2 enemies is a letdown.
  • Florist: exclusive class that learns "Furispore". Puts 1 skill of the nearest 1 enemy on cooldown; in combat, spell power rises and damage taken drops per skill on cooldown on the target. No range limit on the cooldown effect (only proximity priority), so it's quite strong. Furispore is a very strong main skill, so priority leveling is recommended.
  • Dream Weaver: exclusive class that learns "Mass Hypnosis", following the Charming Mage - Dream Weaver exclusive tree. Nearby allies gain spell power and reduced AoE damage taken; when Este uses an action-control skill, her chance to be crit and AoE damage taken drop. An ally spell-power support class.

Exclusive skills and ult: three exclusives: Furispore (Florist), Deadly Charm (Charming Mage), Mass Hypnosis (Dream Weaver).

  • Furispore: TP 2. Deals 150% spell power to an enemy within 1-3 tiles. If the caster has a debuff, an extra hit adds 100%. If the target is male, another extra hit adds 50%. When self-debuffed and attacking a male, she can deal a huge 300% spell-power single-target burst.
  • Deadly Charm: TP 1. Cuts target movement by 1 and forces it to move toward Este on its turn ("Deadly Charm" debuff). If the enemy ends adjacent to Este, Deadly Charm is removed and replaced by the unremovable "Lingering Attachment" debuff (counter-disabled, damage dealt to Este -80%).
  • Mass Hypnosis: TP 3. Designate 1 tile within 3 tiles; enemies within 2 tiles of it get a 1-turn Hypnosis debuff and one skill put on cooldown. Hypnosis = action-disabled + TP-recovery-disabled for 1 turn, removed if damaged; duration +1 turn vs males.
  • Ult Hedonic Grab: designates 1 enemy for instant control; when the debuff is removed it deals spell-power-based damage and applies "Confusion". As a Mind Control type ult, it's a tremendous instant-control skill. Este's main skills have low TP cost, so timing it is easy. As a neat original-work nod, Roberto de Medici gets +1 turn debuff duration.

Weapon & recommended gear: Exclusive weapon Lure Butterfly (bonus stat: spell power), a legend staff increasing magic damage to enemies that have an action-control effect; Este's exclusive effect additionally raises extra-hit damage vs debuffed enemies in combat and reduces damage received from the target. Since Furispore triggers extra hits, it's a Furispore-enhancement weapon. Besides the exclusive weapon, Diablo's exclusive "Lord of the Moon" also has action-control-based effects and is usable. Armor: because main skills have long cooldowns, the Pope set (Rubens Heisenberg papal-costume motif, has cooldown reduction) is most recommended; the Chancellor set (Veradin motif) can go all-in on spell power for a Furispore-main build. The Great Sage set (Vishnu Man motif) is not recommended unless dropping single-target for TEB + Electric Wave (Great Sage 2-set is worth considering). TP cost is low, so the Cardinal set (Van Dyke cardinal-costume motif) is also not recommended; at Hero grade give the Dark Mage set (no other choice anyway). Accessory: at Legend grade the Stopwatch is most recommended (spell power + cooldown reduction); at Hero grade give a Whetstone to boost Furispore burst, or Black Dice for survivability.

Evaluation: An action-control specialist tuned against male enemies. The background emphasizes her femme-fatale side and greatly downplays the "competent, well-governing lord" side; via exclusive weapon/skills it establishes an original character trait not in prior lore: a "haraguro (black-hearted) sly-fox villainess who specializes in hypnosis and brainwashing". In PvE most enemies are male so she works well, but she is merely stronger vs males with no penalty vs female/genderless, so she's usable anywhere; with TEB and Furispore (up to 300% spell-power dealing) her damage isn't lacking either. Ignoring efficiency, she can be run as a magic dealer with Dark Wizard + Electric Wave + TEB + Furispore. In PvP, given her concept she performs well even at the name-card (unawakened) stage, and her hypnosis/brainwashing goes through all male characters with no restriction whatsoever, making her a decisive anti-male weapon that can almost certainly take out one targeted male. To counter the opponent's Black Prince and protect one's own, top-tier users unconditionally max Este first. Because of this, although within the realm of gameplay allowance, complaints arose that it might be a setting error.

KR Creator Guide

Character review of Este Dode by 하막군의게임튜브 · 2026-08-04

Este Dode arrived to a lot of "she's another Erica" scepticism and the reviewer came away impressed: a genuine PvP character after a long run of raid units, with an unusually strong M-ATK stat line and a kit built almost entirely around control. He describes her as the hope of the low-investment player for dealing with Black Prince. (21:43)

Her stat line is the thing he keeps returning to. At 5 copies, no runes, wearing Clausewitz's hand-me-down gear, she reads over 2,500 M-ATK where Clausewitz in the same kit read 2,300-2,400. (20:40)

Phase Leap is the survival tool

When attacked in combat, if an adjacent tile is free, she leaves a clone behind and phases out — taking no damage, unaffected by magic. It works exactly like the thief classes' mirror image. She can't recover TP while out, and returns at the start of the next ally turn with an M-ATK boost. (1:04)

Mirage stacks up to 3 and matter more with rank; see the manifestation notes below. (2:05)

Class: both are good, and both target men

Florist puts one skill of the nearest enemy male character on cooldown at turn start — and there is no range limit. Anywhere on the map. He points out it will happily burn a Pandragon soldier's taunt, and failing that reaches into the enemy back line. On defence it disrupts whatever the attacker planned. (2:35)

Dream Weaver gives +30% M-ATK and -30% area damage taken while an allied male character is within 2 tiles, and casting an Action Control skill grants Blurred Boundary to all allied male characters, again with no range limit.

He flags a probable bug: using Mass Hypnosis's big brother, the ultimate, means the mind-controlled enemy briefly counts as an ally and picks up Blurred Boundary too. (4:10)

Deadly Charm turns one enemy off

Deadly Charm cuts Movement by 1 and then forces the target to walk toward her on its own turn, unable to do anything else. When it finally reaches an adjacent tile the effect clears and leaves Lingering — during which it cannot counterattack and takes 80% less damage.

The line he demonstrates: walk forward, apply Deadly Charm, then use the skill's own 7-tile reposition to retreat to safety. The target spends its turns marching toward her and eating hits the whole way. (5:41)

Mass Hypnosis is the best area control in the game

3-tile targeting, everything within 2 tiles of the marked tile gets Hypnosis for a turn plus a skill put on cooldown — and male characters get an extra stack.

Hypnosis is Sleep with a difference: it also blocks TP recovery. The usual counterplay against a Sleep — wait it out and then unload an ultimate — doesn't work here. He demonstrates putting an entire enemy team of four to sleep at once. (8:20)

Nemesis can hit for 300%

Nemesis deals 150% of M-ATK, adds a 100% follow-up if Este Dode herself has a debuff, and another 50% if the target is male. Against a male target with a debuff on herself, that is 300% — enough to kill outright.

Getting a debuff on herself is the awkward part, so an enemy Iyolin actually helps her. And he names Crow as the ideal victim: male, and his own weapon applies a debuff to her as it hits. (10:22)

The ultimate, and its one hard counter

Hedonic Grab mind-controls the target immediately. Against de Medici it lasts two turns instead of one — which the reviewer finds almost funny, because de Medici is precisely the counter to her.

Every control skill she has is Action Control, and de Medici's shout both clears Action Control and prevents it. He tested it: mind-control de Medici, cast that shout while controlling him, and it clears everything Este Dode has applied. Her debuffs are removable, which is the kit's real weakness. (11:25)

What makes the ultimate frightening anyway is the fixed damage on expiry — 70% at his rank, 140% at full awakening. He describes using it on Black Prince, killing an enemy with him, and having the expiry damage trigger his revive. (12:57)

Gear

Chancellor of Darkness with Necronomicon — the standard mage kit. (14:28)

Weapon: good, not required

+30% magic damage taken on Action-Controlled targets — a damage increase, not an M-ATK increase, so much bigger than it looks — plus +30% follow-up damage against debuffed enemies, which stacks directly into the Nemesis combo above.

There is a correction here he is unhappy about: the weapon originally read Range +2 and was silently patched to Range +1, which he says he specifically awakened it for. If you take it, ★4 or better. (14:28, 15:31)

Runes

Cloud, Cloud, Ultimate, Ultimate, Lightning, Lightning. Swap to Cloud/Lightning/Sun repeating if you want Crit-damage-taken reduction for survivability. (17:22)

Manifestation

2 starts her with an extra Mirage and adds area-damage reduction per stack against male attackers. 4 drops the TP-recovery cooldown to 2 turns. 6 starts her with two extra Mirages — three total, i.e. -45% area damage taken from male characters before the match begins.

The reviewer notes how pointed all of this is: "almost every one of these looks designed with Black Prince in mind." (18:35)

Artifacts

Attribute Overload+9 more → — more damage on a favourable elemental matchup — is another one he reads as aimed at Black Prince.

Mana Pulse he rates well and it suits how she is actually played: it builds a stack whenever she waits, and she waits often. His caveat is that he hasn't tested whether applying a debuff counts as attacking for the purpose of clearing it; he suspects not. Peak Condition+13 more → is the survivability option. (20:08)
