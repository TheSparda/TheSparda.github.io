Guide

Note: The following is NamuWiki community assessment (KR server), subjective community opinion, translated faithfully. No explicit S/A/B tier letter rating is present.

Performance: Base class is Defender. Tier 3 classes are White Knight / Inquisitor / Ascetic. Ultimate is Consecration Interval Rending Wave. The unique passive "Liberated Spirit" is a broken passive with everything a tanker needs rolled in. As awakening rank rises, defense increases unconditionally at a large multiplier; when HP is healthy, Escort triggers, but when in danger the Escort effect turns off automatically. It pulls all debuffs onto itself and heals HP per debuff count, and auto-cleanses action/skill control, after which DoT damage is also halved. Because the numeric scaling by awakening level is very large, high awakening is recommended; conversely this means it is underwhelming at the base stage.

Classes: The three Tier 3 classes are defense-specialized White Knight, resistance-based strong magic counter Inquisitor, and the aggressive Ascetic (self-heal, resistance-based attack scaling). All of Dunamis's classes have a passive granting adjacent allies Cover and creating a Control Zone.

  • White Knight: learns "Torch of Glory", takes the Armor Knight -> White Knight dedicated tree. Passive "Full Support" increases DEF and RES by adjacent ally count x20%; if only 1 ally is adjacent, that ally gets +30% DEF and RES and immunity to action/skill control. The easy pick for tanking use; can be an escort tank via passive and a taunt tank via Armor Knight's Taunting Shout. Torch of Glory also gives a RES buff so it is strong vs magic.
  • Inquisitor: learns "Guts Charge". When attacked, RES +up to 30%; when hit by a combat attack, after combat deals magic fixed damage equal to 100% of RES to the enemy. Focused on resistance-based counter damage. Counter damage scales at 100% of RES; passive gives +30% and greaves can bring it to +15%, so e.g. at 1000 RES counter damage reaches 1500. Downside: passive has no DEF increase, which needs compensating for.
  • Ascetic: learns dedicated skill "Deep Blaze Flash". RES up to 30% converts to ATK and SPI; if an ally within 2 tiles is attacked and drops to 60% HP or below, recover 10% of total HP and gain "Penance" debuff. "Penance" is a 2-turn debuff stacking up to 3, dealing 10% of total HP as magic damage when it expires, but can be removed by killing an enemy. Most attack-focused of the Tier 3 classes; effectively borrowing HP from your future self. Want to build RES since it raises ATK.
  • Recommended default: go White Knight first (highest DEF/RES gain, learns taunt and mobility). Inquisitor has no DEF gain and needs RES investment for judgment damage; Ascetic leans attack over tanking.

Key skills & Ultimate:

  • Charge Slash: TP 2. Deals physical fixed damage = 80% DEF, pushes enemy 2 tiles hard; if pushed into a wall/enemy, stun + rush for extra damage = 30% ATK. Cooldown 2 turns. Precious DEF-based attack for the tanker; also usable as a stun/mobility skill.
  • Double Impact Wave: TP 3. Designate 1 tile within 3 tiles; character jumps and slams, dealing physical fixed damage = 60% DEF to enemies within 8 surrounding tiles and applying "Slowed" for 2 turns. Cooldown 2 turns. DEF-based AoE that also works as mobility; can leap over terrain/obstacles.
  • Taunting Shout: TP 3. Applies "Taunt" to enemies within 2 tiles for 1 turn, and damage taken from targets -20%. Taunt blocks other actions for 1 turn and forces only basic attacks on the caster; caster's damage dealt -20%, for a combined 40% reduction. Ideal tanker skill.
  • Torch of Glory: TP 1. For 2 turns gives allies within 2 tiles RES 20% and HP recovery = 50% of AoE damage taken, plus grants extra movement. Covers AoE damage she cannot tank, and gives mobility (tanker move is only 3). A must-have. Cooldown 3 turns.
  • Deep Blaze Flash: TP 2. Deals damage = 160% ATK and applies "Suppression" for 2 turns (ATK/SPI/crit chance -25%, class passive cannot trigger). Cooldown 3 turns. High multiplier plus a top-tier debuff; almost feels wasted on Dunamis who values DEF/RES over ATK.
  • Ultimate Consecration Interval Rending Wave: AoE dealing 90120% DEF to enemies within 3 tiles, +510% DEF damage per debuff on self; after use removes all debuffs on self and Escort range increases to 2 tiles. Since her passive absorbs allies' debuffs, damage is easy to scale; the widened Escort range is also nice.

Recommended Equipment:

  • Weapon: Last Fantasm, legendary sword/shield; bonus stats HP and damage-taken reduction. Weapon skill gives ATK +1015% of DEF and heals 35% HP per debuff; for Dunamis, ATK gain becomes 15~25% of DEF and heal fixed at 5%. At turn start, if she has no debuff, gains an effect-less unremovable debuff "Unease" for 2 turns (cooldown 4 turns). Fits her well since debuff count boosts her passive recovery and ultimate damage, though from a pure tanking view some use other weapons.
  • Armor: go all-in on DEF and RES. Full RES investment gives excellent counter damage (reports of 3000 damage). Use Reversal 2, +13% DEF and RES plus another 2-set heavy armor. Choose one of Endurance 2, +20% HP, +10% DEF/+10% HP, or +15% DEF.
  • Accessories vary by playstyle: legendary/hero greaves (DEF+RES staple); Golden Apple Hairpin, reduces crit damage taken, one HP recovery per stage below a threshold; Healing Censer, legendary, heals below 50% HP, boosts HP/DEF/RES, helps meet Escort's HP condition; Cursed Eye, applies "Weakening Curse" pre-combat cutting ATK/SPI for 2 turns; recommended for escort tank; Ancient Hero's Ring, ATK + crit-damage reduction, recovers 25~50% of damage once per turn on crit hit; but very rare and needed by dealers.

Evaluation: Rated as one of the strongest legendary tankers, considered to have no substitute after Isabel Riffine, surpassing her "older brother". Not only protects allies but absorbs all debuffs while healing per debuff count and gaining immunity to act despite them; with Torch of Glory can even recover from AoE. Passive is strong enough to be said to have every tanker function; with just DEF and RES built, all 3 classes are usable, making her one of the most actively build-researched legendary characters. Somewhat underwhelming at name-card stage, but enhancement-stage efficiency is very good, so high awakening pays off. Downside: below a certain HP Escort stops triggering, so she cannot perfectly protect a single ally, but this also raises her own survival odds, a give-and-take. After the 2025 Inner Break update, enhancing the passive removes the HP condition on Escort, and awakening skills further raise HP and toughness, making her even sturdier with investment. Testimony includes: paired with Jebi, players "don't know how to kill" Dunam-i; someone tried Black Prince against her and instead the Black Prince collapsed from Inquisitor judgment damage.
