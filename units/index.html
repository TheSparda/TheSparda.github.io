Projected Banner Timeline

Estimated from KR dates · global picks out of Korean order

Global releases units out of Korean order, so there is no single gap to apply: the 13 units with confirmed dates arrived between 11 and 19 months after their Korean release (median 17). Each fortnightly patch brings 1 to 3 of them. This forecast simply queues the remaining units in Korean order at that cadence, which is why units get skipped and dates move. Confirmed dates are fixed; everything else is a best-effort estimate.
