Classes

Fighter (Tier 1)

Medium defense · Dealer · Weapons: Sword, Greatsword

A melee swordsman who gains extra actions via Sprint to land back-to-back heavy strikes, then advances into the vacated tile on a kill to keep the pressure going.

Knight (Tier 2)

Medium defense · Support, Dealer · Weapons: Sword

A formation swordsman who grows stronger the closer he fights beside allies, wrapping adjacent allies in DEF and Vigor buffs while boosting his own ATK.

Elevation

LvEffect
1Damage taken by self and adjacent allies is reduced by 10%, and if there is an adjacent ally in combat, ATK increases by 20%.
2Damage taken by self and adjacent allies is reduced by 10%, and if there is an adjacent ally in combat, ATK increases by 25%. (War Cry of Vigor: (TP 4, Range Self, Area AoE, CD 2 turns): Applies the Vigor effect for 2 turns to self and adjacent allies.)

Command Knight (Tier 3)

Medium defense · Dealer/Tank, Support · Weapons: Sword

One war cry lights everything up: his own ATK/damage-taken buffs, allied ATK/M-ATK/HP +20%, an extra action, cleanse, and enemy buff removal all fire at once. The key is every-other-turn play timed to the cry's 2-turn Cooldown.

Battlefield Command

LvEffect
1When using a Cry skill, once per turn gains the Battlefield Command 1 effect and an extra attack or skill use. After using a Cry skill, removes 1 debuff from allies who received the effect. Damage taken is reduced by 10%.
2When using a Cry skill, once per turn gains the Battlefield Command 2 effect and an extra attack or skill use. After using a Cry skill, removes 1 debuff from allies who received the effect. Damage taken is reduced by 20%. (Battlefield Cry: (TP 1, Range Self, Area AoE, CD 2 turns): Removes all Fear/Weaken effects from allies within 3 tiles and applies the Battlefield Cry effect. Removes 2 buffs from enemies within the same area.)
