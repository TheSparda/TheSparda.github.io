Signature Gear

Guide

NamuWiki community assessment (KR server). Subjective community opinion.

Overall: A glass-cannon burst dealer whose "true body" is the dedicated weapon at 3rd Awakening. In PVP, theoretically capable of a solo all-kill. But unlike the original, his fragile durability means he collapses after a single stray poke, and his main class LTK loses its attack buff the instant he takes damage, a fatal weakness. He only performs when the front row is solid enough that he never gets hit. Generally best to fill the front line based on the buffs of "the father" and the debuffs of "the girlfriend." Like most conditional-extra-attack and glass-cannon characters in these games, the gap between the base (business-card) version and the enhanced version is huge; since Christian has both traits, he needs at minimum 4th Awakening + dedicated weapon 3rd Awakening to use properly, or in extreme cases fully maxed/enhanced, so he is regarded almost as a whale-only character.

Performance / Passive: Base job Gunner. Tier 3 classes: Ace / Spitfire / L.T.K. Ult is Satellite Attack (per original). Unique passive 'Decisive Shot': at character turn end, gain 1 stack of Adrenaline per tile moved this turn; if an enemy was killed that turn, Confirmed Kill triggers. Adrenaline: +2% attack, +4% crit rate per stack, unremovable, up to 5~8 stacks, lasts 1 turn. Must keep moving to get the attack buff; Gunner range is fairly long but Christian is so squishy it's a burden, so pair with Stealth Move for a one-shot. Confirmed Kill: at turn end, deal 80% attack to lowest-HP enemy in range, triggers even with no bullets. At Awakening 4, killing with Confirmed Kill retriggers it once more; from 5th Awakening, up to 2 more times. If in Stealth, Confirmed Kill first cancels Stealth and grants Opening Found buff (+15% attack, 1 turn).

Class notes: Ace = all-in on crit rate (loosest buff condition). Spitfire = gets attack/crit buff only when out of bullets (max 4 bullets, +20% at Awakening); harshest condition but learns the key skill Barrage, worth securing; high-Awakening Christian runs out of bullets easily from Confirmed Kill so it's surprisingly usable. LTK = learns Indiscriminate Fire; gains 'Perfect Shot' infinite-duration buff (+up to 25% attack/crit) at stage start and on kill, +1 TP on kill; easy to understand but buff vanishes the moment any damage is taken. LTK has the highest firepower and is the "finisher class," but in raids like Cerberus or Diene (where you can't kill enemies and can't avoid damage) its class passive is nearly nonexistent, so avoid it in raids.

Key skills: Stealth Move: no TP, removes all debuffs, 2-turn stealth, extra movement (max Adrenaline stacking). Enemies in this game break stealth with wide attacks, so don't use it as a survival tool, fire immediately after for the buff. Barrage: TP 3, all bullets; 80% attack in a direction, push 1 tile; with a full Magic Bullet magazine the multiplier rises to 105% and higher with Adrenaline; main AoE. Indiscriminate Fire: TP 3, all bullets; empties then reloads Magic Bullet to max, fires at random targetable enemies in range for 25% attack each. Looks weak but is his core damage skill: treat as single-target, each bullet carries Magic Bullet so one shot is really 50%, real multiplier starts at 150%, and with LTK + dedicated weapon (magazine up to 5) reaches 250%, higher with Adrenaline. Its wide range is actually a penalty; aim one target. Satellite Attack (ult): 85~100% attack to enemies within 4 tiles of the target; higher Awakening applies Shock to unshocked enemies and 1-turn Stun to shocked ones. Can't receive Magic Bullet, mediocre multiplier, so performance isn't good, though the area-designation may find situations.

Dedicated weapon / gear: M.C Valentine; extra stat is attack. +1~2 max bullets; at turn end with 0 bullets, gain 1-turn crit-damage buff (3-turn cooldown) and +1 TP; unique effect loads Magic Bullet at 0 bullets (2 rounds base, up to full magazine at 5th Awakening). Crucially, at 3rd Awakening the weapon skill adds "at action end with 0 bullets, gain an extra attack or skill use," which enables LTK Christian's burst mechanism. This is why Christian's dedicated weapon at 3rd Awakening is treated as his true body. Armor: defense is useless (paper), so stack crit rate/crit damage. Hero grade: Assassin set; Legendary: Crow-motif Raven set. Accessory: Ancient Hero's Ring is best (dealer staple). Arrowhead works for Rapid Fire/Suppressive Fire but not for AoE (Barrage/Indiscriminate Fire); Fang usable for attack but physical penetration only applies to combat attacks so not ideal for AoE mains; Whetstone is meaningless since Confirmed Kill isn't an extra hit. Hero grade: Wound Aggravation charm or Golden Dice for crit.
