Unique Passive

Song of Light Passive

  • First Obtained: When an allied character within 2 tiles around ends their turn, if their health is not 100%, restore health equal to 20% of Spell Power and remove 1 debuff.
  • Max Awakening: When an allied character ends their turn, if their health is not 100%, restore health equal to 50% of Spell Power and remove 1 debuff.

Guide

Note: NamuWiki community assessment (KR server), subjective community opinion. No explicit S/A/B tier rating is present.

  • National healer. Despite being an opening (starter/free) character, once at max Awakening she can, at turn end, restore health with no range limit and remove 1 debuff, so she gets deployed into all kinds of combat including raids.
  • Usually you open up to the Tier 2 class Cleric, acquire Warrant Barrier, then choose Summon Lord (Summonlord), because Summon Lord continuously raises spell power when a summon is nearby. Since Nina's healing amount scales with spell power, this is an essential route for her.
  • Nina is used in such a wide variety of situations that it is not a waste to use a Transparent Opal to open all class routes for her.
  • For gear, the hero rod Ravide Pulcra is usually used, the accessory is the Chalice of Devotion, and since healing amount is key, the Light's Devotion set is favored.
