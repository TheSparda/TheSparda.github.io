Signature Gear

Guide

NamuWiki community assessment (KR server); subjective opinion. No explicit S/A/B tier rating is present.

  • Concept: Trap specialist + buffer; considered the prototype of the "loli bomber" character archetype, but the Master Trapper passive's buff is so strong that she is noted more as a buffer than a dealer. These are not mutually exclusive: combining Master Trapper with "Cutie Cutie Trap" rapidly stacks "Master's Touch" on allies while turning the field into a minefield.
  • Passive: "Genius Craftsmanship" replaces the basic attack with Fuze Bomb or Teshub's Fallout if she did not attack that turn. It recreates the original's bomb-making/throwing. Downsides: does not trigger on counterattack and clears after one use. Awakening only raises multipliers, but the passive enhancement at Manifestation 2 adds ATK +30% and damage-taken -30% plus extra action when allies recover Debris, a huge upgrade.
  • Classes: Tier 3 options are Sniper (armor-pen, isolated-target specialist), Fletcher (armor-pen; no crits but converts crit rate into ATK, exclusive), and Master Trapper (buffs allies via skills, exclusive). Master Trapper's "Master's Touch" grants an ally ATK/Magic/Crit rate/DEF/Resistance up to +32% (8% x 4 stacks); since her trap skills all cost TP 1 and can be used multiple times per turn, she can start with 3 stacks from turn 1, which is why she is valued as a buffer.
  • Signature skills / Ultimate: Handle With Care! (TP 1 barricade box, cover + 60% fire explosion on destruction, spawns Debris, cd 3), Special Explosive Arrow (TP 3, 50% + 60% fire, knockback, spawns Debris, cd 3), Cutie Cutie Trap (TP 1, reusable with leftover TP, 60% fire explosion, cd 3) is her core skill and can flood the field with mines. Ultimate Napalm Blossom is effectively 110% total ATK (60% hit + 25%/turn Sticky Flame over 2 turns) but has limited windows since she is busy laying traps.
  • Weapon / gear: Exclusive weapon Taranis Shooter (crossbow) grants "Explosive Idea" ATK stacks (up to 5-10) on dealing damage or placing installations, reinforcing the trap-specialist identity; awakening raises the stack cap for higher ceiling. Since crit is designed to be hard to use (installation damage is ATK-based, Fletcher converts crit to ATK), she stacks ATK: Hero-tier Knight-Errant set; Legendary Ilgidangcheon set or Assassin+Knight-Errant. Accessories: Hero-tier Wound Aggravation charm (heal reduction); Legendary "Goyeongban" or Balanced Blade.
  • Synergy: Fire-damage focused, so pairing with Kana Milanovich (who can cut fire resistance by up to 80%) is worth considering. After the Awakening/Manifestation update, investment gives a clear return.

Awakening/Manifestation progression: - Stage 1: Ultimate Enhancement 1 / Stat Enhancement 1 - Stage 2: Unique Passive Enhancement 1 / Stat Enhancement 2 - Stage 3: Ultimate Enhancement 2 / Artifact Acquisition - Stage 4: Unique Passive Enhancement 2 / Artifact Enhancement 1 - Stage 5: Ultimate Enhancement 3 / Artifact Enhancement 2 - Stage 6: Unique Passive Enhancement 3 / Artifact Enhancement 3 Effects by node (from passive/ult max tiers): - Ultimate (Napalm Blossom): damage scales 60% (initial) -> 90% (max awakening) -> 130% (max manifestation), 3 tiles, applies Sticky Flame (25% ATK fixed fire damage on turn end, 2 turns). - Unique Passive (Genius Craftsmanship): Fuze Bomb / Teshub's Fallout tier goes from 1 to 4. At max manifestation, also grants ATK +30%, damage taken -30%, and allies gain extra action by recovering Debris.
