Privacy Policy

Effective 2 August 2026

Antaria Lore is a free, non-commercial fan wiki for Genesis War Mobile, run by hobbyists. It is not affiliated with the game’s developers or publishers. We do not sell products, run ads, or make money from this site, and we have no interest in collecting personal information. This page explains, in plain terms, the little data the site does touch.

The short version

  • No account, sign-up, or login. We never ask for your name, email, or password.
  • We don’t sell, rent, or share your data with anyone. There’s nothing to sell.
  • Your settings (theme, spoiler level, roster, and similar) are stored only in your own browser and never sent to us.
  • We use privacy-respecting analytics to see which pages are popular, in aggregate.

Settings saved in your browser

To remember your preferences between visits, the site stores a few small values in your browser’s localStorage. This data stays on your device, is never transmitted to us or anyone else, and you can clear it anytime by clearing your browser’s site data. It includes:

  • Your theme choice (Kosmos / Genesis).
  • Your spoiler settings (chapter level and the Genesis War 3+ toggle).
  • Your Roster — the units you mark as owned.
  • Draft loadouts in the Character Builder and Equipment Rater.
  • Which coupon codes you’ve marked as redeemed.
  • An optional in-game Account ID you may save on the Coupons page to speed up redeeming. This is stored only in your browser to pre-fill the official redeem link; it is never sent to Antaria Lore.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand which pages people find useful, measured in aggregate (page views, rough location, device type, referring site). We do not use it to identify individual visitors, and we don’t combine it with any other data. If you’d rather not be counted, a browser “Do Not Track” setting, an ad/analytics blocker, or Google’s opt-out add-on will stop it.

Content from other services

Some pages load content hosted elsewhere, and those providers may see your IP address and set their own cookies under their own privacy policies:

  • YouTube (guide and trailer videos), embedded in privacy-enhanced mode — nothing loads from YouTube until you press play.
  • Google Fonts for typography.
  • Game artwork and reference data drawn from official and community sources (the official site, NamuWiki, gmap.gg).

Links out to the app stores, the official site, our Discord, and other sites are governed by those destinations’ own policies, not this one.

Children

This site is a general-audience wiki and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If the site’s data practices change, we’ll update this page and its effective date. Since we keep no contact details, we can’t notify you directly — please check back here.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us on our Discord.
