Chapters

Follows the in-game Story Recap order: Prologue, then Chapters 1–34, with Chapter 51+ collecting everything past the current chapter list as a single unbroken block for now.

  1. ProThe Battle of GratiasEY 1202 — the Geysir Empire shatters the Pandragon Kingdom in a single day.
  2. 1Legacy of the KingdomThe Black Prince vanishes, Pandragon's survivors flee to Dagal, and the Scepter of Glory raid sets the whole story in motion.
  3. 2Gray HyenaIn the cold Duchy of Bifrost, the man who will become the story's hidden engine lives a quiet life under an alias — until the disappearances start.
  4. 3Northern LightsThe Paladins reach Bifrost, hire G.S as their guide, and begin the hardest crossing on the continent.
  5. 4Ice CastleThe crossing gets harder, Lacid goes missing, and the search for him becomes his first real test — while Beradin quietly strikes the castle from the shadows.
  6. 5Dragon SlayerIn the desert, Karls Brandt reunites with an old comrade from the Seven Knights, Kasim — a thin chapter on the surface, but one that matters enormously later.
  7. 6Warriors of CutisVincent Bernstein's staged "assassination" shocks Cutis — and while the Empire tightens its grip, the Paladins mount a rescue that grows into a full liberation campaign.
  8. 7Temple of the SunThe Paladins race to Astania to defend the Temple of the Sun — and confront the Black Shadows guarding Beradin's real target.
  9. 8The Thief of CyrupsA thief named Robin steals a parchment that belongs to Chancellor Beradin himself — and finds an unlikely ally in a wandering wizard.
  10. 9One-Eyed MercenaryCutis's crisis point — the Allied Forces liberate Castle Cutis, and the masked man helping from the inside reveals who he really is.
  11. 10Silver ArrowGriam Lang invades Astania before the Alliance can even celebrate Cutis — the Silver Arrow Alliance is officially revived, and a romance quietly begins.
  12. 11The Steel TitanKashmir's leader Arcion Blacksword first crosses paths with Silver Arrow's cause.
  13. 12The Secret of the WaterfallA traitor on Bifrost's own ruling council — hunted down, and silenced, before the conspiracy against Duke Moselle even breaks the surface.
  14. 13Chief's Crown IWith the full Silver Arrow Alliance reunited, the campaign to reclaim Castle Hastings — and Kelberos, the airship guarding the road to Pandragon — begins.
  15. 14Chief's Crown IIThe march on Pandragon Castle — and Griam Lang's real reckoning, not at the Wake Plains but before the throne itself.
  16. 15Storm Envoy IA covert order from Draupnir sends G.S back to Bifrost — and straight into a frame-up.
  17. 16Storm Envoy IIFramed and on the run, G.S closes the loop on the traitor from Chapter 12 — and the wizard who's been chasing his statue ever since.
  18. 17Chief's Crown IIIThe siege of the impregnable fortress of Thunderdoom — and an unexpected new ally.
  19. 18Chief's Crown IVThe Second Battle of the Gratias Plains — a hard-won victory that avenges Silver Arrow's first defeat here, but costs it one of its own.
  20. 19Desert StormIyolin's bold advance into Trisis proves to be exactly the wrong call.
  21. 20Path of the Holy KingCrow saves Lacid, trains him in ten astonishing days, and a family secret comes to light.
  22. 21Blazing a TrailKaja's push for the Eastern Sea Route brings together the Amigo Guild's cast — and quietly resolves an old loose thread for Zeke Stafford.
  23. 22Search for the Sacred Sword IThunderdoom is fought back a second time, and the search for the last sacred treasure begins.
  24. 23Search for the Sacred Sword IIThe hunt for the Stormbringer leads to a direct clash with Beradin himself.
  25. 24BlueseedArcion's Kashmir party searches for the missing Kaja and runs headlong into pirates, then the Polar Kraken.
  26. 25Finding the GodsLacid's own search for the Divine Weapon converges with Arcion's — and a small mercy at Notesi Island turns into much more.
  27. 26Demonic CorpsBeradin's full counter-offensive breaks both of Silver Arrow's capitals in one stroke.
