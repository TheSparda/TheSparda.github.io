Kosmos Discs

The 82 Kosmos Discs — codified fragments of the Inner World's history kept by the Kosmos System. Lore-only collectibles; translated from the KR database (gmap.gg).

#001Kosmos System›

The core of the Seeds, which manages the cyclical repetition of Inner World history.

The Kosmos System can run history simulations without limit so that the flow of history cycles smoothly. It can model an enormous range of situations: minor and major clashes, full-scale wars, the definition of relationships between specific figures, experiments on error rates, and more. The results of each simulation are automatically uploaded to the Seeds' computer cloud, where the Seeds may review them. Access to that information, of course, differs greatly depending on a Seed's rank and office.

코스모스 시스템 · Seeds Manual

#002The Ark›

A safe facility built to protect the Kosmos System and the Seeds in the event of an emergency.

The Ark houses the main body of the Kosmos System, the history simulator known as the Inner World, and various training facilities and databases. Access to its sections is restricted according to a Seed's grade. It is said that the Ark was created to shelter the Seeds and the Kosmos System after the Recount incident.

방주 · Doridori's Notes

#003Inner World›

The collective name for the history simulators that exist within the Ark.

It is called the Inner World because it recreates real spaces and real people inside the Ark. By contrast, entities that intrude from outside are called Outers, and among them the especially threatening ones are called Outer Ones.

이너월드 · Doridori's Notes

#004Simulation Training›

Combat training for the Seeds is carried out within Simulation Training.

Newly created Seeds almost all begin their training here. Enemies are generated using the Ether data of every figure who has ever existed in recorded history, so the variety of possible combinations is boundless. Because the training is meant to raise a Seed's skill quickly, the combat difficulty is far from gentle.

시뮬레이션 트레이닝 · Seeds Manual

#005Seeds Grades›

The Seeds System is agile and highly refined; to advance toward a progressive history, the Seeds are divided into several ranks.

Four grades, from A to D, have been confirmed so far. Grade A consists of the twenty-four Numbering Seeds, who oversee the major tuning of history and also raise the lower grades. Grade B assists Grade A in the work of tuning history; possessing selfhood, they each follow a particular Grade A Seed whom they revere. Grade C takes direct part in minor missions, handling small roles in history and responding nimbly as circumstances require. Grade D merely carries out the commands entered into it and is granted no selfhood.

시즈 등급 · Author Unknown

#006Numbering Seeds›

The Kosmos System judged that Seeds without the capacity for keen independent judgment, that is, without selfhood, could not cope with high error rates.

For this reason it programmed independent Seeds, each with its own individuality and selfhood, to form and manage the rank structure. In particular, among the several grades, the twenty-four Grade A Numbering Seeds are responsible for the major tuning of history. The Numbering Seeds refer to one another not by plain numbers but by special code names, and the final position, Omega, is currently vacant.

넘버링 시즈 · Doridori's Notes

#007Numbering Seed, Codename Alpha (α)›

Ranked first among the twenty-four Grade A Numbering Seeds.

A very flighty and intensely curious Seed, Alpha enjoys leaping into history to experience it firsthand. Alpha directs the whole of the Kosmos simulation, whose purpose is the stable cycling of Antaria's history. Lately Alpha has found the Book of Antaria, the standard guideline for Antarian history, far too dull, and wishes it would develop a little further. Alpha uses the Seeds' default body and, claiming to have not yet found a figure to their liking, goes about draped in a black robe. Alpha acts as a partner to Psi (ψ), ranked twenty-third.

#008Numbering Seed, Codename Psi (ψ)›

Ranked twenty-third among the twenty-four Grade A Numbering Seeds.

With cold reason and meticulous calculation, Psi is responsible for the security of the Kosmos System. Psi holds that personal taste is unimportant and that keeping to the rules is what matters most. Of late, an error code of unknown origin has arisen, causing considerable trouble. Psi uses the body of 'Joanne Cartwright' and acts as a partner to Alpha (α), ranked first.

#009Outer One›

An Outer One refers to an entity that has intruded into the Kosmos System from somewhere outside without authorization.

Their Ether signatures are not registered in the Seeds' computers, making precise analysis or tracking impossible. Even so, each is presumed to possess tremendous Ether power and combat strength strong beyond prediction. In some cases a figure already registered in the internal data has fused with intruding external data and awakened; these too are classified as Outer Ones.

아우터 원 · Psi's (ψ) Report

#010Inner Break›

An Inner Break is the phenomenon of figures within the Kosmos data awakening their own Ether power to its utmost limit.

Figures who attain this awakening come into abilities that far transcend their original power. It is understood to manifest most often in situations where the error rate has been driven to an extreme.

이너 브레이크 · Psi's (ψ) Report

#011Ether›

Ether is a kind of biological energy that sustains human life.

In certain human religions it is also called the soul. Where a particular civilization has discovered the concept, the powerful energy produced by the interaction of Ether, that is, Ether power, is put to many uses in both combat and daily life. When a human meets true death, the Ether begins to leave the body and disperses into nature. The time it takes for all of the Ether to depart differs greatly according to the person's level of Ether power and the way the body is preserved.

영자 · Seeds Manual

#012Ultimate Grima›

Ultimate Grima is an Ether-replenishing technique devised by the dark god Deimos that absorbs surrounding Ether into the body for use.

By using Ultimate Grima, one absorbs and circulates the surrounding Ether to surpass one's own limits. However, if the amount of Ether absorbed exceeds the threshold the user's body can bear, the balance of Ether collapses and the user loses their selfhood or their form disintegrates. For this reason, when using Ultimate Grima one must carefully control both the amount and the speed of Ether absorption.

궁극 그리마 · Seeds Manual

#013Asura›

Asura is a Grima-form sword that Carl Steiner summons by transforming his arm.

It exists in nature in the form of Ether, and it moves, vanishes, is summoned, and transforms in response to particular Ether power. In its complete form, Asura absorbs the Ether power of surrounding living things without limit, and its destructive force grows in proportion to how much it takes in. It makes no exception even for the sword's own owner, so without some countermeasure it will damage even the wielder's body. Absent any large error rate, its principal wielders are confirmed to be Carl Steiner, Cyrano Bernstein, Clausewitz Pendragon, Philip Pendragon, and Vermode.

#014Chaos Cube›

A Chaos Cube is a medium that absorbs and stores human Ether.

It is presumed to have arisen naturally in the earliest days of the universe, and its exact origin is unknown. In the Arke civilization, Chaos Cubes were used to harness energy by extracting, storing, and releasing the Ether of other people. Depending on the level of the Chaos Cube and of the Ether power, its applications are wide-ranging: ultra-long-range mass teleportation, piloting high-output War Mecha, weapons embedded with ultimate techniques, the fabrication of Doll bodies, and more.

#015Annihilation Hell Sword›

The Annihilation Hell Sword is a Chaos Cube-based weapon and one of the famed blades of great destructive power.

It is understood to have grown even stronger after absorbing part of the Ether power of the fire dragon Hellkite. It is the weapon whose form changes most often and most dramatically according to its wielder; in some cases it has been observed to split into twin blades. Absent any large error rate, its principal wielders are confirmed to be Carls Brandt, Cyrano Bernstein, Clausewitz Pendragon, Philip Pendragon, and Albertini de Medici.

#016Book of Antaria›

The Book of Antaria provides the most basic guideline for history.

The Seeds compare events against this guideline to calculate and adjust the error rate. It is, in effect, a basic instruction manual. When newly born Seeds must be trained in history, there is no better teaching material to be found.

#017Error Code›

When an anomaly that cannot be analyzed is found during a Kosmos simulation, it is classified as an Error Code.

Because these Error Codes only began to appear relatively recently, information about them is very scarce for now. All that can be presumed is that they are likely entities that have intruded from outside.

#018The Recount Incident›

Long ago a certain incident nearly wiped out the Seeds and nearly collapsed the Kosmos System; this is called the 'Recount Incident.'

Afterward the Kosmos System and its data were restored as far as possible, but much of the data from past cycles was lost, and the count of cycles had no choice but to begin again from the start. Data regarding the Recount Incident is very tightly access-restricted.

#019The Current Cycle›

The Recount Incident that occurred long ago put the Kosmos System at great risk of collapse; the data of past cycles was lost at that time, and the count is said to have started over from the beginning. It is said the Ark, built to protect the Kosmos System, was also created then.

#020Standard Error Rate›

Data on the earliest ancient history of Antaria has mostly been lost; in particular, almost none remains from before the Recount Incident.

The standard error rate, which serves as the baseline for current error-rate measurement, is re-collated by the Kosmos System after each cycle ends, steadily refining the standard historical model so that stable cycles can be maintained. This standard error rate is closely tied to the contents of the Book of Antaria produced in each cycle. Yet no one knows how greatly the contents of long-past cycles differ from those of the current one. The history from before the Recount Incident, in particular, is known as uncharted territory.

Unlike a Chaos Cube, which allows Ether to be freely injected and released, a fixed quantity of Ether lies frozen within it, as though it had always been that way. With our current knowledge, using Carnilite is impossible. Further research will be needed.

#065Channels of the Inner World›

The Inner World consists of seven channels in total, and the Kosmos System's seven cores are connected in parallel to each channel.

Each channel can be considered a separate parallel world, and the Kosmos cores tune history by synthesizing the simulation results of every channel. For example, a single historical figure might be realized in a different channel with a separate Ether code and mass-production Doll, in which case the system may judge and synchronize their memories or experiences. Conversely, if data is deleted in one channel for some reason, that figure's existence is reconstructed as vanished in the other channels as well.
