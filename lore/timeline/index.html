Chronological Timeline

Key events of the story in Ezekiel calendar (EY) order. Some dates are approximate where sources don't give a specific day or year.

Before the Story

  2. EY 1185 (approx.)
    Karl Steiner (the Black Prince) first active in warfare at age 12.
  3. EY 1194 (approx.)
    The Black Prince rules Geysir as regent from age 17 after his father Carl the Great is seriously wounded.
  4. EY 1200
    Cyrups raises grain prices; Geysir uses this as a pretext to invade.

    Pandragon mobilizes Silver Arrow.

  5. EY 1201
    The Black Prince conquers Cyrups (age 23).

    The war approaches its decisive moment.

The Fall (EY 1202)

  1. EY 1202
    The Battle of Gratias.

    King Ashur and princes Akron and Lint killed. Ice Pandragon defeated. Pandragon and Cutis fall. Silver Arrow annihilated.

  2. EY 1202 (shortly after)
    Beradin engineers the Black Prince's assassination.

    The airship is destroyed at 3,000 metres near Bifrost. The Black Prince crashes and loses all memory.

  3. EY 1202
    The crash survivor is found by old ranger Anagi.

    He gives the amnesiac the name Gray Scavenger (G.S). Pandragon survivors flee to Dagal.

The Exile Years (EY 1202–1207)

  1. EY 1202–1207
    G.S lives as a Bifrost ranger under Anagi's guidance.

    Spends one year wandering Antaria searching for anyone who recognises him. No one does.

  2. EY 1204 (approx.)
    Anagi dies of illness. G.S continues alone.
  3. EY 1205–1207
    Beradin consolidates power over the Empire.

    Redirects Imperial resources from conquest to relic-hunting (God of Destruction statues).

The Story Begins (EY 1207)

  1. Day 17, EY 1207
    Gray Hyena period

    G.S, Strider, and Sarah expose Imperial scheming in Bifrost (Tara Valley / Sulfur Cavern).

  2. Day 30, EY 1207
    Legacy of the Kingdom

    Iyolin leads the Curtis raid and recovers the Scepter of Glory. The Imperial trap springs. Flight to Bifrost begins.

  3. EY 1207
    G.S is hired by Duke Moselle to guide the Pandragon party over the Gigantes Mountains.

    The Irregular Knights are formed in Dagal.

  4. EY 1207
    Vincent Bernstein's assassination is staged.

    Hanzo fakes the killing on Beradin's orders; Duke Stellian is installed as Cutis's new governor; Arcia Landino is falsely blamed (Chapter 6).

The Silver Arrow Arc (EY 1207–1210)

  1. EY 1207–1208
    Cutis liberated, Astania secured, Silver Arrow revived.

    Iyolin crowned first queen of Pandragon.

  2. EY 1208–1209
    G.S dispatched as envoy to Bifrost. Duke Moselle assassinated; G.S framed.

    Iyolin cannot wait and launches the Trisis expedition.

  3. EY 1209–1210
    Trisis disaster: Holy Knights annihilated, Iyolin captured.

    Lacid escapes; trained by Crow; receives Barisada and Arondite.

  4. EY 1210–1211
    Lacid's counteroffensive: Stormbringer recovered, Kashmir joins, Heaven's Castle activated by G.S.

    Three gods reveal Veramod's plan (Esprit bloodline). Silver Arrow drives to the gates of Geysir.
